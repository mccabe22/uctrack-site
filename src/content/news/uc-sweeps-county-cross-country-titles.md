<h2 class="article-image-caption">UC Sweeps County Cross-Country Titles, Brosnan Smashes Course Record</h2>
</div>
<div class="article-content row">
<header>
<p class="article-byline"><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Written by:</span> Jim Lambert</p>
<p class="article-byline">Lots of great runners and teams that have toed the line at Warinanco Park&#8217;s dual-meet course since it started being used in 1988.</p>
</header>
<p>But there has never been a group of runners from one team who have blown apart the 3.19-mile layout the way the Union Catholic boys did at Thursday&#8217;s 84th Union County Cross-Country Championships.</p>
<p>Union Catholic, ranked No. 1 in NJ, dropped an absolute bomb as the Vikings put on a sizzling show by having two runners go under the course record on the way to demolishing the course record average at Warinanco!</p>
<p>It was a performance for the ages!</p>
<p>When the smoke cleared, UC&#8217;s <a title="View athlete profile for Shane Brosnan" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/8322837-shane-brosnan" aria-label="View athlete profile for Shane Brosnan">Shane Brosnan</a> (15:28) and <a title="View athlete profile for Jimmy Wischusen" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/10214165-jimmy-wischusen" aria-label="View athlete profile for Jimmy Wischusen">Jimmy Wischusen</a> (15:39) became the two fastest runners in course history, and <a title="View athlete profile for Myles Plummer" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/8233818-myles-plummer" aria-label="View athlete profile for Myles Plummer">Myles Plummer</a> (15:53) moved into the No. 7 spot all-time as he completed a 1-2-3 finish for UC, igniting the Vikings to a course record average of 16:03 and a second straight title.</p>
<p>The blistering16:03 average by UC smashed the mark of 16:22 that Westfield ran in 2013.</p>
<p>UC junior <a title="View athlete profile for Ryan Cichocki" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/9434171-ryan-cichocki" aria-label="View athlete profile for Ryan Cichocki">Ryan Cichocki</a>, eighth in 16:33, and junior <a title="View athlete profile for Caudell Cajuste" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/9434163-caudell-cajuste" aria-label="View athlete profile for Caudell Cajuste">Caudell Cajuste</a>, ninth in 16:40, ran very strong races to put the finishing touches on UC&#8217;s masterpiece as the Vikings defeated No. 7 ranked Westfield, 23-54. No. 10 Summit was third with 81.dThe greatest team performance in course history was highlighted by the stunning races by Brosnan and Wischusen.</p>
<p>With his eye-popping time of 15:28, Brosnan, a senior who was third last year, took down the course record of 15:42 that Tim Styler of Cranford set at the Union County meet in 1998. Brosnan hit the first mile in 4:55 and two miles in 9:38, nearly identical to Styler&#8217;s splits. Brosnan then poured it on over the final 1,200 meters to break the record.</p>
<p>Wischusen, a sophomore who also ran under Styler&#8217;s record with his 15:39, has been one of the state&#8217;s fastest rising stars this fall. Last year, Wischusen was second in the JV race at the county meet, and now he&#8217;s No. 2 in course history at Warinanco! That&#8217;s amazing!</p>
<p>And how about Plummer. As a junior last year, Plummer won the county title in 16:24. This year, he slices 31 seconds off his winning time and places third. That&#8217;s how insanely fast this race was! The 16:33 from Cichocki was a PR for any course, and Cajuste&#8217;s 16:40 is a Warinanco PR.</p>
<p>Styler was at the meet working as the official timer as he&#8217;s done for several years now. He congratulated Brosnan and Wischusen for breaking his record.&#8221;I never thought the record would last this long,&#8221; said Styler. &#8220;There have a few guys that came close, and I had a feeling that he (Brosnan) had a good chance to get it today because of how fast he&#8217;s been running this season.&#8221;<br />
<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/2021CountyXCAbbyLeilani.jpeg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-large wp-image-14430 alignleft" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/2021CountyXCAbbyLeilani.jpeg" alt="" width="620" height="413" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/2021CountyXCAbbyLeilani.jpeg 620w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/11/2021CountyXCAbbyLeilani-300x200.jpeg 300w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 620px) 100vw, 620px" /></a>Union Catholic also captured the girls team title in a thriller vs. Kent Place.</p>
<p>Kent Place, behind the 1-2 punch of <a title="View athlete profile for Miranda Lorsbach" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/7459998-miranda-lorsbach" aria-label="View athlete profile for Miranda Lorsbach">Miranda Lorsbach</a>, first in 18:41, and<a title="View athlete profile for Lindsay Hausman" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/7459995-lindsay-hausman" aria-label="View athlete profile for Lindsay Hausman"> Lindsay Hausman</a>, fourth in 18:53, made a strong bid to capture its first title.</p>
<p>But Union Catholic, ranked No. 12 in the state, overcame Kent Place&#8217;s formidable frontal power by putting together its best race of the season, and receiving a huge boost from the back of its pack to edge No.13 ranked Kent Place, 54-56.</p>
<p>UC, which averaged 20:00, put its five scoring runners ahead of Kent Place&#8217;s No. 4 runner to rally for the victory. UC went 5-8-10-14-16 and Kent Place went 1-4-12-18-21.</p>
<p>The top five runners for UC were junior <a title="View athlete profile for Kaleigh Gunsiorowski" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/10428253-kaleigh-gunsiorowski" aria-label="View athlete profile for Kaleigh Gunsiorowski">Kaleigh Gunsiorowski,</a> fifth in 19:15, sophomore <a title="View athlete profile for Leilani Gibson" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/10214180-leilani-gibson" aria-label="View athlete profile for Leilani Gibson">Leilani Gibson</a>, ninth in 19:55, senior <a title="View athlete profile for Abby Granrath" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/8233810-abby-granrath" aria-label="View athlete profile for Abby Granrath">Abby Granrath</a>, 10th in 19:58, junior <a title="View athlete profile for Maameyaa Nyinah" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/9434141-maameyaa-nyinah" aria-label="View athlete profile for Maameyaa Nyinah">Maameyaa Nyinah</a>, 14th in 20:23, and senior <a title="View athlete profile for Jenna Keith" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/8233811-jenna-keith" aria-label="View athlete profile for Jenna Keith">Jenna Keith</a>, 16th in 20:31.This is Union Catholic&#8217;s second title in three years, and its fourth overall. The Vikings also won in 2014, &#8217;15, and &#8217;19.</p>
<p>Union Catholic also edged Kent Place, 47-50, to win the Watchung Division title at the Union County Conference Championships two weeks ago. But Kent Place ran that race without Hausman, who was out with a stress reaction. With Hausman returning to the lineup for the county race, it seemed like Kent Place would have the edge in the rematch</p>
<p>But the back of UC&#8217;s pack surged late and closed hard as Granrath, Nyinah, and Keith all picked off multiple runners over the final quarter mile to give UC the title.</p>
<p>In addition to the varsity title sweep, UC also picked up a victory from Tobey Garcia, who won the Freshman Division boys title in 13:18 over the 2.2-mile course.</p>
