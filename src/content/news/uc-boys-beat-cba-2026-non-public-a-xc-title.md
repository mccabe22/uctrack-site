<p>Union Catholic defeated top-ranked Christian Brothers Academy 34–40 to win the NJSIAA boys’ Non-Public A cross-country championship on November 7 at Holmdel Park. Nick Frank led the Vikings in fourth place with a Holmdel personal best of 16:07.68. Danny Kuran finished fifth in 16:08.06, and Nathan Simonich placed seventh in 16:10.54.</p><p>The result was the boys’ first group championship since 2021 on the same day the girls won their title with a perfect score. Union Catholic returned to Holmdel Park the following week for the Meet of Champions.</p>
