<p><strong>By James Lambert | November 2, 2026, 3:05 PM</strong></p>
<p>Jasmine Parran finished first to spark the Union Catholic girls to the team title at the New Jersey Catholic Track Conference Championships on Saturday at Greystone in Morris Plains.</p>
<p>Parran ran 19:31 to ignite a 1-2-4-5-8 finish as the Vikings outscored Holy Angels, 19-65.</p>
<p>Union Catholic&#8217;s six finishers were Jasmine Parran (1st, 19:30.36), Kailyn Brown (2nd, 19:40.96), Gabriella Mitchell (4th, 19:51.31), Myra Sakhuja (5th, 19:52.14), Aubrey Devers (8th, 20:33.15) and Naomi Akakpo (14th, 21:08.57).</p>
