<p>The Union Catholic cross-country teams absolutely torched the course at historic Warinanco Park on Thursday when the Vikings threw down the most impressive team sweep in the 85-year history of the Union County Championships.</p>
<p>Peyton Hollis led the charge for the UC girls with an epic and electrifying victory when the junior edged Lindsay Hausman of Kent Place in a race for the ages as both runners were timed in 17:49, a whopping 37 seconds under the former course record!!! Hollis hit the mile in 5:40 and two miles in 11:16 on the way to shattering the course record of 18:26, which was set by Victoria Vanriele of Gov. Livingston.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p align="center"><img decoding="async" src="https://assets.sp.milesplit.com/articles/322440/images/cover-1666314311.png" alt="" /></p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>The 17:49 by Hollis on the 3.19-mile layout helped propel Union Catholic to the greatest team performance ever as the Vikings, ranked No. 1 in the state, put five runners in the top nine and ran a blistering course record average of 18:54 on their way to a 24-55 victory over No. 11 ranked Kent Place. No. 16 Westfield was third with 98. It&#8217;s the second straight title for UC and its fifth overall.</p>
<p>UC, despite missing two of its usual top five runners, still chopped 34 seconds off the course record of 19:28 that was set by Westfield at the 2013 Union County Conference Championships.</p>
<p>The four other scorers behind Hollis for UC all ran their best times ever on this course. UC senior <a title="View athlete profile for Kaleigh Gunsiorowski" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/10428253-kaleigh-gunsiorowski" aria-label="View athlete profile for Kaleigh Gunsiorowski">Kaleigh Gunsiorowski </a>also ran one of the fastest times in course history, placing third in 18:35. Junior <a title="View athlete profile for Courtney Kaiser" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/11770202-courtney-kaiser" aria-label="View athlete profile for Courtney Kaiser">Courtney Kaiser</a>, another first-year XC runner, was fourth in 18:46, junior <a title="View athlete profile for Leilani Gibson" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/10214180-leilani-gibson" aria-label="View athlete profile for Leilani Gibson">Leilani Gibson</a> finished seventh in 19:36, and senior<a title="View athlete profile for Victoria Urbaez" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/11134431-victoria-urbaez" aria-label="View athlete profile for Victoria Urbaez"> Victoria Urbaez</a> finished ninth in 19:48.<br />

<p><strong>UC BOYS RISE TO THE CHALLENGE </strong></p>
<p>There certainly was a cloud hanging over the boys race when Union Catholic star <a href="https://nj.milesplit.com/articles/322457/injury-may-sideline-ucs-wischusen-for-rest-of-xc-season" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Jimmy Wischusen didn&#8217;t run due to an injury that may sideline him for the season.</a> We wish Jimmy a speedy recovery!!!</p>
<p>Wischusen absence raised a big question!</p>
<p>Could Union Catholic, No. 2 ranked in the state, overcome such a big loss in its lineup and still win its third straight title in its showdown with No. 4 ranked Westfield?</p>
<p>That question was answered empathically as the Vikings embraced the challenge and rose to the occasion as several runners stepped up to spark the Vikings to a 44-56 victory over Westfield. No. 7 Summit was third with 82.</p>
<p>Junior <a title="View athlete profile for Alex Saldana" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/10238377-alex-saldana" aria-label="View athlete profile for Alex Saldana">Alex Saldana</a> led the charge for UC, by placing third with a PR of 15:46, the 7th fastest time in course history. Saldana, who said he nearly stopped running after hurting his ankle near the mile-mark, gutted it out the rest of the way as he ignited a 3-6-8-11-16 finish for UC, who averaged 16:06, the second fastest in course history. UC set the record of 16:03 last year on it way to winning the Meet of Champions title.UC&#8217;s scorers behind Saldana were senior <a title="View athlete profile for Ryan Cichocki" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/9434171-ryan-cichocki" aria-label="View athlete profile for Ryan Cichocki">Ryan Cichocki</a>, sixth in 15:59, senior <a title="View athlete profile for Caudell Cajuste" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/9434163-caudell-cajuste" aria-label="View athlete profile for Caudell Cajuste">Caudell Cajuste</a>, eighth in 16:01, junior<a title="View athlete profile for Sean Wilson" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/10155489-sean-wilson" aria-label="View athlete profile for Sean Wilson"> Sean Wilson</a>, 11th in 16:16, and sophomore <a title="View athlete profile for Evan Weber" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/10271118-evan-weber" aria-label="View athlete profile for Evan Weber">Evan Weber</a>, 16th in 16:28.<br />
