<h2 class="article-headline">UC girls steamroll its way to fourth straight State Non-Public A T&amp;F Championship</h2>
<div class="noprint story_email"><a href="https://www.addthis.com/bookmark.php?v=250&amp;pub=xa-4a7320e7645958d0"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="https://s7.addthis.com/static/btn/lg-share-en.gif" alt="Bookmark and Share" width="125" height="16" /></a> <time class="article-date">6/3/2018 4:13:00 PM</time></div>
<p class="article-subheadline">Morant, Lufrano, Tucker, Chadwick and the 4&#215;100 and 4&#215;400 all win</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic girls track and field team completed another undefeated season when the Vikings put together a remarkable performance to runaway with its fourth straight title at the NJSIAA Non-Public A State Championships that concluded on Saturday at Franklin High School in Somerset.</p>
<p>The Vikings steamrolled the competition by ringing up 153 points, the most of any winning team at the Group Championships. Runner-up Paramus Catholic finished with 73 points. Full results of the meet are<a href="http://nj.milesplit.com/meets/310014/results/596934/formatted#.WxUpcUgvxPY"> here </a><br />
<iframe loading="lazy" title="Video: UC girls steamroll its way to fourth straight State Non-Public A TF Championship - Union Catholic High School" src="https://ucvikings.com/showcase/embed.aspx?title=UC%20girls%20steamroll%20its%20way%20to%20fourth%20straight%20State%20Non-Public%20A%20T%26F%20Championship%20-%20Union%20Catholic%20High%20School&amp;youtube=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fembed%2FeXRho2rgsCY&amp;autoplay=false" width="560" height="315" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen="allowfullscreen" aria-hidden="true" data-mce-fragment="1"></iframe><br />
UC picked up individual wins from sophomore <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=407" rel="smarttag" rev="407">Leena Morant</a></dfn>, who won her second straight 800 (personal best and NJ #4 2:11.33), senior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=393" rel="smarttag" rev="393">Amaya Chadwick</a></dfn>, who won her third straight 100 title in a NJ #4 14.04, sophomore <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=415" rel="smarttag" rev="415">Sydney Tucker</a></dfn> in the 100 (12.33) and from junior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=404" rel="smarttag" rev="404">Jerika Lufrano</a></dfn> in the 3,200 (a PR of 10:55.15). Lufrano also placed second in the 1,600 with a PR of 5:02.38. Lufrano&#8217;s times in the 1,600 and 3,200 are both school records.</p>
<p>The Vikings also smashed the meet record in the 4&#215;100 (47.50) and won the 4&#215;400 in a NJ #1 3:51.04. In the 4&#215;100, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=389" rel="smarttag" rev="389">Kira Alexander</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=415" rel="smarttag" rev="415">Sydney Tucker</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=411" rel="smarttag" rev="411">Felicia Quainoo</a></dfn>, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=395" rel="smarttag" rev="395">Khamil Evans</a></dfn> shared the baton in the 4&#215;100. The splits in the 4&#215;400 were 58.8 for <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=402" rel="smarttag" rev="402">Avery Jordan</a></dfn>, 56.8 for Morant, 57.4 for <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=420" rel="smarttag" rev="420">Zamira Ysaac</a></dfn>, and 57.8 for <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=395" rel="smarttag" rev="395">Khamil Evans</a></dfn>. Senior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=413" rel="smarttag" rev="413">Tyler Scott</a></dfn> also had a  big meet, placing second in the triple jump (38-6) and fifth in the high jump (5-2).</p>
<p>UC will be back in action this Saturday at the State Meet of Champions at 2 p.m. at Northern Burlington High School.
