<p>In an initiative to help the students in need in the Houston area, the UC Cross Country Team has agreed to join with Rahway HS to provide a minimum of 10 backpacks filled with school supplies. Below is a list of what is needed for ONE backpack. We are hoping that team members/families can join up to make 10 filled backpacks. Our deadline for dropoff is September 21st!!! We ask that you bring in your donations by <strong>Wednesday, September 20th. </strong></p>
<p><strong>ALL DONATIONS SHOULD BE DROPPED OFF IN MR. THORNTON&#8217;S CLASSROOM (ROOM 227) OR AT PRACTICE.</strong></p>
