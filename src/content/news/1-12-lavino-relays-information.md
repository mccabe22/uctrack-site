<p class="article-subheadline">The Vikings ran 3:34.35 at Saturday&#8217;s meet at Lawrenceville</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>Union Catholic&#8217;s sprint medley relay team went to the Lavino Relays in Lawrenceville on Saturday with one goal in mind. To drop a red hot time.</p>
<p>That&#8217;s exactly what they did as the Vikings burned a 3:34.35, the third fastest time in the nation this season by a high school boys team, to finish first. UC finished just ahead of runner-up Lawrenceville, second in a US #4 3:34.71.</p>
<p>The only two teams in the country to run faster than UC are St. Benedict&#8217;s Prep of Newark (3:33.46) and Bronxville of N.Y. (3:33.79).</p>
<p>The four runners that cranked out the sizzling time for UC were senior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=739" rel="smarttag" rev="739">Chinedu Okoro</a></dfn> (23.1 for 200), junior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=741" rel="smarttag" rev="741">Conor Reilly</a></dfn> (24.1 for 200), junior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=732" rel="smarttag" rev="732">Elijah Brown</a></dfn> (49.5 for 400), and freshman <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=748" rel="smarttag" rev="748">Myles Plummer</a></dfn> (1:57.4 for 800).</p>
<p>In addition to its SMR victory, the Vikings also ran a very fast shuttle hurdle relay, placing second in 31.22, the fourth fastest time by a N.J. team this winter. UC finished just behind the N.J. #3 time of 31.07 run by Nottingham. UC&#8217;s four hurdles were <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=729" rel="smarttag" rev="729">Obadare Animashaun</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=738" rel="smarttag" rev="738">Ryan McLaughlin</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=736" rel="smarttag" rev="736">Brandon Leslie</a></dfn>, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=730" rel="smarttag" rev="730">Oluwafemi Asenuga</a></dfn>.</p>
<p><strong>The Girls Squad won the 4&#215;200 and 4&#215;400 Relays and finished 2nd in the 4&#215;800 and Long Jump Relays.<br />
</strong>-Felicia Quainoo, Avery, Jordan and Sydney McDonald were all on the 4&#215;2 and 4&#215;4 Relay Wins!<br />
-Morgan Jackson, Morgan Williams, Abby Granrath and Jenna Keith were on the 2nd place 4&#215;8 Squad<br />
-Sophomores A&#8217;Liyah Thomas and Danielle Rappa combined to place 2nd in Long Jump</p>
