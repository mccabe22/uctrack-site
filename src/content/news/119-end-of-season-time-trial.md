<p style="text-align: left;"><strong>Wednesday, November 9th, 2016</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: left;"><strong>End of Season Time Trial</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: left;">All members of the Cross Country Team are expected be at practice on Wednesday (11/9). There will be a 1600m Time Trial for all athletes who are not competing at the state meet. This is a great way to see how far you have come since the early season Time Trial!</p>
<p style="text-align: left;">THOSE WHO ARE NOT TRAINING FOR THE STATE MEET, DO NOT HAVE PRACTICE ON MONDAY OR TUESDAY. But an easy run on Tuesday is highly recommended. Will help you run fast on Wednesday.</p>
<p style="text-align: left;">If you are running the Time Trial, please bring your uniform to return  on Wednesday.</p>
<p style="text-align: left;">Also, bring your racing spikes. We will run the Time Trial on the track.</p>
<p style="text-align: left;">You ARE running the Time Trial, unless you are on the following list.</p>
