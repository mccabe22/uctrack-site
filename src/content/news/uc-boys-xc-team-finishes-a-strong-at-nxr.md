<p>The Union Catholic boys cross-country team, ranked No. 8 in the state, finished their season with a very strong eighth-place at the Nike Northeast Regional Championships on November 23rd at Bowdoin Park in Wappingers Falls, N.Y.</p>
<p>The Vikings, who scored with 361 points and averaged 17:15 with a compression of 41 seconds, finished ahead of eight teams ranked in the top 20 in New Jersey, including No. 3 Cherokee and No. 4 Westfield.</p>
<p>The five scorers for the Vikings were sophomore <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/sports/mens-cross-country/roster/danny-kuran/3294" rel="smarttag" rev="3294" data-airgap-id="44">Danny Kuran</a></dfn>, 67th in 17:02, senior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/sports/mens-cross-country/roster/aidan-cichocki/3266" rel="smarttag" rev="3266" data-airgap-id="45">Aidan Cichocki</a></dfn>, 69th in 17:04, junior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/sports/mens-cross-country/roster/elijah-mccoy/3296" rel="smarttag" rev="3296" data-airgap-id="46">Elijah McCoy</a></dfn>, 84th in 17:10, sophomore <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/sports/mens-cross-country/roster/finn-riely/3298" rel="smarttag" rev="3298" data-airgap-id="47">Finn Riely</a></dfn>, 98th in 17:18, and freshman Manny Sanchez,140th in 17:43. Sophomore <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/sports/mens-cross-country/roster/nick-frank/3293" rel="smarttag" rev="3293" data-airgap-id="48">Nick Frank</a></dfn> finished 151st in 17:50, and senior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/sports/mens-cross-country/roster/michael-devine/3267" rel="smarttag" rev="3267" data-airgap-id="49">Michael Devine</a></dfn> also ran 17:50 to finish 153rd.</p>
