<p>The Girls Cross Country Team dominated the 5150 meter course at Warinanco Park and won their 2nd consecutive Union County Championship! The girls were led with a 2nd place finish by Amari Onque-Shabazz. Amari ran a UC Course Record time of 19:00.49. Rounding out the top 5 were Asia Johnson (3rd-19:27.06), Jerika Lufrano (5th-19:48.05 / UC Freshman Class Record), Tressa Novack (11th-20:20.76) and Megan McCabe (17th-20:36.03).           <span style="text-decoration: underline;">Girls Scoring:</span> 2, 3, 5, 10, 16  |   <span style="text-decoration: underline;">Team Average:</span> 19:50 <em>(School Record)</em></p>
<p><a href="https://uctrack.smugmug.com/CrossCountry/2015-Cross-Country/2015-Union-County/i-DTd56pC">https://uctrack.smugmug.com/CrossCountry/2015-Cross-Country/2015-Union-County/i-DTd56pC</a></p>
<p>The boys had a very strong performance, placing 2nd Overall. The Vikings were led by Brendan McCabe (16:40.85-4th), Justin Cadas (17:01.99-11th), Andre Rodriguez (17:22.62-18th), Trevor Garrity (17:38.15-27th) and Jimmy Perona (17:39.62-29th). The boys ran a very spirited race despite the absence of  #1 man Nate Papachristos.<br />
<span style="text-decoration: underline;">Boys Scoring:</span> 4, 11, 18, 27, 29   |   <span style="text-decoration: underline;">Team Average:</span> 17:16</p>
