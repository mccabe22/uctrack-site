<p>All season, the Union Catholic girls 4&#215;200-meter relay squad knew they had the talent and potential to do something historic.</p>
<p>They were right!!!</p>
<p>Sophomore <a title="View athlete profile for Taylor Aska" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/11003763-taylor-aska">Taylor Aska</a>, senior <a title="View athlete profile for Ajanae Thompson" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/9661128-ajanae-thompson">Ajanae Thompson</a>, junior <a title="View athlete profile for Leah Gould" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/10428238-leah-gould">Leah Gould</a>, and sophomore <a title="View athlete profile for Taylor Cox" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/11253110-taylor-cox">Taylor Cox</a> burned up the track at Saturday&#8217;s 115th Millrose Games at the NY Armory by blasting a 1:38.78 to take down the state record!! That broke the state record of 1:38.99 set by Columbia when it finished first at the Millrose Games in 2014, and is the 4th fastest time in the nation this season.</p>
<p>UC finished second in the race behind Bullis of Maryland, who ran 1:37.06.</p>
<p>The Union Catholic girls also dropped a red hot time to win the 4&#215;800</p>
<p>Senior <a title="View athlete profile for Maameyaa Nyinah" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/9434141-maameyaa-nyinah">Maameyaa Nyinah</a> (2:16.80), freshman <a title="View athlete profile for Jimmiea King" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/12289897-jimmiea-king">Jimmiea King</a> (2:20.68), junior<a title="View athlete profile for Peyton Hollis" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/3891242-peyton-hollis"> Peyton Hollis </a>(2:14.83) and<a title="View athlete profile for Kaleigh Gunsiorowski" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/10428253-kaleigh-gunsiorowski"> Kaleigh Gunsiorowski</a> (2:11.82) combined to win in 9:04.12, the second-fastest time in the country this season. It was a strong return for Hollis, who was running her first race since the Non-Public A XC Championships in November. She had been sidelined with a leg injury.</p>
<p>The UC girls 4&#215;400 also had a strong showing as Vonya Gould, Kiara Fleming, Alexandra Moore, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/sports/womens-indoor-track/roster/leilani-gibson/1967" rel="smarttag" rev="1967">Leilani Gibson</a></dfn> combined to run 4:10.90 to finish third.</p>
<p>On the boys side, the UC had a pair of great performances.</p>
<p>The Vikings finished fourth in the 4&#215;800 with a US No. 5 time of 7:55.18, and third in the 4&#215;400 with a NJ No. 2 time of 3:23.45.</p>
<p>In the 4&#215;800, Sean Wilson, <a title="View athlete profile for Ryan Cichocki" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/9434171-ryan-cichocki">Ryan Cichocki</a>, <a title="View athlete profile for Caudell Cajuste" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/9434163-caudell-cajuste">Caudell Cajuste</a>, and <a title="View athlete profile for Jimmy Wischusen" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/10214165-jimmy-wischusen">Jimmy Wischusen</a> carried the baton for the Vikings. Wischusen, a junior, split 1:54.3 on the anchor leg. In the 4&#215;400, Avery Atexide, Al-Qamar Rojas, Emerson McKindra and Marcus McCoy shared the batonl.</p>
