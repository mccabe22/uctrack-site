<h2 class="article-headline">Brown wins 3 golds, Reilly grabs 2 golds to spark UC boys to runner-up finish at State Championships</h2>
<div class="noprint story_email"><a href="https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1dN0jA7KumCMbSqGK38pm508NhWcNXyz4TqlrV-lpiOc/edit#gid=1896537346" target="_blank" rel="noopener">RESULTS</a></div>
<p class="article-subheadline">The Vikings nearly upset defending champion CBA, placing second by just four points</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic boys track and field team, led by triple gold medalist <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=732" rel="smarttag" rev="732">Elijah Brown</a></dfn> and double-winner <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=741" rel="smarttag" rev="741">Conor Reilly</a></dfn>, put together a sensational performance when the Vikings placed second at Saturday&#8217;s NJSIAA Non-Public A Track and Field Championships at the Bennett Center in Toms River.</p>
<p>The Vikings gave defending champion Christian Brothers Academy all they could handle and nearly pulled off the upset before CBA rallied for a hard-fought 76-72 victory as the Colts captured its ninth straight title.</p>
<p>Brown, a junior, burned up the track to lead the charge for Union Catholic.</p>
<p>Brown won the 400 in a NJ#2 49.38, led a 1-2 finish for UC in the 800 with a winning time of 1:56.66, and anchored the winning 4&#215;400 relay with a 49.5 carry. Brown is a major contender to win the 800 at the Meet of Champions next Saturday at the Bennett Center.</p>
<p>Reilly, a junior, also had a huge meet for UC by leading a 1-2-3 finish for the Vikings in the pole vault with a PR equaling 15-0, and Reilly also led off the 4&#215;400 with a 52.3 carry.</p>
<p>Union Catholic took a 62-50 lead with just two events to go when Brown won the 800 and freshman teammate <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=748" rel="smarttag" rev="748">Myles Plummer</a></dfn> finished right behind him in second in 1:58.00. But CBA countered with a 1-2 finish in the 3,200 and then placed second to UC in the 4&#215;400 relay to win the team title.</p>
<p>In addition to his runner-up finish in the 800, Plummer also split 51.4 on the victorious relay.</p>
<p>Several other athletes contributed to UC&#8217;s strong performance.</p>
<p>Senior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=738" rel="smarttag" rev="738">Ryan McLaughlin</a></dfn> was second in the 55 hurdles for UC in a NJ#6 7.55, which is a new personal record. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=747" rel="smarttag" rev="747">Connor Cooper</a></dfn> contributed a 51.7 split for UC on the 4&#215;400, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=729" rel="smarttag" rev="729">Obadare Animashaun</a></dfn> placed second in the pole vault (12-0), <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=750" rel="smarttag" rev="750">Justin Lopez</a></dfn> placed third in the vault (11-6), <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=728" rel="smarttag" rev="728">Elijah Abraham</a></dfn> finished sixth in the 55 hurdles in 8.01, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=730" rel="smarttag" rev="730">Oluwafemi Asenuga</a></dfn> was sixth in the high jump (5-8).</p>
