<h2 class="article-headline">UC girls dominate the Metuchen Relays with 9 wins, and 2 national qualifiers</h2>
<p class="article-subheadline">Lufrano smashed meet record in 5K, Kwasnik had huge PR, and Morant and Evans shined</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic girls solidified its status as arguably the best track and field squad in New Jersey with a remarkable all-round performance at Saturday&#8217;s ninth annual Metuchen Relays.</p>
<p>It all started with <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=404" rel="smarttag" rev="404">Jerika Lufrano</a></dfn>&#8216;s sizzling meet and school record run in the 5K when she blasted a 17:45.79 to qualify for the New Balance Nationals, and ended with the Vikings winning the meet-concluding 4&#215;400-meter relay in 4:07.98, the No. 4 time in N.J. this season.</p>
<p>In between those two first-place finishes, the Vikings won seven other events for a total of nine victories, qualified for New Balance Nationals in the 800 sprint medley, and racked up numerous NJ No. 1 performances.</p>
<p>On the track, sophomore <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=407" rel="smarttag" rev="407">Leena Morant</a></dfn> and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=395" rel="smarttag" rev="395">Khamil Evans</a></dfn> were sensational for UC.</p>
<p>Morant split 2:16.0 to anchor the 4&#215;800 to a come from behind victory in a NJ #2 9:42.47, and dropped a 58.5 on the winning 1,600 sprint medley that rna a NJ #2 4:15.64. Evans ran 24.9 on the winning 4&#215;200 that ran a NJ #1 1:44.60, and split 58.1 as the anchor on the 800 sprint medley that place second in a NJ #2 1:49.71, which qualified for NB Nationals. UC also placed second in the 4&#215;100 in a NJ #2 50.87</p>
<p>UC also dominated the hurdles as <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=396" rel="smarttag" rev="396">Brianna Ewing</a></dfn> exploded with a big personal best and NJ #1 14.87 to win the 100 hurdles and placed second in the 400 hurdles, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=420" rel="smarttag" rev="420">Zamira Ysaac</a></dfn> won the 400 hurdles in a NJ #1 1:06.00. Ysaac also anchored the winning SMR with a 2:23.8 carry.</p>
<p>In the field, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=466" rel="smarttag" rev="466">Alexandra Kwasnik</a></dfn> and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=394" rel="smarttag" rev="394">Asiah Cooper</a></dfn> scored big victories.</p>
<p>Kwasnik, a sophomore, continued her very impressive early season by clearing a personal best 5-2 to win the high jump and she just missed making 5-4. Cooper soared 34-3 to finish first in the triple jump.</p>
