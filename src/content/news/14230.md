<h2 class="article-headline">UC girls burn up the track with pair of red hot wins at Millrose Games Trials</h2>
<p class="article-subheadline">The Vikings won the 4&#215;800 and 4&#215;400 to qualify for the Millrose Games</p>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic girls burned up the track with a pair of sizzling victories at the Millrose Games Trials on Wednesday night at the 168th Street Armory in New York.</p>
<p>In the 4&#215;800-meter relay Invitational, the Vikings rolled to victory in 9:21.86, the fastest time in the country this season. The four runners for UC were <a title="View athlete profile for Ashleigh Patterson" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/6399424" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" data-auth="NotApplicable" aria-label="View athlete profile for Ashleigh Patterson">Ashleigh Patterson</a> (2:22.32), <a title="View athlete profile for Morgan Jackson" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/6399416" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" data-auth="NotApplicable" aria-label="View athlete profile for Morgan Jackson">Morgan Jackson</a> (2:19.69), <a title="View athlete profile for Zamira Ysaac" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/6399434" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" data-auth="NotApplicable" aria-label="View athlete profile for Zamira Ysaac">Zamira Ysaac</a> (2:20.69), and <a title="View athlete profile for Morgan Williams" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/7348298" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" data-auth="NotApplicable" aria-label="View athlete profile for Morgan Williams">Morgan Williams</a> (2:19.14).</p>
<p>The Vikings also dropped a red hot 3:56.34, the No. 1 time in NJ and third fastest time in the nation this season, to win the 4&#215;400 Private School race. UC was in a close battle with Paul VI through three legs,but then UC senior <a title="View athlete profile for Chidumga Nkulume" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/6654610" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" data-auth="NotApplicable" aria-label="View athlete profile for Chidumga Nkulume">Chidumga Nkulume</a> blasted a 56.31 anchor carry to give UC a two second victory over Paul VI, second in a US#7 3:58.34.<br />
