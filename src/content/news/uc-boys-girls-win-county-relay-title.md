<h2 class="article-headline">UC receives several strong performances on the way to 7th Union County Relay title in 8 years</h2>
<div class="noprint story_email"><a href="https://www.addthis.com/bookmark.php?v=250&amp;pub=xa-4a7320e7645958d0"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="https://s7.addthis.com/static/btn/lg-share-en.gif" alt="Bookmark and Share" width="125" height="16" /></a> <time class="article-date">5/6/2018 10:22:00 AM</time></div>
<p class="article-subheadline">The Vikings smashed the school record in the discus on way to 30 point win over Rahway</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic boys track and field team received several great performances on its way to a second straight title and seventh championship in the past eight years at Friday&#8217;s 75th Union County Relays on Friday&#8217;s at Gary Kehler Stadium in Westfield.</p>
<p>The Vikings piled up 107 points to outscore runner-up Rahway by 30 points.</p>
<p>There were lots of highlights for UC.</p>
<p>The trio of <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=365" rel="smarttag" rev="365">Uchenna Duru</a></dfn>, who threw a huge PR and NJ #15 of 156-0, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=380" rel="smarttag" rev="380">Brandon Patterson</a></dfn> (138-2), and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=384" rel="smarttag" rev="384">David Saggio</a></dfn> (129-0) combined to win the discus  with a school record total 423-2. The old school record of 419-6 set in 2014.</p>
<p><dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=381" rel="smarttag" rev="381">Conor Reilly</a></dfn> cleared a meet best 14-0 and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=357" rel="smarttag" rev="357">Obadare Animashaun</a></dfn> made 11-0 to give the Vikings a victory in the team pole vault.</p>
<p>Animashaun also blasted a 22.5 on the 4&#215;200 that finished first in a NJ #6 1:29.42, and ran on the runner-up shuttle hurdles.</p>
<p>The 4&#215;100 team ran a NJ #5 42.80 to place second.</p>
<p><dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=367" rel="smarttag" rev="367">Trevor Garrity</a></dfn> ran a 2:02 on the anchor of the third-place sprint medley, 2:03 on the third-place 4&#215;800, and 4:44 on the third-place 4&#215;1600.</p>
<p><dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=388" rel="smarttag" rev="388">Krishna White</a></dfn> cleared a meet best 6-4 to lead the Vikings to a runner-up finish in the high jump, and went 45-9 to hellp UC get second in the triple jump.</p>
<p><dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=363" rel="smarttag" rev="363">Justin Cadas</a></dfn> went 3:14 on the runner-up distance medley relay, and 1:57 in the 4&#215;800.<br />
&nbsp;</p>

<h2 class="article-headline">UC girls dominate Union County Relays, roll to fourth straight title</h2>
<div class="noprint story_email"><a href="https://www.addthis.com/bookmark.php?v=250&amp;pub=xa-4a7320e7645958d0"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="https://s7.addthis.com/static/btn/lg-share-en.gif" alt="Bookmark and Share" width="125" height="16" /></a> <time class="article-date">5/6/2018 9:25:00 AM</time></div>
<p class="article-subheadline">The Vikings won seven events on the track and smashed two meet records</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic girls track and field team broke two meets records and won eight events during a dominating performance at the 75th Union County Relays when the Vikings rolled to its fourth straight title on Friday at Gary Kehler Stadium in Westfield.</p>
<p>The Vikings, which received contributions from several athletes, won with 99 1/2 points. Westfield was second with 56.</p>
<p>On the opening day of competition on Thursday, UC broke meet records in both the distance medley relay and the 4&#215;100 relay.</p>
<p>In the DMR, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=391" rel="smarttag" rev="391">Alexandra Borik</a></dfn> ran 3:47.9 (a 14 second personal best), Ashleigh Paterson ran a PR of 59.1, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=401" rel="smarttag" rev="401">Morgan Jackson</a></dfn> went 2:20.2 and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=404" rel="smarttag" rev="404">Jerika Lufrano</a></dfn> anchored in 5:08.0 for a meet record 12:17.53, tied for No. 2 in N.J. this season. The former meet record of 12:27.81 was set by New Providence in 2016 .</p>
<p>UC, using <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=415" rel="smarttag" rev="415">Sydney Tucker</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=389" rel="smarttag" rev="389">Kira Alexander</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=395" rel="smarttag" rev="395">Khamil Evans</a></dfn>, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=393" rel="smarttag" rev="393">Amaya Chadwick</a></dfn>, also 47.51 to break its own meet record of 47.76 set last year.</p>
<p>The UC girls now own 8 of the meet records nine meet records on the track. The only one the Vikings don&#8217;t own is the 3&#215;400 hurdles.</p>
<p>The Vikings also ran NJ #1 times to win the 4&#215;200 in 1:40.23 and the sprint medley in 4:07.45, and captured the 4&#215;400 in 3:54.52, the 4&#215;800 in 9:46.01 the 3&#215;400 records in 3:20.44, the triple jump with a combined 101-2 1/4.</p>
<p>In the SMR, junior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=395" rel="smarttag" rev="395">Khamil Evans</a></dfn> blasted a 56.1 400 leg, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=407" rel="smarttag" rev="407">Leena Morant</a></dfn> split 2:19.0, and soph. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=420" rel="smarttag" rev="420">Zamira Ysaac</a></dfn> ran a meet best 1:04.37 in the 400 hurdles, and Tucker ripped a 24.1 in the 4&#215;200. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=413" rel="smarttag" rev="413">Tyler Scott</a></dfn> of UC leaped a meet best 35-7 3/4 in the triple jump.</p>
<p>And for anyone that thinks the Vikings won&#8217;t be as strong next year, the winning 4&#215;800 and DMR teams and the runner-up 4&#215;1,600 team didn&#8217;t use any seniors.</p>
<p>The UC girls now own NJ #1 times in four events-the 4&#215;100 (47.42), 4&#215;200 (1:40.23), 4&#215;800 (9:10.04), and sprint medley (4:07.45), and are NJ #2 in the DMR (12:17.53) and 4&#215;400  (3:54.38).</p>
