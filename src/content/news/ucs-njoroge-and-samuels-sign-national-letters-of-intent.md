<p>5/11/2018 9:16:00 AM</p>
<p>Njoroge will play soccer at Jefferson University, Samuels will continue her T&amp;F career at Wagner</p>
<p>By Jim Lambert</p>
<p>Two members of Union Catholic&#8217;s Class of 2018 gathered with their families, coaches, classmates, and school administrators for a special ceremony in the main lobby of the school on Friday morning to sign their National Letters of Intent.</p>
<p>Josh Njoroge signed to play soccer at Jefferson University in Philadelphia, and <a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=412">Christina Samuels</a> signed to continue her track and field career at Wagner College on Staten Island.</p>
<p>Njoroge, who lives in Union, combined great field vision, remarkable balls skills, and tremendous leadership to become one of the top defensive soccer players in Union County this past season.</p>
<p>The rock steady play in the back by Njoroge, who was a captain for coach <a href="https://ucvikings.com/staff.aspx?staff=14">Tim Breza</a>&#8216;s squad , was a big reason why Union Catholic won its second straight Sky Division title in the Union County Conference, and finished with a 10-4-3 record.</p>
<p>&#8220;This really means the world to me,&#8221; said Njoroge, who wants to be an occupational therapist. &#8220;To see all my hard work, the blood, sweat and tears pay off like this feels great.</p>
<p>Samuels, one of the top throwers in Union County, through a personal best of 35-9 ½ in the shot put when she was fourth at the Union County Indoor Championships this past winter, and has a personal best of 102-10 in the discus, which came when Samuels was fifth at the Union County Championships last spring.</p>
<p>Samuels, who has helped the Vikings win several county and state titles during her great career at UC, placed third in the discus at the Union County Conference Championships yesterday.</p>
<p>&#8220;I&#8217;m really proud of myself because I never really thought I&#8217;d be going to a Division 1 school for track,&#8221; said Samuels, who lives in Roselle. &#8220;But I really worked hard this past year to make this happen, so this really means a lot to me.&#8221;</p>
