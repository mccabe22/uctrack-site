<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Highlights</span><br />
-Greg Gibbs placed 3rd and Brendan McCabe placed 11th in the Freshman Race. Good for the 2nd and 3rd fastest times in UC History. <a href="/?page_id=10655" target="_blank">Class Records</a><br />
-Jimmy Perona won the JV Race in convincing fashion with a time of 17:44. JV Team placed 3rd and every boy ran a personal best!<br />
-Boys Varsity defended their Varsity &#8220;C&#8221; Title with 67 Points. Top Finisher was Andrew Ghizzone. Every boy ran a personal best!<br />
-Girls Varsity won the first Team Title in School History by scoring 37 points. Besting the next team by 31 points! Every girl ran a personal best!<br />
-Asia Johnson led the charge with her first High School Cross Country Victory!<br />
-Girls JV was led by Alexandra Williams and the Girls freshman by Sophia Tangretti.</p>
