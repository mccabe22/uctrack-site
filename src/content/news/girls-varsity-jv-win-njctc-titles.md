<p>The UC Girls successfully defended their Catholic Conference Cross Country Titles at Greystone Park today! The Lady Vikings were led by a 1st Place finish by Jerika Lufrano. The girls averaged 19:32, which is the fastest Team Average in School History! Frosh Daniela Peralta (1st Place) led the JV team to a Perfect Score in victory!</p>
