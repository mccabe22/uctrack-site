<p>The Boys 4&#215;400 ran a US#1 4&#215;400 time of 3:18.80 at the Armory Collegiate Invitational on Friday night. This time is a Union County Record and a School Record!</p>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Splits:</span> Jimerson 49.8, Animashaun 50.9, Z. Ghizzone 50.5, McLaughlin 48.5</p>
<p>On Saturday the Boys 4&#215;200 placed second in a US#3 time of 1:29.44 in the the 4&#215;200 meter relay.<br />
<span style="text-decoration: underline;">Splits:</span> Jimerson 22.2, Z. Ghizzone 22.5, Animashaun 22.5, McLaughlin 22.0</p>
