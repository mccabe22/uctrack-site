<p>PHILADELPHIA, Pa. &#8211;  Union Catholic coach Mike McCabe has been saying all season that his 4&#215;800-meter relay squad had the firepower to run the fastest time in U.S. history if all four legs ran the way they were capable of.</p>
<p>That&#8217;s exactly what happened on Friday when UC&#8217;s pieces all came together perfectly in electrifying, breathtaking and mind-blowing fashion as the combination of sophomore Jimmiea King, senior Peyton Hollis, freshman Sophia Thompson and freshman Paige Sheppard blew up the track at Franklin Field by stopping the clock at a sizzling 8:41.20!!! That shattered the national record as the Vikings left smoke all over the oval when it captured its second straight 4&#215;800 Championship of America title at the 128th Penn Relays.</p>
<p><img decoding="async" src="https://assets.sp.milesplit.com/articles/347264/images/cover-1714177018.png" alt="" /></p>
<p>The Vikings, who threw down a masterclass and masterpiece, smashed the national record and meet record of 8:43.12, which was set at the 2008 Penn Relays by Eleanor Roosevelt of Maryland. UC is just the second US team to ever repeat in the 4&#215;800. NJ power Columbia went back-to-back in 2013 and &#8217;14.</p>
<p><img decoding="async" src="https://assets.sp.milesplit.com/articles/347264/images/union_catholic_coa_4x1_2024_penn_relays.png" alt="" /></p>
<p>UC&#8217;s record-smashing race capped of an epic day for the Vikings, who also finished third in the 4&#215;400 Championship of America 4&#215;400 in 3:41.75, No. 6 in NJ history, and placed 9th in the 4&#215;100 Championship of America race in 46.19!!!!</p>
<p><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/CayleighPenn.jpeg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-full wp-image-14955 alignnone" src="/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/CayleighPenn.jpeg" alt="" width="620" height="413" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/CayleighPenn.jpeg 620w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/04/CayleighPenn-300x200.jpeg 300w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 620px) 100vw, 620px" /></a></p>
<p>That&#8217;s not all. UC freshman Cayleigh Kaiser ran a school record 9:53.93. in the 3K.</p>
<p>The outcome of the 4&#215;800 race was never in doubt as the Vikings simply blew out a loaded field, just like they did last year. Edwin Allen of Jamaica was a distant second in 8:50.76.</p>
<p>UC put the race away after the first two legs when King (2:11.57) and Hollis (2:11.57) opened a 50 meter lead. After that, UC was racing the clock as they chased down Eleanor Roosevelt&#8217;s record. UC, after so many close calls in the past, ran right past Eleanor Roosevelt and became the greatest high school 4&#215;800 squad this country has ever seen!!!!</p>
<p>Thompson went out super fast on the third leg, 61.0 for the first 400,  but through her strong will and toughness was able to hold it together and finish off a huge career-best carry of 2:10.02. Thompson handed the baton to Sheppard with about a 60-meter gap. Sheppard, who runs with so much poise and passion, ran a perfect anchor (63/64) as she split 2:07.86. When Sheppard charged across the line it ignited a wild celebration as the UC foursome all hugged and jumped around on the track in total euphoria. Sheppard&#8217;s anchor leg earned her the Most Outstanding Girls High School Athlete of the Year award for relay events.</p>
<p>Last year, UC ran 8:44.98 to win this race, just missing the national record.</p>
<p>&#8220;This means so much to me and our team after we got so close to the record last year,&#8221; said Hollis, who along with King are the lone returners in the lineup. &#8220;Coach McCabe&#8217;s plan was for us to really hammer the first two legs and try to get as much of a lead as we could. Jimmiea ran perfect, giving me a big lead, and I just kept pushing it. We have so much confidence in Sophia and Paige. We knew they&#8217;d keep the lead and would keep pouring it on.&#8221;</p>
<p>Breaking a national record usually requires a great duel up front as teams trade surges and push each other to fast times.</p>
<p>So taking down a national record without anyone near them makes UC&#8217;s 8:41.20 even more impressive.</p>
<p>&#8220;We wanted to get this record for Peyton since this is her senior year and we know how close they were to breaking it last year,&#8221; said Sheppard. &#8220;My teammates all ran great and I was determined to just give everything I had to get that record. I knew I had a big lead, but I ran like there was someone right on me. We weren&#8217;t leaving her without that record.&#8221;</p>
<p>After UC&#8217;s epic national record run and its red hot times in the 4&#215;100 and 4&#215;400 C of A races, McCabe said he feels his team produced the best day for a U.S. girls program in Penn Relays history.</p>
<p>He&#8217;s not wrong!!!</p>
<p>46.19 &#8211; 9th in the 4&#215;100<br />
3:41.75 &#8211; 3rd in the 4&#215;400<br />
8:41.20  &#8211; 1st place and national record in the 4&#215;800</p>
<p>What US team ever had a triple like that here?</p>
<p>I can&#8217;t find any!!!</p>
