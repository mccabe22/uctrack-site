<p>By Jim Lambert</p>
<p>The Union Catholic girls sprint relay squad dropped a bomb on Sunday by smashing the state record in the 4&#215;200-meter relay at the 116th running of the prestigious Millrose Games at the New York Armory.  </p>
<p>UC freshman Rhia Randolph (24.68), junior Taylor Aska (24.00), freshman Sydney Chadwick (24.61) and junior Taylor Cox (24.75) combined to stop the clock at 1:38.03 to place fourth. That time shattered the state record of 1:38.60 that the Vikings set when they placed fourth at the New Balance Nationals last March in Boston.</p>
<p>That big leadoff leg put the finishing touches on a remarkable double by Randolph.</p>
<p>Earlier in the meet, Randolph finished 6th in the 300 meters in 38.59, the third-fastest time in state history, and the fastest for a non-Olympic gold medalists in NJ high school history. Only Sydney McLaughlin-Levrone of Union Catholic (36.82 in 2017) and Athing Mu of Trenton (37.36 in 2020) have run faster.</p>
<p>The Union Catholic girls also took over the state lead in the 4&#215;800 by placing third in 8:59.98. Delaware&#8217;s Padua (meet record and US No. 1 8:49.86) and Tatnall (8:56.83) finished 1-2. </p>
<p>UC&#8217;s splits were 2:15.70 for freshman Sophia Thompson, 2:11.37 for sophomore Jimmiea King, 2:20.55 for senior Courtney Kaiser, and 2:12.37 for freshman Paige Sheppard.</p>
<p>The UC girls also placed third in 3:53.31 in the 4&#215;400 relay, and senior Jimmy Wischusen, running his first race in two months, finished 11th in the boys mile in 4:17.85.</p>
