<h2 class="article-headline">The UC boys capture the Union County Relay title for seventh time in nine years</h2>
<div class="noprint story_email"><time class="article-date">1/6/2019 12:44:00 PM</time></div>
<p class="article-subheadline">The Vikings edged Elizabeth, 55-52</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic boys track and field team, which scored in all eight events, capped off a spectacular all-round performance by placing second in the meet-concluding 4&#215;400-meter relay to clinch the team title at the Union County Relays on Saturday at the Jersey City Armory.</p>
<p>The Vikings edged Elizabeth, 55-52, to capture its first title since 2016 and its seventh title in the past nine years.</p>
<p>Full results are <a href="https://nj.milesplit.com/meets/337381/results/623713/raw#.XDIqK1xKhPZ">here</a></p>
<p>Several athletes came up huge for Union Catholic.</p>
<p>Two of the big catalysts for UC were <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=732" rel="smarttag" rev="732">Elijah Brown</a></dfn> and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=748" rel="smarttag" rev="748">Myles Plummer</a></dfn> each ran on the first-place distance medley and sprint medley relay teams, and rna a leg on the runner-up 4&#215;400.</p>
<p>Brown, a junior, split 1:55.6 on the 800 leg on the DMR, which crossed first in 10:51.60, ran 50.7 on the SMR, which won in 3:39.54, and had a 50.1 carry in the 400.</p>
<p>Plummer, one of the state&#8217;s top freshman, anchored the DMR with a 4:37.5 carry, split 2:00.9 as the anchor on the SMR, and had a 51.5 anchor carry in the 4&#215;400.</p>
<p><dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=739" rel="smarttag" rev="739">Chinedu Okoro</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=741" rel="smarttag" rev="741">Conor Reilly</a></dfn>, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=747" rel="smarttag" rev="747">Connor Cooper</a></dfn> also had big days for UC.</p>
<p>Okoro ran 23.1 on the SMR, split 24.1 on the fourth-place 4&#215;200, and cleared 5-6 on the runner-up high jump team.</p>
<p>Reilly, one of the state&#8217;s top pole vaulters, ran 52.7 in the 4&#215;400 and 23.3 on the fourth-place 4&#215;400.</p>
<p>Cooper had splits of 53.5 for 400 on the DMR, and 24.2 in the 4&#215;200.</p>
<p>In other UC highlights, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=731" rel="smarttag" rev="731">Matt Axt</a></dfn> came up with a clutch 54.1 leg in the 400. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=738" rel="smarttag" rev="738">Ryan McLaughlin</a></dfn> and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=736" rel="smarttag" rev="736">Brandon Leslie</a></dfn> each split 7.5 on the runner-up shuttle hurdle team, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=729" rel="smarttag" rev="729">Obadare Animashaun</a></dfn> led off the shuttle hurdles and ran a 24-6 in the SMR, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=730" rel="smarttag" rev="730">Oluwafemi Asenuga</a></dfn> cleared 5-10 in the high jump, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=733" rel="smarttag" rev="733">Uchenna Duru</a></dfn> (46-1 1/2), <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=743" rel="smarttag" rev="743">Jared Ware</a></dfn> (43-4 1/4) and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=737" rel="smarttag" rev="737">Ray Lestarchick</a></dfn> (39-7) combined to give UC a runner-up finish in the shot put.</p>

<h2 class="article-headline">UC girls win 5 events, break meet record on way to 6th straight Union County Relay title</h2>
<div class="noprint story_email"> <time class="article-date">1/6/2019 7:15:00 PM</time></div>
<p class="article-subheadline">The Vikings scored 66 out of a possible 80 points</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic girls track and field team won five races, broke a meet record, and ran a NJ #1 time on the way to winning its sixth straight title at the Union County Relays on Saturday at the Jersey City Armory.</p>
<p>Union Catholic, which scored 66 out of a possible 80 points and outscored runner-up Summit by 34 points, won the distance medley relay in a NJ#1 12:38.83, the 4&#215;200 in a meet record 1:44.86, the 4&#215;400 in 4:06.40, the 4&#215;800 in 9:59.04, and the shuttle hurdles in 33.79. UC also ran a NJ#3 4:14.78 to place second in the sprint medley to Gov. Livingston&#8217;s NJ#2 4:13.63.</p>
<p>Several athletes shined for the Vikings.</p>
<p><dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=762" rel="smarttag" rev="762">Jerika Lufrano</a></dfn> anchored the DMR with a 5:06.7 carry. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=771" rel="smarttag" rev="771">Morgan Williams</a></dfn> had splits of 2:23.6 in the 4&#215;800 and 2:29.1 in the DMR. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=766" rel="smarttag" rev="766">Ashleigh Patterson</a></dfn> anchored the 4&#215;800 (2:26.8) and 4&#215;400 (62.2).  <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=712" rel="smarttag" rev="712">Khamil Evans</a></dfn> ran 59.3 in the SMR and 26.0 in the 4&#215;200. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=769" rel="smarttag" rev="769">Sydney Tucker</a></dfn> split 25.5 in the 4&#215;200 and 25.7 in the SMR. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=781" rel="smarttag" rev="781">Chi Chi Nkulume</a></dfn> went 59.0 in the 4&#215;400 and 26.2 in the 4&#215;200. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=786" rel="smarttag" rev="786">A&#8217;Liyah Thomas</a></dfn> cleared 5-0 on the runner-up high jump team, split 8.2 in the shuttles, and went 26.6 in the SMR. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=774" rel="smarttag" rev="774">Zamira Ysaac</a></dfn> anchored the SMR in 2:22.7 and ran 7.9 on the shuttles, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=770" rel="smarttag" rev="770">Angelique Williams</a></dfn> cleared 5-4 in the HJ.</p>
