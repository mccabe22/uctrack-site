<header>
<p class="article-byline">The Union Catholic cross-country teams swept varsity team titles and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/sports/womens-cross-country/roster/paige-sheppard/3263" rel="smarttag" rev="3263" data-airgap-id="46">Paige Sheppard</a></dfn> shattered a record at Saturday&#8217;s 49th Shore Coaches Invitational at Holmdel Park.</p>
</header>
<p>In the girls competition, Sheppard torched the trails in the girls&#8217; E Division race by burning a 17:41, which smashed the sophomore course record and placed Sheppard No. 9 all-time on Holmdel&#8217;s all-time list! It&#8217;s the second straight Shore Coaches title for Sheppard, who said she was seeking redemption for her runner-up finish at the Meet of Champions last year when she ran 17:54.</p>
<p>The 17:41 by Sheppard broke the sophomore course record of 17:47 set by Camryn Wennersten of Ridgewood at the 2019 Shore Coaches Invitational and sparked N.J. No.1/ U.S. No. 7 Union Catholic to the second-fastest average in course history.</p>
<p>The UC girls once again flexed their superior firepower with another epic performance as the Vikings dropped a 5-runner average of 18:46, and nearly threw a shutout. UC set the record of 18:37 at the Meet of Champions last year.</p>
<p>The scoring combination for UC&#8217;s all-underclassmen squad was 1-2-3-5-6 for 17 points!! UC also finished on top in the team merge with just 48 points!! Ocean City was second with 341.</p>
<p>After Sheppard, UC&#8217;s scorers were sophomore <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/sports/womens-cross-country/roster/cayleigh-kaiser/3260" rel="smarttag" rev="3260" data-airgap-id="47">Cayleigh Kaiser</a></dfn>, second in a huge Holmdel PR of 18:26, sophomore <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/sports/womens-cross-country/roster/sophia-thompson/3265" rel="smarttag" rev="3265" data-airgap-id="48">Sophia Thompson</a></dfn>, third in 18:37, junior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/sports/womens-cross-country/roster/jimmiea-king/3261" rel="smarttag" rev="3261" data-airgap-id="49">Jimmiea King</a></dfn> fifth, in 19:26, and junior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/sports/womens-cross-country/roster/ella-solorzano/3264" rel="smarttag" rev="3264" data-airgap-id="50">Ella Solorzano</a></dfn>, sixth in 19:41. The times by Thompson and King were also Holmdel PR&#8217;s.</p>
<p>There doesn&#8217;t seem to be any way to stop the UC Express from getting the 3-Peat at the Meet of Champions next month.</p>
<p>On the boys side, the Vikings, ranked No. 7 in the state, averaged 16:54 and rolled to a 40-115 victory over Pingry.</p>
<p>UC&#8217;s top five were Evan Weber, third in 16:38, Finn Riely, fifth in a big Holmdel PR of 16:49, Michael Devine, sixth in 16:49, Elijah McCoy, 15th in a Holmdel PR f 17:09, and Aidan Cichocki, 16th in 17:10.</p>
