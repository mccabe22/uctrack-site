<p>The Lady Vikings rang up 144 points, more than 100 more than runner-up Elizabeth, second with 39.</p>
<p>UC received six victories and several sizzling performances.</p>
<p>Sophomore <a title="View athlete profile for Abby Granrath" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/8233810">Abby Granrath </a>was a double winner for UC, junior <a title="View athlete profile for A'Liyah Thomas" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/7923268">A&#8217;Liyah Thomas</a> was her usual splendid all-round self, and<a title="View athlete profile for Isabella Gray" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/7608382"> Isabella Gray</a>, <a title="View athlete profile for Chidumga Nkulume" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/6654610">Chidumga Nkulume</a> and the 4&#215;400 squad all picked up gold medals for the Vkinigs.</p>
<p>Granrath, the Union County XC champ this past fall, won the 1,600 in 5:14.27 and the 3,200 in 11:34.21.</p>
<p>Thomas extended her state lead with a PR of 18-9 1/2 to lead a 1-2-3 finish for UC in the long jump, placed second in the high jump (matching her PR of 5-4), and was fifth in the 55 dash in 7.48. Gray cleared 10-6 to win the pole vault.</p>
<p><a title="View athlete profile for Chidumga Nkulume" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/6654610">Nkulume</a> won her first county title by crossing first in the 400 in 57.37. She ran a NJ#3 56.89 last month at the Bullis Invit. at the NY Armory.  She was also second in the 200 in 25.46. And the 4&#215;400 team won in 3:59.59.</p>
<p>Also for UC, Alexandra Bonn finished second in the LJ with PR and NJ#9 17-8 1/2, Sadie Abood placed second in the 3,200 with a PR of 11:39.37, <a title="View athlete profile for Jada Mia Puryear" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/8233816">Jada Mia Puryear </a>was second in the shot with a PR of 35-8 1/2.</p>
<p><a title="View athlete profile for Ajanae Thompson" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/9661128">Ajanae Thompson</a> was fourth in the dash in 7.38 and fifth in the 55 hurdles in 8.85, <a title="View athlete profile for Morgan Williams" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/7348298">Morgan Williams</a> second in the 800 in 2:19.61,<a title="View athlete profile for Zamira Ysaac" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/6399434"> Zamira Ysaac</a> fourth in the 800 in 2:20.28,<a title="View athlete profile for Ashleigh Patterson" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/6399424"> and Ashleigh Patterson</a> was fifth in 2:20.52. <a title="View athlete profile for Felicia Quainoo" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/6399429">Felicia Quainoo</a> was second in the 55m hurdles in 8.49, and third in the LJ, and <a title="View athlete profile for Avery Jordan" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/7348296">Avery Jordan</a> was sixth in the HH in 8.90. <a title="View athlete profile for Autumn Barge" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/7474064">Autumn Barge</a> placed fifth in the girls PV at 7-6, and <a title="View athlete profile for Sydney McDonald" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/8233813">Sydney McDonald</a> placed sixth in the 200 in 26.21</p>
<p>MORE HIGHLIGHTS</p>
