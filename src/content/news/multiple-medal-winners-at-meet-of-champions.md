<h2 class="article-headline">UC girls break county record, win 4&#215;100 relay at State Meet of Champions</h2>
<div class="noprint story_email"><time class="article-date">6/10/2019 7:43:00 AM</time></div>
<p class="article-subheadline">Morant and 4&#215;400 relay place second, Brown and Duru each place third for boys</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic girls 4&#215;100-meter relay team wanted to make a big statement at Saturday&#8217;s NJSIAA Meet of Champions at Northern Burlington High School in Columbus.</p>
<p>And the Vikings delivered by sending a message to the rest of the state that was heard loud and clear by burning a 47.05 to finish first and take down the Union County record in the 4&#215;100 relay. Eastern was second in 47.36.</p>
<p>UC&#8217;s foursome of <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=982" rel="smarttag" rev="982">Chi Chi Nkulume</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=415" rel="smarttag" rev="415">Sydney Tucker</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=395" rel="smarttag" rev="395">Khamil Evans</a></dfn> and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=411" rel="smarttag" rev="411">Felicia Quainoo</a></dfn>, running together for the first time this season in the 4&#215;100, took down the Union County record of 47.07 that Summit ran to win the M of C title in 2017. UC&#8217;s 47.05 is also the fastest time in the state this season.</p>

While the 4&#215;100 victory was the biggest highlight of the meet for UC, there were several other impressive performances turned in by the Vikings.</p>
<p>For the girls, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=407" rel="smarttag" rev="407">Leena Morant</a></dfn> placed second in the 800 in a school record 2:08,91 the third fastest time in the state this season, and anchored the 4&#215;400 relay that placed second in 3:49.37. Tucker finished fourth in the 100 dash in 11.9, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=404" rel="smarttag" rev="404">Jerika Lufrano</a></dfn> ran a school record 4:57.40 to place fourth in the 1,600, the 4&#215;800 squad finished third in 9:28.01, Quainoo placed seventh in the triple jump (38-0 1/2), and Nkulume was seventh in the 400 in 56.76.</p>
<p>For the UC boys, junior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=362" rel="smarttag" rev="362">Elijah Brown</a></dfn> finished third in the 800 in 1:54,.53, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=365" rel="smarttag" rev="365">Uchenna Duru</a></dfn> was third in the discus with a personal best throw of 175-10, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=381" rel="smarttag" rev="381">Conor Reilly</a></dfn> tied for fourth in the pole vault (14-6), the 4&#215;100 team placed sixth in 42.64, the 4&#215;400 squad was ninth (3:21.18), and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=957" rel="smarttag" rev="957">Myles Plummer</a></dfn> placed ninth in the 800 in 1:56.15.</p>
