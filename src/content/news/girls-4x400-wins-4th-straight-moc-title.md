<h2 class="article-headline">Union Catholic&#8217;s 4&#215;400 wins record 4th straight title at Meet of Champions</h2>
<p class="article-subheadline">Bennet-Jones, Evans, Ysaac and Morant combine on the victory</p>
<p class="article-byline">Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic girls, one of the most dominant track and field programs in recent state history, added to its remarkable legacy with a historic victory in the 4&#215;400-meter relay at Saturday&#8217;s NJSIAA Meet of Champions at the Bennett Center in Toms River.</p>
<p>When UC sophomore <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=118" rel="smarttag" rev="118">Leena Morant</a></dfn> crossed the finish line to put the finishing touches on the victory in 3:56.90, it gave the Vikings an unprecedented fourth straight M of C title in the 4&#215;400. No boys or girls teams indoors or outdoors had ever won four straight 4&#215;400 titles at the M of C. The Columbia girls captured three in a row twice indoors (1995-97 and 2012-14) and the UC boys won three in a row two indoors and outdoors from 2013-15.</p>
<p>Senior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=101" rel="smarttag" rev="101">Mia Bennett-Jones</a></dfn> led off the 4&#215;400 with a split of 61.1. Junior<strong> </strong><dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=106" rel="smarttag" rev="106">Khamil Evans</a></dfn> then ran 58.6 before handing off to sophomore <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=130" rel="smarttag" rev="130">Zamira Ysaac</a></dfn> (59.4). Morant closed it out with a 58.4 carry.</p>
<p>UC had several other medal winners at the meet.</p>
<p>The 4&#215;800 team of <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=102" rel="smarttag" rev="102">Alexandra Borik</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=120" rel="smarttag" rev="120">Ashleigh Patterson</a></dfn>, Ysaac, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=112" rel="smarttag" rev="112">Morgan Jackson</a></dfn> placed third in the 4&#215;800 in 9:28.97. Evans was eighth in the 400 in 58.21, and Morant placed fifth in the 800 in 2:15.45.</p>
