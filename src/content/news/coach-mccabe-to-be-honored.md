<p>Coach McCabe has earned national recognition by the National Federation of Coaches Association (NFCA) and will be honored at the New Jersey Scholastic Coaches Association Hall of Fame Awards Program.  He will receive the NFCA Coach of the Year award for Boys Indoor Track and Field.</p>
<p>Congratulations Coach McCabe!</p>
