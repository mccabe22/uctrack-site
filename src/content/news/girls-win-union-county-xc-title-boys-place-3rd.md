<strong>Warinanco Park, Elizabeth</strong></p>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Results:</span> <a href="/Reports/Meet_Results/Boys/PDF/Boys-2014_10_22-Warinanco%20Park-Union%20County%20Championship_ID534.pdf">Boys</a> | <a href="/Reports/Meet_Results/Girls/PDF/Girls-2014_10_22-Warinanco%20Park-Union%20County%20Championship_ID460.pdf">Girls</a></p>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Highlights</span><br />
-Girls Varsity Wins the Team Championship for the 1st Time in School History!<br />
-Boys Varsity Places 3rd with a 17:21 Team Average!<br />
-Boys JV Places 3rd!<br />
-Girls Team Average is a School Record 19:55! Led by a 1-2 Finish by Caitlin Glynn &amp; Asia Johnson!<br />
-Caitlin breaks the School Record with a time of 19:05. She is the 1st Female Union County Champ from UC!<br />
-Asia breaks the UC Junior Class Record with a time of 19:19!<br />
-Nate Papachristos breaks the UC Sophomore Class Record with a time of 16:30!<br />
-Andre Rodriguez breaks the UC Freshman Class Record with a time of 18:03!</p>
