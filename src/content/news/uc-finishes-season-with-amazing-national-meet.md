<h2 class="article-headline">UC track and field teams conclude season with sensational showing at New Balance Nationals</h2>
<div class="noprint story_email"><time class="article-date">6/17/2019 7:02:00 AM</time></div>
<p class="article-subheadline">The girls won the 4&#215;800 relay, and the boys and girls combined for five All-American finishes</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic girls track and field team once again showed why it&#8217;s one of the top programs in the country when the Vikings put the finishing touches on one of the greatest seasons in state history with a remarkable showing at the New Balance National Championships that concluded on Sunday at Aggie Stadium on the campus of North Carolina A&amp;T University in Greensboro, N.C.</p>
<p>The UC boys also had a very strong meet, highlighted by a runner-up and All-American finish in the sprint medley relay.</p>
<p>In a mind-boggling display of depth and dominance, the UC girls, who went undefeated in team competition this season, flexed its superior firepower by lighting up the track with a victory in the 4&#215;800-meter relay, earning All-American in a total of four relays, placing in the top 10 in two other relays, and recording some of the fastest times ever run in state history.</p>
<p>The biggest highlight was the victory in the 4&#215;800 on Saturday when <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=417" rel="smarttag" rev="417">Morgan Williams</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=404" rel="smarttag" rev="404">Jerika Lufrano</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=409" rel="smarttag" rev="409">Ashleigh Patterson</a></dfn>, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=407" rel="smarttag" rev="407">Leena Morant</a></dfn> combined to finish first in 8:59.78, which broke its own Union County record and moved the Vikings up to No. 8 in state history. Williams split 2:16.78, Lufrano ran 2:14.43, Patterson split 2:18.40, and Morant anchored in 2:10.17. The 2:14.43 by Lufrano, which gave UC the lead, was the fastest second leg split of all the teams competing.</p>
<p>UC also earned All-American honors by placing second in the shuttle hurdles in 58.60, which is No. 2 in state history, third in the 4&#215;200 in 1:38.11, which is No. 3 in state history, and fourth in the 4&#215;400 in 3:42.97, which No. 5 in state history. The Vikings also placed eighth in the distance medley relay in 12:07.11, and ran 47.06 in the 4&#215;100 trials, just .01 off its state leading and Union County record of 47.05 that the Vikings ran to finish first at the NJSIAA Meet of Champions.</p>
<p>The Vikings, which won the sprint medley relay at the New Balance National Indoor Championships in March, finished with the No. 1 times in the state for the season in the 4&#215;100, 4&#215;200, 4&#215;400, 4&#215;800, and the shuttle hurdles, and finished No. 3 in the state in the distance medley, and No. 6 in the sprint medley (4:07.72).</p>
<p>Several athletes shined for the UC girls.</p>
<p><dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=982" rel="smarttag" rev="982">Chi Chi Nkulume</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=395" rel="smarttag" rev="395">Khamil Evans</a></dfn> and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=415" rel="smarttag" rev="415">Sydney Tucker</a></dfn> all ran on the 4&#215;100, 4&#215;200, and the 4&#215;400 for UC. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=402" rel="smarttag" rev="402">Avery Jordan</a></dfn> ran on the 4&#215;200 and the shuttle hurdles, Felicia Quianoo ran on the 4&#215;100 and shuttle hurdles. Morant anchored the 4&#215;400, 4xx800, and placed 10th in the 800 in 2:09.27. In addition to her strong 800 leg Lufrano finished fourth in the Emerging Elite mile in a personal best 4:57.13, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=466" rel="smarttag" rev="466">Alexandra Kwasnik</a></dfn> placed fourth in the Emerging Elite high jump with a personal best 5-7.</p>
<p>For the UC boys, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=947" rel="smarttag" rev="947">Connor Cooper</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=378" rel="smarttag" rev="378">Chinedu Okoro</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=463" rel="smarttag" rev="463">Tyhir Jones</a></dfn>, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=362" rel="smarttag" rev="362">Elijah Brown</a></dfn> nearly captured the sprint medley relay title by placing second in 3:26.48, just behind the 3:26.26 run by the winner, Hall Track Club of Connecticut. UC also finished 13th in the boys 4&#215;400 in 3:18.56. Brown anchored both the SMR (split of 1:53.42) and the 4&#215;400 (48.04), and Jones and Cooper ran on the SMR and 4&#215;400.</p>
<p><dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=957" rel="smarttag" rev="957">Myles Plummer</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=365" rel="smarttag" rev="365">Uchenna Duru</a></dfn>, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=381" rel="smarttag" rev="381">Conor Reilly</a></dfn> also turned in great performances for the Vikings.</p>
<p>Plummer won the Freshman 800 in a personal best 1:55.52, placed second in the Freshman mile in 4:23.26, and he ran a leg on the 4&#215;400. Duru placed 12th in the championship discus division with a personal best 178-3, No. 4 in the state this season, and Reilly cleared 15-1 1/2 to place 11th in the Emerging Elite pole vault.</p>
