<h2 class="article-headline">UC Girls Wins 7th straight State Non-Pubic A Relay Title, boys place third</h2>
<p class="article-byline"><span style="text-decoration: underline;">By:</span> Jim Lambert</p>
<p class="article-byline">The names keep changing, but the winning ways remain the same for the Union Catholic girls.</p>
</header>
<p>Year after year, the powerhouse track and field program loses All-State athletes and several key point scorers to graduation, but new stars keep emerging to maintain their status as the best program in the state.</p>
<p>The latest example came on Friday when Union Catholic blitzed the competition en route to a seventh straight title at the NJSIAA Non-Public A Relays at the Bennett Center in Toms River. The Vikings racked up 72 of a possible 90 points to defeat runner-up Paramus Catholic by 40 points</p>
<p>UC&#8217;s seven straight titles is the second most for a girls program in meet history behind the nine in a row that Hopewell Valley captured from 2002-10.</p>
<p>The UC boys were short-handed as some of the top runners didn&#8217;t compete, but the Vikings still finished a very solid third in the team standings.</p>
<p>In the girls competition, UC began its march to the title by burning a US No. 1 32.96 to win the shuttle hurdles. The Vikings also received a NJ No.2/US No. 8 throw of 42-10.75 from senior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1330" rel="smarttag" rev="1330">Jada Mia Puryear</a></dfn>, who broke her own school record, and UC finished first in the 4&#215;200 in a NJ No. 2 1:46.98, first in the sprint medley relay in 4:22.03, and second in the high jump, 4&#215;400, 4&#215;800, and DMR.</p>
<p>UC&#8217;s shuttle hurdles consisted of Taylor Cox (7.8), <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1331" rel="smarttag" rev="1331">Ajanae Thompson</a></dfn> (7.7), McKenzie Johnson, and <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1328" rel="smarttag" rev="1328">Alexandra Bonn</a></dfn> (7.9). Thompson, who was second in the 100 hurdles at the Meet of Champions last June, also split 26.0 on the 4&#215;200, and Cox went 26.7 on the 4&#215;200. Bonn was also a member of the runner-up high jump squad. <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1345" rel="smarttag" rev="1345">Maameyaa Nyinah</a></dfn> had a very impressive anchor carry of 2:22.2 for UC in the SMR, and <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1333" rel="smarttag" rev="1333">Asydiah Pray-Brown</a></dfn> ran a 2:26.9 in the 4&#215;800 for UC. UC junior Seton Dill was the only girl in the meet to clear a height in the pole vault as she made a personal best 7-0. Dill also ran on the 4&#215;800. Simone Motley teamed with Puryear to win the shot put.</p>
<p>The hottest race of the meet came in the girls distance medley relay when Kent Place and UC hooked up in a great duel. Kent Place emerged with the victory in 12:25.17 and UC was second in 12:28.50 as the Union County distance powers ran the second and third fastest times in the nation this season.</p>
<p>UC&#8217;s DMR splits were 3:49 for <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1305" rel="smarttag" rev="1305">Abby Granrath</a></dfn>, 64.4 for Erica Dines, 2:25.0 for <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1577" rel="smarttag" rev="1577">Leilani Gibson</a></dfn>, and 5:09.0 for Kaleigh Gunsiorowski. Gibson also split 63.5 leading off the SMR.</p>
<p>&#8220;I am proud of the girls,&#8221; said UC coach <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/coaches.aspx?rc=57" rel="smarttag" rev="57">Mike McCabe</a></dfn>. &#8220;They have worked hard to win this title. Great to see our seniors win this meet every year. I&#8217;m excited to see them progress throughout the year.&#8221;</p>
<p>On the boys side, the Vikings scored 44 points to place third behind Christian Brothers Academy, first with 72, and Seton Hall Prep, second with 49.50.</p>
<p>The Vikings won the shuttle hurdles in 32.67, and finished first in the high jump, and placed second in 4&#215;800, second in the 4&#215;200, and second in the pole vault.</p>
<p>Nick Givan led the charge for UC. The senior all-round star ran on the shuttles, cleared 6-0 in the high jump, and led off the 4&#215;200 with a 23.0 split. Noah Cooper and Al-Qamar Rojas each ran on the shuttles and 4&#215;200. Aidan Reidy (9-6) and Matthew Schutz (9-0) combined to place second in the pole vault for UC. Justice Hicks was on the runner-up HJ team, and Caudell Cajuste ran 2:05.3 in the 4&#215;800.</p>
