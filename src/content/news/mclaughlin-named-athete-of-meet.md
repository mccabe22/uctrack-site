<p>Congratulations to Sydney McLaughlin for being named Outstanding Female Varsity Athlete of the Meet at the Bishop Loughlin Games! Sydney earned this honor because of her 300 meter victory(38.55), which broke the State Record and just missed the National Freshman Class Record! <em>(Sydney pictured below with legend Ed Bowes)</em></p>
