<h2 class="article-headline">UC girls nearly record a perfect score, roll to 5th straight Union County Relay title</h2>
<p class="article-subheadline">The Vikings scored a meet record 76 out of a possible 80 points</p>
<p class="article-byline">Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic girls track and field team produced the most dominant performance in meet history when the Vikings nearly recorded a perfect score and smashed a meet record en route to winning the Union County Relay title for the fifth straight year on Sunday at the Jersey City Armory.</p>
<p><a href="http://nj.milesplit.com/meets/297276/results/563980/raw#.Wlz5Yd-nFPY">Full results of the meet</a></p>
<p>The Vikings won seven of the eight events, including NJ #1 times in the shuttle hurdles and sprint medley relay, and racked up a remarkable meet record 76 points out of possible 80 on the way to a 76-29 victory over runner-up Rahway. UC broke the meet record of 72 points that they set last year.</p>
<p>UC won the shuttle hurdles in a NJ #1 32.12, the sprint medley in a NJ #1 4:10.66, the distance medley in 12:34.79, the 4&#215;200 in 1:46.19, the 4&#215;400 in a meet record 4:05.67, the 4&#215;800 in 10:04.23, and the high jump at 10-0. The only event UC didn&#8217;t win was the shot put where the Vikings placed third, led by <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=116" rel="smarttag" rev="116">Sophia Martinez</a></dfn>&#8216;s 35-1 1/4, which was the second best throw of the meet.</p>
<p>Sophomore <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=118" rel="smarttag" rev="118">Leena Morant</a></dfn> had a huge day for UC with a 2:15.0 800 anchor split in the SMR, and a 59.2 carry in the 4&#215;400. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=115" rel="smarttag" rev="115">Jerika Lufrano</a></dfn> ripped a 5:05 to anchor the winning DMR team, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=128" rel="smarttag" rev="128">Angelique Williams</a></dfn> cleared a meet best 5-2 in the high jump.</p>
<p>The Vikings, who now own the top times in N.J. this season in the shuttle hurdles, 4&#215;400, 4&#215;800, SMR amd DMR, will be heavily favored to repeat as champions at this Thursday&#8217;s Non-Public State Relay Championships at 4 p.m. the Bennett Center in Toms River.</p>
