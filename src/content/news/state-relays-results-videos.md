<aside class="summary cf"><em>Toms River, N.J. &#8211; The Vikings received several outstanding performances at Thursday&#8217;s NJSIAA Non-Public A Relays</em></aside>
<section class="body cf">
<div>
<p>The <strong>Union Catholic girls</strong> won the final two events on the track to secure its third straight title and the boys placed second at the NJSIAA Non-Public A Relay Championships on Thursday night at the Bennett Center in Toms River.</p>
<p>The UC girls were clinging to a 43-42 lead over Monmouth County powerhouse Red Bank Catholic when the Vikings closed out the meet by winning the sprint medley relay in 4:15.64 and the 4&#215;400 relay in 4:03.75 to win the team title, 63-42, over runner-up RBC.</p>
<p>In addition to its victory in the SMR and 4&#215;400, the UC girls also won the 4&#215;200 in a N.J. No. 1 1:44.58, and the shuttle hurdles in 31.83, and placed second in the distance medley relay and third in the 4&#215;800.</p>
<p>For the Vikings, Olympian<strong> Sydney McLaughlin</strong> ran 24.2 in the 4&#215;200 and 55.8 for the 400 in the SMR. <strong>Amaya Chadwick</strong> led off the shuttle hurdles with a 7.6 and ran 26.2 in the 4&#215;200.<strong> Felicia Quainoo</strong> ran 7.6 in the hurdles, 26.8 in the 4&#215;200, and 61.8 in the 4&#215;400. <strong>Leena Morant</strong> split 2:19.9 in the SMR, and  2:26.1 in the 4&#215;800. <strong>Cassie Lamadieu</strong> ran 59.2 for 400 in the DMR, and 60.0 in the 4&#215;400, and <strong>Jerika Lufrano</strong> anchored the DMR with a 5:20.2 carry and ran 2:29.4 in the 4&#215;800.</p>
<p>On the boys side, the Vikings had a strong meet, placing second in the distance medley, 4&#215;800 and 4&#215;400 on the way to a runner-up finish to defending champion Christian Brothers Academy, 86-52. In the DMR, UC’s time of 10:29.49 is No. 3 in N.J. this season.</p>
<p>For UC, freshman Elijah Brown had a strong meet for UC, splitting 52.5 for his 400 leg in the distance medley, and ran 50.9 in the 4&#215;400. Justin Cadas anchored the DMR with a 4:24.5 carry and ran 2:01.3 in the SMR. Freshman Conor Reilly cleared 12-0 in the pole vault. Greg Gibbs ran 1:58.5 for 800 in the SMR. Nate Papachristos ran 3:12.8 in the DMR and 2:02.1 in the 4&#215;800, and Makay Johnson split 22.5 in the 4&#215;200, and 50.9 in the 4&#215;400.</p></div>
