<p>The Union Catholic girls won the Nike Cross Regionals Northeast championship on November 21 at Bowdoin Park in Wappingers Falls, New York. Racing as Union Catholic XC Club, the Vikings scored 38 points in the Northeast championship 5K. Ridgewood was second and Penn Charter was third, both with 121 points.</p><p>Union Catholic’s five scorers averaged 18:14.9 and finished within 46.5 seconds of one another. The win earned the Vikings an automatic berth in Nike Cross Nationals on December 5 in Portland, Oregon.</p>
