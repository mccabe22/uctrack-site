<p><strong>New Jersey Catholic Track Conference Championship Meet<br />
</strong><strong>Sunday, February 10, 2013<br />
</strong><strong>Jersey City Armory</strong></p>
<p>Boys Team wins the Team Championship! 80 points to CBA&#8217;s 46 points. Girls team places 6th.  <span style="text-decoration: underline;">Results:</span> <a href="/Reports/Meet_Results/Boys-2013_02_10-Jersey%20City%20Armory-NJCTC%20Championship_ID441.pdf">Boys</a> | <a href="/Reports/Meet_Results/Girls-2013_02_10-Jersey%20City%20Armory-NJCTC%20Championship_ID373.pdf">Girls</a><br />

Highlights</span><br />
-Imani Beauliere shatters two School Records (55m dash-7.28 &amp; 300m dash-41.94)<br />
-Jenny Herron breaks the School Record in the 1000m (3:13.21), while freshman Asia Johnson breaks the former record (3:15.77) for 6th place.<br />
-Boys win 4 Events (55 Hurdles, 300m, 600m, 4&#215;400 relay)<br />
-Jordan Jimerson breaks the 300m School Record with a time of 35.61. Taylor McLaughin finishes 2nd in 36.66, also breaking the former record.<br />
-Andrew Ghizzone wins the 600m and places 2nd in the 1000m.<br />
-Christoffe Scott wins the 55 Hurdles with a personal best performance of 7.60. Zach Ghizzone 2nd in 7.67 and Obafemi Animashaun 5th in 7.77</p>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Performers of the Meet:</span> Imani Beauliere, Andrew Ghizzone and Owen Igori</p>
