<h2 class="article-headline">Union Catholic boys and girls sweep titles at Union County Championships</h2>
<p><strong><a href="https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1dN0jA7KumCMbSqGK38pm508NhWcNXyz4TqlrV-lpiOc/edit?usp=sharing" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Results</a> | <a href="https://nj.milesplit.com/meets/337422/videos#.XFHWX89Khm8" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Videos</a> | <a href="https://nj.milesplit.com/photos/albums/43070" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Photos</a></strong></p>
<p class="article-subheadline">The girls have now won six championships in a row and the boys two straight</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>Union Catholic&#8217;s dominance at the Union County Track and Field Championships continued on Saturday when the Vikings swept the team titles for the second straight year at the Jersey City Armory.</p>
<p>It&#8217;s the sixth straight title for the UC girls, which rolled to a 115-47 victory over runner-up Kent Place. The UC boys outscored cross-town rival Scotch Plains, 62-50, for its second championship in a row.</p>
<p>Union Catholic also swept the team titles at the county relays earlier this month.</p>
<p>In the girls competition, UC lit up the track with several sizzling performances.</p>
<p><dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=769" rel="smarttag" rev="769">Sydney Tucker</a></dfn> blasted a 7.13, the second fastest time in N.J. this season, to win the 55 dash, and she ran on the winning 4&#215;400 that went 4:03.45. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=762" rel="smarttag" rev="762">Jerika Lufrano</a></dfn> won the 1,600 in 5:13.04 and the 3,200 in 11:13.44. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=768" rel="smarttag" rev="768">Felicia Quainoo</a></dfn> won the 55 hurdles in 8.45. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=764" rel="smarttag" rev="764">Leena Morant</a></dfn> finished first in the 800 in 2:19.49 and was fourth in the 400 (58.34). <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=781" rel="smarttag" rev="781">Chi Chi Nkulume</a></dfn> was second in both the 55 dash (7.28) and the 400 (57.86). <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=771" rel="smarttag" rev="771">Morgan Williams</a></dfn> ran on the winning 4&#215;400 and was second in the 800 (2:20.04). <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=774" rel="smarttag" rev="774">Zamira Ysaac</a></dfn> ran on the 4&#215;400 and was fourth in the 800 (2:23.49), and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=770" rel="smarttag" rev="770">Angelique Williams</a></dfn> cleared 5-4 to place second in the high jump.</p>
<p>In the boys competition, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=732" rel="smarttag" rev="732">Elijah Brown</a></dfn> and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=748" rel="smarttag" rev="748">Myles Plummer</a></dfn> led the charge for Union Catholic.</p>
<p>Brown, a junior, win the 800 in a NJ# 5 1:57.92 and the 400 in 49.91, and ran on the third-place 4&#215;400. Plummer, a freshman, captured the 1,600 in 4:32.23 and was third in the 3,200 in 9:56.74. Also for UC, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=738" rel="smarttag" rev="738">Ryan McLaughlin</a></dfn> was third in the 55 hurdles in 7.73, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=733" rel="smarttag" rev="733">Uchenna Duru</a></dfn> took third in the shot put (47-9 3/4), <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=730" rel="smarttag" rev="730">Oluwafemi Asenuga</a></dfn> was third in the high jump (5-8), <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=728" rel="smarttag" rev="728">Elijah Abraham</a></dfn> placed sixth in the 55 hurdles and ran on the relay, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=734" rel="smarttag" rev="734">Matt Granizo</a></dfn> placed sixth in the 3,200 in 10:14.27.</p>
