<p>The Union Catholic girls track and field team made its presence felt in a huge way with several eye-popping results at Saturday&#8217;s Marine Corp Holiday Classic at the 168th Street Armory in New York.</p>
<p>In the most dramatic finish of the meet, the 4&#215;400-meter relay squad blasted a NJ#1 and US#2 time of 3:53.95 as the Vikings were edged by Bullis of Maryland, who ran a US #1 3:53.90.  <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=781" rel="smarttag" rev="781">Chi Chi Nkulume</a></dfn> led off for UC in 59.64, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=764" rel="smarttag" rev="764">Leena Morant</a></dfn> followed with a 57.31, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=769" rel="smarttag" rev="769">Sydney Tucker</a></dfn> then ran 59.49, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=712" rel="smarttag" rev="712">Khamil Evans</a></dfn> anchored in 57.49.</p>
<p>UC also ripped a hot time in the 4&#215;800 as combined to run a NJ#3 9:54.70 to place third. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=762" rel="smarttag" rev="762">Jerika Lufrano</a></dfn> led off with a 2:20.89 carry, Abby Granath ran 2:27.92, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=763" rel="smarttag" rev="763">Megan McCabe</a></dfn> went 2:37.54, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=761" rel="smarttag" rev="761">Zoe Lee</a></dfn> anchored in 2:28.34.</p>
<p>Lufrano&#8217;s lead off leg in the 4&#215;800 was part of big day for the senior. She also won the mile with a NJ#1 time of 5:04.09.</p>
<p>Senior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=717" rel="smarttag" rev="717">Hope Handlin</a></dfn> continued climbing to new heights in the pole vault as the senior smashed her own school record by placing sixth with a clearance of 11-0, which ties for her No. 2 in N.J. this season. Handlin broke her own school mark of 10-6 that she set earlier this month.</p>
<p>Rising star <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=786" rel="smarttag" rev="786">A&#8217;Liyah Thomas</a></dfn> had a great day for the Vikings. The sophomore placed ninth in the long jump with 17-0, matched her personal best by clearing 5-0 to place 10th in the high jump, and ran a huge personal best of 8.68 in the trials of the 55 hurdles.</p>
<p>In other highlights for UC, Morant placed sixth in the girls 800 in a NJ#2 2:19.17, Evans, the state leader in the 300 at 40,23, was ninth in the 300 in 40.83, Tucker placed eighth in the  200 in 26.17, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=768" rel="smarttag" rev="768">Felicia Quainoo</a></dfn> was eight in the 55 hurdles in 8.48.</p>
<p>Saturday&#8217;s meet put the finishing on a busy and great weekend for the Vikings at the Armory.</p>
<p>At Friday&#8217;s New Jersey Catholic Track Conference Relays, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=774" rel="smarttag" rev="774">Zamira Ysaac</a></dfn>, Quainoo, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=760" rel="smarttag" rev="760">Avery Jordan</a></dfn> and Thomas combined to run a NJ#2 31.64 in the shuttle hurdles to place second to Paramus Catholic&#8217;s 30.84.</p>
