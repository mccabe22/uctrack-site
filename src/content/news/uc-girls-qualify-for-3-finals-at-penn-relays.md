<h2 class="article-headline">UC girls qualified for 3 finals and ran fastest NJ times in 3 races at Penn Relays</h2>
<p class="article-subheadline">The Vikings advanced to the 4&#215;800 C of A, two 4&#215;100 finals and ran the top NJ times in 4&#215;100, 4&#215;400 and 4&#215;800</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>PHILADELPHIA &#8211; The Union Catholic girls have a history of making their presence felt on the biggest track and field stages.</p>
<p>The opening day of the 124th Penn Relays on Thursday at historic Franklin Field proved to be no different as UC once again showed the crowd of 22,862 that even without Olympian Sydney McLaughlin (now starring at the University of Kentucky) the Vikings are one of the best programs in the country.</p>
<p>Showcasing its great range and depth, the Vikings used 10 different athletes to qualify for the 4&#215;800 Championship of America race for the first time ever, two 4&#215;100 finals, and they broke one county record and ran the fastest times among all New Jersey squads in the 4&#215;100, 4&#215;400, and 4&#215;800.</p>
<p>UC&#8217;s remarkable triple started in the morning when the all underclassmen quartet of <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=404" rel="smarttag" rev="404">Jerika Lufrano</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=407" rel="smarttag" rev="407">Leena Morant</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=420" rel="smarttag" rev="420">Zamira Ysaac</a></dfn>, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=401" rel="smarttag" rev="401">Morgan Jackson</a></dfn><a href="http://ucvikings.com/news/2018/4/26/girls-outdoor-track-and-field-uc-girls-smash-union-county-record-advance-to-4x800-c-of-a-at-penn-relays.aspx"> ran a Union County record 9:10.04 </a>to place second in its heat and qualify for Friday&#8217;s Championships of America race. This is the third straight year that UC will compete in a girls C of A race. The Vikings, with McLaughlin on the anchor, placed third in the 4&#215;400 C of A in 2016 and &#8217;17.</p>
<p>A few hours later, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=389" rel="smarttag" rev="389">Kira Alexander</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=415" rel="smarttag" rev="415">Sydney Tucker</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=395" rel="smarttag" rev="395">Khamil Evans</a></dfn>, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=393" rel="smarttag" rev="393">Amaya Chadwick</a></dfn> teamed up to <a href="http://ucvikings.com/news/2018/4/26/girls-outdoor-track-and-field-uc-girls-qualify-for-two-finals-in-the-4x100-at-the-penn-relays.aspx">win its heat in a school record 47.42 </a>to qualify for Friday&#8217;s Northeast Championship and Saturday&#8217;s Tri-State Championship.</p>
<p>The Vikings capped off its memorable trifecta when the foursome of Evans (57.35), Tucker (58.11), Felicia Quianoo (59.15) and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=390" rel="smarttag" rev="390">Mia Bennett-Jones</a></dfn> (59.78) placed fifth in its heat in 3:54.38, the 16th fastest time among the 611 teams that competed and the ninth fastest among U.S. teams.</p>
<p>UC&#8217;s 9:10.04, 47.22, and 3:54.38 are all the fastest times run by a NJ this season.</p>
<p>&#8220;It was a real team effort,&#8221; said UC coach <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/staff.aspx?staff=2" rel="smarttag" rev="2">Mike McCabe</a></dfn>. &#8220;The girls are reaping the rewards of their hard work and dedication to the team.&#8221;</p>
