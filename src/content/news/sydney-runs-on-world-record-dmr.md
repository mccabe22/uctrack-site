<aside class="summary cf">Boston, Mass. &#8211; Union Catholic Olympian Sydney McLaughlin split 52.32 on the 400-meter leg on world record breaking distance medley relay at the New Balance Grand Prix meet</aside>
<section class="body cf">
<div>
<p><em>By Jim Lambert</em></p>
<p>Union Catholic Olympian <strong>Sydney McLaughlin</strong> added to her remarkable track and field resume on Saturday when she ran on an all-star distance medley relay team that smashed the world record at the New Balance Indoor Grand Prix meet at the Reggie Lewis Center in Boston.</p>
<p>McLaughlin, an Olympian in the 400-meter hurdles this past summer, split 52.32on the 400-meter leg of the DMR as she combined with three other Olympians to finish first in 10:40.31, which shattered the world indoor record of 10:42.57 that was set in 2015.</p>
<p>The 17-year-old McLaughlin, the youngest U.S. track and field Olympian in 44 years, teamed up with Emma Coburn, Brenda Martinez, Jenny Simpson on the star-studded DMR in Boston.</p>
<p>Coburn, who won a bronze medal in the steeplechase at the Olympic Games this past summer, led off  the DMR with a 1,200 leg of 3:18.40. Then the Kentucky-bound McLaughlin, the national high school indoor 400 record holder, ran 52.32 for her two laps before handing off to Martinez in the lead. Martinez split 2:01.92 for 800, and then Simpson, the bronze medalist in the 1,500 in Rio, finished off the record breaking performance with a 4:27.66 anchor carry.</p>
