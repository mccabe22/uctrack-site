<h2 class="article-headline">UC boys and girls sweep 4&#215;400 relays with meet record performances at Millrose Games</h2>
<div class="noprint story_email"><time class="article-date">2/9/2020 10:02:00 AM</time></div>
<p class="article-subheadline">Elijah Brown placed third in the boys 600 with the fastest time by a NJ runner this season</p>
</header>
<p>Union Catholic put on a sizzling sprint show when the Vikings burned up the track by taking down the meet records in both the boys and girls 4&#215;400-meter relays at Saturday&#8217;s 113th Millorse Games at the 168th Street Armory in New York.</p>
<p>The boys dropped a NJ#1/US#3 time of 3:20.70 to break the meet mark and win the Private School 4&#215;400. Senior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1295" rel="smarttag" rev="1295">Tyhir Jones</a></dfn> (50.27), junior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1292" rel="smarttag" rev="1292">Connor Cooper</a></dfn> (51.49), sophomore <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1299" rel="smarttag" rev="1299">Myles Plummer</a></dfn> (50.37) and senior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1291" rel="smarttag" rev="1291">Elijah Brown</a></dfn> (48.58) shared the baton for UC. CBA was second in 3:21.39.</p>
<p>The George Mason-bound Brown came back after his 4&#215;400 anchor to place third in the boys 600 in a:120.89, the fastest time by a New Jersey runner this season, Gabriel Moronta of Pleasantville, who came into the race leading the state with a 1:20.90, was fifth in 1:21.75.</p>
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="https://ucvikings.com/common/controls/image_handler.aspx?thumb_id=0&amp;image_path=/images/2020/2/9/Union_Catholic_1__71.png" alt="UC girls 4x800 Millrose Trials 2020" width="620" height="349" /><br />
In the girls Private School 4&#215;400, Union Catholic finished first in an NJ#1/US#6 and meet record 3:52.91 as freshman <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1345" rel="smarttag" rev="1345">Maameyaa Nyinah</a></dfn> (59.54), junior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1310" rel="smarttag" rev="1310">Avery Jordan</a></dfn> (58.56), freshman <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1333" rel="smarttag" rev="1333">Asydiah Pray-Brown</a></dfn> (58.77) and senior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1316" rel="smarttag" rev="1316">Chi Chi Nkulume</a></dfn> (56.05) combined on the victory. Paul VI was second in a NJ#2 3:53.81.</p>
<p>UC girls had a very busy day as the Vikings displayed their superior depth and balance.</p>
<p>In addition to its 4&#215;400 victory, UC also placed sixth in the girls 4&#215;800 in 9:20.86, the top time by a NJ team this season, and  finished sixth in the girls 4&#215;200 in an NJ#3 1:43.10.<br />
In the 4&#215;800, senior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1317" rel="smarttag" rev="1317">Ashleigh Patterson</a></dfn> led off in 2:21.96, senior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1325" rel="smarttag" rev="1325">Zamira Ysaac</a></dfn> ran 2:21.76, senior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1309" rel="smarttag" rev="1309">Morgan Jackson</a></dfn> split 2:19.77,and junior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1324" rel="smarttag" rev="1324">Morgan Williams</a></dfn> anchored in 2:17.39.</p>
