<h2 class="article-headline">UC boys win three events, finish a very strong second at the State Non-Public A Relays</h2>
<p><strong><a href="https://nj.milesplit.com/meets/337347/coverage#.XEYsmM9KhQN" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Results | Race Videos | Photos</a></strong></p>
<p class="article-subheadline">The Vikings won the 4&#215;400, shuttle hurdles and the pole vault</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic boys put together several impressive performances and won three events when the Vikings placed a very strong second at Thursday&#8217;s Non-Public A Relay Championships at the Bennett Center in Toms River.</p>
<p>UC, which scored in eight of the nine events, won the shuttle hurdles in 31.13, captured the 4&#215;400 in dramatic fashion in 3:27.04, and finished first in the team pole vault as the Vikings piled up 56 points. Christian Brothers Academy finished first with 76.</p>
<p>There were lots of Vikings who sparkled on the track and in the field.</p>
<p>Junior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=741" rel="smarttag" rev="741">Conor Reilly</a></dfn> matched his personal best and school record in the vault when he cleared a meet best 15-0, and had splits of 52.7 in the 4&#215;400 and 24.1 leading off the third-place 4&#215;200</p>
<p>Junior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=732" rel="smarttag" rev="732">Elijah Brown</a></dfn> anchored the third-place 4&#215;800 with a 1:58.7 carry, and went 50.2 in the 4&#215;400.</p>
<p>Freshman <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=748" rel="smarttag" rev="748">Myles Plummer</a></dfn> went 51.8 when he came from behind on the anchor leg to catch CBA right at the line to win the 4&#215;400, and split 1:59.6 in the 4&#215;800.</p>
<p><dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=729" rel="smarttag" rev="729">Obadare Animashaun</a></dfn> led off the winning shuttle hurdles for UC with a 7.8 split, cleared 12-0 in the vault, and ran a 200 on the fifth-place SMR.</p>
<p><dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=728" rel="smarttag" rev="728">Elijah Abraham</a></dfn> anchored the shuttles and 4&#215;200, and ran the 400 leg on the SMR.</p>
<p><dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=736" rel="smarttag" rev="736">Brandon Leslie</a></dfn> ran 7.8 in the shuttles, and 23.9 in the SMR. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=747" rel="smarttag" rev="747">Connor Cooper</a></dfn> blasted a 22.9 in the 4&#215;200 and ran on the 4&#215;400. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=739" rel="smarttag" rev="739">Chinedu Okoro</a></dfn> ran 23.6 in the 4&#215;200 and made 5-6 for the fourth-place high jump team. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=738" rel="smarttag" rev="738">Ryan McLaughlin</a></dfn> ripped a 7.3 in the shuttles, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=733" rel="smarttag" rev="733">Uchenna Duru</a></dfn> (50-7 ¼) and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=743" rel="smarttag" rev="743">Jared Ware</a></dfn> (44-7 ¼) teamed up to place second in the shot put. Duru&#8217;s 50-7 ¼ is No. 16 in the state this season.</p>
