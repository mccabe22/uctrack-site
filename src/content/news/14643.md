<p>The Union Catholic boys showed its depth and balance on the way to a very impressive victory at the NJSIAA Non-Public B Relay Championships on Wednesday at the Bennett Center in Toms River.</p>
<p>Union Catholic, which dropped down from Non-Public A this season, won four events and placed fourth or better in all nine events on the way to a 76-57 victory over defending champion St. Rose. It&#8217;s the third state relay championship for the Vikings, which won the Non-Public B title in 2013, and the Non-Public A championship in 2014.</p>
<p>Union Catholic won the shuttle hurdles in 32.41, the 4&#215;200 in 1:34.04, the sprint medley in 3:45.14, and the pole vault. The Vikings also placed second in the distance medley, second in the 4&#215;800, second in the 4&#215;400, second in the high jump, and fourth in the shot put.</p>
<p>For UC, <a title="View athlete profile for Noah Cooper" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/11253106-noah-cooper">Noah Cooper </a>anchored the shuttles in 7.8, and led off the 4&#215;200 with a 23.5.<a title="View athlete profile for Marcus McCoy" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/10214167-marcus-mccoy"> Marcus McCoy</a> went 8.0 in the shuttles, and 23.5 in the 4&#215;200, and<a title="View athlete profile for Al-Qamar Rojas" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/10214170-al-qamar-rojas"> Al-Qamar Rojas</a> anchored the 4&#215;200 with a 23.1, and led off the shuttles with a 7.8. <a title="View athlete profile for Sean Wilson" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/10155489-sean-wilson">Sean Wilson</a> anchored the winning SMR for UC with a 2:02. In the pole vault, <a title="View athlete profile for Matthew Schutz" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/9434159-matthew-schutz">Matthew Schutz</a> cleared a personal best matching 11-6, and <a title="View athlete profile for Aidan Reidy" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/9434167-aidan-reidy">Aidan Reidy </a>made 11-0. Those were the two best vaulting performances of the meet.</p>
<p>In other highlights, Emeron McKindra ran 53.8 in the 400 and cleared 5-10 in the high jump, and Gavin Devine ran 2:05.9 in the 4&#215;800, and 56.4 in the 4&#215;400.,</p>
<p>The UC girls will be shooting for their eighth straight Non-Public A Relay Championship on Friday at the Bennett Center.</p>
