<p><em><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Written by:</span> Jim Lambert for MileSplit</em></p>
<p>Union Catholic showed that it just might be the best team in the state with a historic display of balance and depth as the Vikings piled up a mind-blowing meet record total of 138 points! That&#8217;s just 22 points away from a perfect score! UC did it by placing third or better in all 16 events! And the Vikings won seven events and dropped two NJ#1 times on the way to a 138-76 victory over runner-up Scotch Plains-Fanwood.</p>
<p>The former meet record for points was 129 by the Union Catholic girls in 2015.</p>
<p>UC, which won the pole vault, SMR and 400 hurdles on Thursday, added victories in the first three events on the track on Friday-the shuttle hurdles, the 4&#215;800, and the 4&#215;200 to put the meet away. UC&#8217;s seventh wins came in the field from its strong javelin crew.</p>

<p>There were several key contributors for the Vikings.</p>
<p><a title="View athlete profile for Avery Jordan" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/7348296">Avery Jordan</a> ran on three winning relays, she led off the NJ#1 shuttles (1:01:11) with a 15.0, ran the third leg on the NJ#1 4&#215;200 (1:41.99), and was a member of the wining 400 hurdle squad. <a title="View athlete profile for Ajanae Thompson" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/9661128">Ajanae Thompson</a> ran on the shuttles and anchored the 4&#215;200. <a title="View athlete profile for Kaleigh Gunsiorowski" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/10428253">Kaleigh Gunsiorowski </a>anchored the SMR and 4&#215;800 to victories with strong carries. In the field, <a title="View athlete profile for Jada Mia Puryear" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/8233816">Jada Mia Puryear</a> had the top throws of the meet in both the shot put (NJ# 2 40-8.50) and discus (118-8) and<a title="View athlete profile for Daniela Peralta" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/8233812"> Daniela Peralta</a> had the top throw of the meet in the javelin (107-5), and cleared 8-0 on the winning pole vault team. Versatile stars <a title="View athlete profile for Alexandra Bonn" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/9695524">Alexandra Bonn</a>  and<a title="View athlete profile for A'Liyah Thomas" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/7923268"> A&#8217;Liyah Thomas</a> also had very strong performances. Bonn ran on the shuttles, and had the top TJ performance (35-10) of the meet, and was on the runner-up high jump and long jumps teams. Thomas anchored the shuttles, and was on the LJ, TJ and HJ teams that all placed second.</p>
<hr />
<p>The UC boys dropped the first big bomb of the meet by hammering out a 10:23.92 in the DMR, that&#8217;s No. 2 all-time in meet history, No. 1 in NJ this season, and No. 11 in the U.S. Junior <a title="View athlete profile for Myles Plummer" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/8233818">Myles Plummer</a> led off with a 3:04.9 carry to open a big gap, and junior<a title="View athlete profile for Shane Brosnan" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/8322837"> Shane Brosnan</a> closed it out with a 4:18.1 anchor. They will be back to run the 4&#215;1,600 on Friday.</p>
