<p>By Jim Lambert</p>
<p>Many of New Jersey&#8217;s top stars put on sparkling shows at Friday&#8217;s Marine Corps Holiday Classic at the New York Armory.</p>
<p>Taylor Cox, a junior at Union Catholic, led the charge for the Garden State by taking over the national lead with her winning time of 7.82 in the 55-meter hurdles. Cox edged sophomore Kassidy Hopkins of Bullis (Md.), who ran a US No. 3 time of 7.91.</p>
<p>Cox&#8217;s 7.82 broke her PR of 7.88 that she ran to win at the NJ Meet of Champions last year, and is No. 5 all-time in NJ history. The four hurdlers she&#8217;s chasing are Sydney McLaughlin of UC (7.60 in 2015), Charmaine Walker of Plainfield (7.71 in 1997), Grace O&#8217;Shea of Ramapo (7.75 in 2019), and Dawn Bowles of Neptune (7.81 in 1988).</p>
<p>Union Catholic freshman Rhia Randolph and the Union Catholic girls 4&#215;200 relay also ran NJ No. 1 times.</p>
<p>Randolph ran the fastest time in the state so far this season in the 300 when she placed fourth in the 300 in a US No. 8 39.51, and UC&#8217;s foursome of Jola Strayhorn, Natalia Thompson, Abriyah Thompson, and Kiara Fleming ran a NJ No. 1/US No. 9 1:42.71 in the trials of the 4&#215;200. UC finished third in the final in 1:44.30. Willingboro placed second in 1:43.87.     </p>
