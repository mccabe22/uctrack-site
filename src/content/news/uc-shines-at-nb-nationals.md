<p><strong>2016 New Balance Outdoor Nationals</strong></p>
<p>Union Catholic completed the New Balance National Championship Meet with 5 National Champions, 9 All-Americans and 2 NATIONAL RECORDS!</p>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">400 Hurdles:</span> Sydney McLaughlin 54.46 &#8211; National Record, World Youth Record, US Junior Record, World Junior #2 All-Time</p>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Swedish Relay</span> (Tatiana Ifill, Amaya Chadwick, Amari Onque-Shabazz, Sydney McLaughlin) &#8211; 2:07.99 &#8211; National Record<br />
-Sydney&#8217;s 400 split of 50.93 is the fastest in National History!</p>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">800m Sprint Medley</span> (Tatiana Ifill, Tyler Scott, Amaya Chadwick, Amari Onque-Shabazz) &#8211; 3rd Place</p>
