<p><strong>Union County Championship<br />
</strong><em>Sunday, January 20th, 2013</em><br />
<em>Jersey City Armory </em></p>
<p>-Congratulations to the boys team for winning their 3rd Consecutive Union County Championship with a score of 85 points, runner up had 28 points!<br />
-Girls team places 3rd, just missing 2nd place!<br />
-3 Meet Records were broken by UC athletes. Jordan Jimerson: 55m Dash (6.52), 400m Dash (49.77) and Zach Ghizzone: 55 High Hurdles (7.58)<br />
-4 School Records: Jimerson 55m Dash, Zach Ghizzone: 55 High Hurdles,  Imani Beauliere: 55m Dash (7.30), Jenny Herron: 1600m (5:21.40)</p>
