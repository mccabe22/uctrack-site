<p><strong>June 25th &#8211; June 26th, 2013</strong><br />
<strong>Southern Illinois University-Edwardsville</strong></p>
<p>7 Track &amp; Field athletes from UC made the trip to Edwardsville, Illinois to compete in the World Youth Trials. This meet was the qualifier to make Team USA and to compete at the World Youth Track &amp; Field Championships in Donetsk, Ukraine.  <em><a href="http://uctrack.smugmug.com/Sports/Spring-2013/World-Youth-Trials/30218808_sMNBXn#!i=2600525664&amp;k=T4zkRr3" target="_blank">Photos</a>  |  <a href="http://www.usatf.tv/gprofile.php?mgroup_id=45365&amp;mgroup_event_id=6565&amp;do=videos&amp;year=2013" target="_blank">Meet Videos on USATF.tv</a></em></p>
<p><strong>Jordan Jimerson and Taylor McLaughlin qualified for Team USA!</strong> Jordan and Taylor have the honor to represent the United States in the Ukraine from July 10th &#8211; July 14th, 2013! Congratulations and Good Luck in the Ukraine! Union Catholic, New Jersey and the rest of the country are behind you!</p>
