<p><strong>Directions for Submitting Union Catholic Athletic Paperwork</strong></p>
<p><em><strong>UNION CATHOLIC HAS A NEW WAY TO REGISTER FOR SPORTS!!!</strong></em></p>
<p>To access the team registration page, please go to <a href="https://ucrhs-ar.rschooltoday.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">https://ucrhs-ar.rschooltoday.com/</a></p>
<p>Once on that page, read the information provided for any updates/instructions.</p>
<p>Click the “Athletic Team Registration” icon to begin.</p>
<p>Create a family account (this will be saved so that you can add other children to the same family and can re-register students for other sports/seasons without having to re-enter all of your family information).</p>
<p>Through the Activity Registration, register a new student by entering information about your student and select the sport(s) that he/she wishes to play.</p>
<p>In Step 4, download the physical paperwork to be filled out by the doctor and handed in to the school in person along with the participation fee.</p>
<p>Complete information for all 6 Steps and submit. Paperwork will then be completed. Students will be cleared once participation fees and physicals have also been handed in, and will be noted on each student’s account.</p>
