<h3 class="article-headline">UC girls T&amp;F team wins 4&#215;400 and 4&#215;800 at Millrose Games</h3>
<h5 class="article-headline"><a href="http://results.nyrrmillrosegames.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Official Results</a></h5>
<p class="article-subheadline">The 4&#215;800 ran the fastest time in the nation this season and shattered the school record</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic girls, despite not having its regular lineup in either race, still put on a remarkable show by sweeping the girls 4&#215;400 and 4&#215;800-meter relays in dramatic fashion at Saturday&#8217;s prestigious 112th Millrose Games at the Armory in New York.</p>
<p>In the 4&#215;800, UC threw down a red hot 9:07.44, the fastest time in the nation this season, the eighth fastest time in NJ indoor history, and a new school record. The Vikings finished just ahead of Mt. De Sales of Maryland, second in a US#2 9:07.87. UC anchor <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=764" rel="smarttag" rev="764">Leena Morant</a></dfn> went to the lead at the bell on her way to a 2:11.28 anchor split and she held off Juliette Whittaker of Mt. De Sales, who split 2:08.96. Union Catholic&#8217;s first three legs on the 4&#215;800 were handled by <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=771" rel="smarttag" rev="771">Morgan Williams</a></dfn> (2:18.06), <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=762" rel="smarttag" rev="762">Jerika Lufrano</a></dfn>(2:16.47), and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=766" rel="smarttag" rev="766">Ashleigh Patterson</a></dfn> (2:21.63).</p>
<p>The 9:07.44 by UC demolished the school indoor record of 9:13.42 set in 2015 and is nearly three seconds faster than the school outdoor record of 9:10.40 set last April at the Penn Relays</p>
<p>UC, also the state leader in the 4&#215;400 at 3:50.93, came back later to run 3:53.40 and rally to finish first in the Suburban Division 4&#215;400, edging Rancocas Valley, second in a NJ#2 3:53.97. Millville was third in a NJ# 3:55.04, and Ramapo sixth in a NJ#4 3:58.20.</p>
<p>UC&#8217;s 4&#215;400 lineup consisted of <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=760" rel="smarttag" rev="760">Avery Jordan</a></dfn> (59.83), <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=769" rel="smarttag" rev="769">Sydney Tucker</a></dfn> (57.88), <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=774" rel="smarttag" rev="774">Zamira Ysaac</a></dfn> (58.90) and Chidumga Nkulume (56.81). Nkulume got the baton in the third place, but didn&#8217;t stay threre for long as she overtook both Rancocas Valley and Millville to secure the win for the Vikings.</p>
