<h2 class="article-headline">Morant and Granrath ignite UC XC girls team to 5th straight title at Conference meet</h2>
<div class="noprint story_email"> <time class="article-date">10/18/2019 8:55:00 AM</time></div>
<p class="article-subheadline">The boys placed a very strong third at the Conference Championships</p>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic girls, led by <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=549" rel="smarttag" rev="549">Leena Morant&#8217;s</a></dfn> stunning course record breaking victory, threw down a sensational performance to capture its fifth straight title at the Union County Conference Cross-County Championships on Thursday at Oak Ridge Park in Clark.</p>
<p>The Vikings packed its first five runners in the top 13 and outscored runner-up Summit, 34-46, to win the Watchung Division title for the second straight year. UC won the Mountain Division championship from 2015 through 2017.</p>
<p>Morant, the only senior in UC&#8217;s top five, won the race in 18:40 to smash the course record of 19:02 set last year by Victoria Vanriele of Gov. Livingston.</p>
<p>Sophomore <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=553" rel="smarttag" rev="553">Abby Granrath</a></dfn> also had a huge performance, running well under the former course record as she led the underclassmen-powered pack for UC with a runner-up finish in 18:48. UC freshman <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1168" rel="smarttag" rev="1168">Kathryn Power</a></dfn> was sixth in 19:24, freshman <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1172" rel="smarttag" rev="1172">Maameyaa Nyinah</a></dfn> placed 12th in 19:53, and freshman Elsa Spoor finished 13th in 19:53. UC averaged 19:20 and had a 1-5 compression of 1:13.</p>
<p>In the boys competition, the Vikings finished a very strong third with 74 points in the always tough Watchung Division. Westfield, the No. 7 ranked team in the state, finished first with 36 points and Summit was second with 37.</p>
<p>The five scorers for UC were junior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=561" rel="smarttag" rev="561">Matt Granizo</a></dfn>, third in 16:42, senior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=540" rel="smarttag" rev="540">Zachary Turner</a></dfn>, 14th in 16:57, junior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1165" rel="smarttag" rev="1165">Sam Hernandez</a></dfn>, 20th in 17:19, sophomore <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1166" rel="smarttag" rev="1166">Noah Handlin</a></dfn>, 22nd in 17:23, and sophomore <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1167" rel="smarttag" rev="1167">Noah Alston</a></dfn>, 24th in 17:36.</p>
<p>Next up for the Vikings will be the Union County Championships on Oct. 29 at Warinanco Park in Elizabeth.</p>
