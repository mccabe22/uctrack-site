<h2 class="article-headline">UC girls break state record and three school records at New Balance Nationals</h2>
<div class="noprint story_email"><a href="https://www.addthis.com/bookmark.php?v=250&amp;pub=xa-4a7320e7645958d0"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="https://s7.addthis.com/static/btn/lg-share-en.gif" alt="Bookmark and Share" width="125" height="16" /></a> <time class="article-date">6/19/2018 7:18:00 AM</time></div>
<p class="article-subheadline">The shuttle hurdle team broke the state record, and the DMR and 4&#215;100 relays broke school records</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p dir="ltr">The Union Catholic girls certainly made its presence felt at the New Balance National Championships that concluded on Sunday at Aggie Stadium on the campus of North Carolina A&amp;T in Greensboro, N.C.</p>
<p dir="ltr">The biggest highlight for the Vikings came when the<a href="https://ucvikings.com/news/2018/6/17/girls-outdoor-track-and-field-uc-girls-break-state-record-in-shuttle-hurdles-at-national-championships.aspx">y placed fourth in the shuttle hurdles in a school and state record</a> 58.30, finished ninth in the distance medley relay in a school record and a NJ #2 12:09.53, and finished ninth in a school record and NJ #4 47.38.</p>
<p dir="ltr">In the DMR, junior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=391" rel="smarttag" rev="391">Alexandra Borik</a></dfn> led off in 3:46.91. Freshman <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=402" rel="smarttag" rev="402">Avery Jordan</a></dfn> followed with a 1:00.98 400 carry. Sophomore <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=407" rel="smarttag" rev="407">Leena Morant</a></dfn> then ran 2:18.94 in the 800, and junior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=404" rel="smarttag" rev="404">Jerika Lufrano</a></dfn> anchored in 5:02.72. The 12:09.53 shattered the school record of 12:17.53 that the Vikings ran at April&#8217;s Blue Devil Relays in Westfield</p>
<p dir="ltr">In the 4&#215;100, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=389" rel="smarttag" rev="389">Kira Alexander</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=415" rel="smarttag" rev="415">Sydney Tucker</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=395" rel="smarttag" rev="395">Khamil Evans</a></dfn>, and Felicia Quianoo combined to run 47.38, breaking the school record of 47.42 that the Vikings ran at the Penn Relays in April.</p>
<p dir="ltr">UC also placed sixth in the 800 sprint medley in 1:46.13, and ninth in the 1,600 sprint medley relay in a NJ #2 4:03.66.</p>
