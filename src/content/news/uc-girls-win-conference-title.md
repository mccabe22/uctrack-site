<p>The UC Girls Won the Watchung Division Title by 3 points over Westfield. Top finishers&#8230;Leena Morant, Abby Granrath, Zoe Lee, Megan McCabe, Zamira Ysaac, Ashleigh Patterson, Jenna Keith.</p>
<p>Daniela Peralta finished 2nd in the JV Race in a PR of 20:36.</p>
<p>Frosh Myles Plummer finished 3rd in the varsity boys race with a frosh School Record time of 16:27.</p>
