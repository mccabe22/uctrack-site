<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Results:</span> <a href="/Reports/Meet_Results/Boys-2013_05_02-Westfield%20Track-Union%20County%20Relays_ID460.pdf">Boys</a> | <a href="/Reports/Meet_Results/Girls-2013_05_02-Westfield%20Track-Union%20County%20Relays_ID390.pdf">Girls</a>        <span style="text-decoration: underline;">Performers of the Meet:</span> Tom Wang, Sean Brennan, Joy Clarke and Diana Arcure</p>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Highlights</span><br />
-Boys&#8217; Team Captures 3rd Consecutive Championship with a Record Total of 133 Points<br />
-Boys win 10 of 16 events and score in every event<br />
-Boys Break 6 School Records (4&#215;100, 4&#215;200, 4&#215;1600, High Jump Relay, Discus Relay, Javelin Relay)<br />
-Girls Break 2 School Records (4&#215;100 and Triple Jump Relay)<br />
-Many great personal bests on the girls and boys sides!</p>
