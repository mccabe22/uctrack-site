<p>Union Catholic placed third in the boys’ race at the NJSIAA Meet of Champions on November 14 at Holmdel Park. The Vikings finished with 105 points, nine behind Christian Brothers Academy and West Windsor-Plainsboro North. CBA won the title on the sixth-runner tiebreaker. The Vikings averaged 16:23.64 across their scoring five.</p><p>Nathan Simonich led Union Catholic in 13th place with a time of 15:59.61. Danny Kuran (31st), Finn Riely (38th), Jayden Aine (43rd), and Nick Frank (48th) rounded out the scoring five. Manny Sanchez and Jeremiah Shepherd completed the varsity lineup.</p>
