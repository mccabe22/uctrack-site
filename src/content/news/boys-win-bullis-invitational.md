<h2 class="article-headline">White and Patterson win to help propel UC boys T&amp;F team to title at Bullis Invit.</h2>
<p class="article-subheadline">Brown, Abraham, Saggio, and Duro also excel for the Vikings</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic boys track and field made a big statement by winning the team title at the prestigious Bullis Bulldog Invitational on Saturday in Potomac, Maryland.</p>
<p><dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=380" rel="smarttag" rev="380">Brandon Patterson</a></dfn> won the shot put with a throw of 55-0 1/4, and was fifth in the discus (136-9) as the Vikings outscored Dover of Maryland, 64-43. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=388" rel="smarttag" rev="388">Krishna White</a></dfn> also had a big day in the jumps for UC. He won the triple jump with a PR and NJ #2 3 46-7 3/4, was fourth in the high jump (6-2), and went 21-0 in the high jump.</p>
<p>Also for UC, sophomore <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=362" rel="smarttag" rev="362">Elijah Brown</a></dfn> was third in the 400 with a NJ #2 48.67, the 4&#215;800 team was second in 8:13.96, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=365" rel="smarttag" rev="365">Uchenna Duru</a></dfn> was third in the shot put with a a PR of 50-11 1/4, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=384" rel="smarttag" rev="384">David Saggio</a></dfn> placed fourth in the discus with a PR of 139-0, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=355" rel="smarttag" rev="355">Elijah Abraham</a></dfn> placed fourth in the 400 hurdles in 58.21. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=375" rel="smarttag" rev="375">Ryan McLaughlin</a></dfn> was 6th in the 110 hurdles in a NJ #6 14.92.</p>
<p>In the 4&#215;800, UC&#8217;s splits were 2:04.8 for <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=360" rel="smarttag" rev="360">Kyle Bartley</a></dfn>, 2:01.0 for <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=363" rel="smarttag" rev="363">Justin Cadas</a></dfn>, 2:02.5 for <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=382" rel="smarttag" rev="382">Andre Rodriguez</a></dfn>, and 2:04.5 for <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=367" rel="smarttag" rev="367">Trevor Garrity</a></dfn>.</p>
