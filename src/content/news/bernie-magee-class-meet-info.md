<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>DIRECTIONS</strong></span></p>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Warinanco Park</span><br />
FROM THE GARDEN STATE PARKWAY: Take exit 137. At the end of the exit ramp turn left onto Westfield Ave. Continue to park according to directions below from Westfield Ave.</p>
<p>FROM WESTFIELD AVENUE: Take Westfield Ave. (Route 28) to Linden Rd. Turn left if traveling west; turn right if traveling east. Linden Rd. leads into Warinanco Park.</p>
<p>FROM MORRIS AVENUE (Rt. 82): Follow Morris Ave. to North Ave. Turn right if traveling east; turn left if traveling west. North Ave. becomes Elmora Ave. Continue to Linden Ave. and turn right. Linden Ave. leads into Warinanco Park.</p>
<p>FROM ROUTE 1: Take Route 1 (north or south) to Park Ave. in Linden. Turn right if traveling south; turn left if traveling north. Go through 3 traffic lights and straight into Warinanco Park.</p>
<p>FROM ST. GEORGES AVENUE (Rahway Ave. in Elizabeth): Follow St. Georges Ave. (north or south) to Park Ave. Warinanco Park is located at the intersection of these streets. Turn into the park.</p>
