<p class="article-byline">The Union Catholic track and field teams got the season off to a great start with several great performances at the fourth annual Essex County Coaches Invitational on Friday night at Ocean Breeze on Staten Island.</p>
</header>
<p dir="ltr">Leading the charge for the Vikings were Taylor Cox, Alexandra Bonn, and Noah Cooper, who all broke meet records, and Ajanae Thompson, who also picked up an impressive victory.</p>
<p>Full results are <a href="https://nj.milesplit.com/meets/507669-essex-county-track-coaches-assn-season-opener-invite-2023/results#.Y54_uHbMJPY">here</a></p>
<p>Cox, a sophomore, ran a meet record 8.14 to win the girls 55 hurdles, and was second in the 400.The Yale-bound Bonn, who won the triple jump at the indoor and outdoor Meet of Champions last year, soared 17-8 to break the meet record in the girls long jump.</p>
<p>Cooper, a sophomore, broke the meet record in the long jump with a mark of 22-0.50, and Thompson, who is headed to Villanova, won the girls 55 dash in 7.25, just off her personal best of 7.20.</p>
