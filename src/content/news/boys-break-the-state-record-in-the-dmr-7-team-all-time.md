<h2 class="article-headline">Union Catholic boys take down state record in distance medley relay</h2>
<div class="noprint story_email"><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Written by:</span> Jim Lambert</div>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic boys distance medley relay squad was looking to drop a fast time when they took the line at the Varsity Classic on Monday at the New Balance Track and Field Center in New York.</p>
<p>They got much more than that.</p>
<p>How about the fastest time in state indoor history!!!</p>
<p>That&#8217;s right, the foursome of <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1959" rel="smarttag" rev="1959">Myles Plummer</a></dfn>, <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1954" rel="smarttag" rev="1954">Nick Givan</a></dfn>, Caudell Cajuste, and <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1951" rel="smarttag" rev="1951">Shane Brosnan</a></dfn> combined to finish first in 10:01.75! That breaks the NJ state record of 10:02.99 set by Christian Brothers Academy when the Colts placed second at the 2011 New Balance Nationals.</p>
<p>UC&#8217;s 10:01.75 also places them No. 7 in US high school indoor history, and is the fastest time in the nation this season.</p>
<p>Plummer, a senior, led off for UC with a 3:03.97 1,200 leg. Givan, a senior, followed with a 50.05 400 carry as he opened a five-second lead on Ridge. Cajuste, a junior, maintained the lead with a 2:00.15 800 leg, and the Harvard-bound Brosnan put an exclamation point on the electrifying performance with a 4:07.57 anchor!!!</p>
<p>In a race dominated by NJ teams, Ridge placed second in 10:14.82, No. 2 in the nation this season, Westfield was third in a US No. 4 10:24.86, West Windsor-Plainsboro North was fourth in a US No. 5 10:25.19, and Christian Brothers Academy was sixth in 10:31.68.</p>
