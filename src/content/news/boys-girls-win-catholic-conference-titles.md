<h2 class="article-headline">Brown Blasts NJ#4 All-Time 600 to help lead UC boys to B Division title at NJCTC Championships</h2>
<p class="article-subheadline">Brown ran 1:20.04 to win the 600, the second fastest time in the nation this season</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p><dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=732" rel="smarttag" rev="732">Elijah Brown</a></dfn> ran one of the fastest 600 meter times in state history to help lead the Union Catholic boys to the B Division team title at Sunday&#8217;s New Jersey Catholic Track Conference Championships at Ocean Breeze on Staten Island.</p>
<p>Brown, a junior, won the 600 in 1:20.04, which is the second fastest time in the nation this season and #4 all-time in New Jersey history.</p>
<p>The only three NJ runners who have gone faster than Brown in high school are Shaquan Brown of Paterson Kennedy (1:17.9 in 2005-the national record for seven years), Clayton Parros of Seton Hall Prep (1:18.90 in 2009), and Luis Peralta of Passaic, who leads the nation this season at 1:19.14.</p>
<p>UC, which finished second to Christian Brothers Academy, 69-64, in the overall team standings (A and B Divisions combined) received several strong performances.</p>
<p><dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=741" rel="smarttag" rev="741">Conor Reilly</a></dfn> won the pole vault by clearing 14-0 and was second in the long jump with a 21-2 1/2. Freshman <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=748" rel="smarttag" rev="748">Myles Plummer</a></dfn> finished second in the 1,000 in 2:32.45. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=733" rel="smarttag" rev="733">Uchenna Duru</a></dfn> was third in the shot put at 50-10 1/2, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=738" rel="smarttag" rev="738">Ryan McLaughlin</a></dfn> took fourth in the hurdles in 7.89, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=731" rel="smarttag" rev="731">Matt Axt</a></dfn> placed third in the triple jump at 40-3, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=736" rel="smarttag" rev="736">Brandon Leslie</a></dfn> in the triple jump (39-7), <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=750" rel="smarttag" rev="750">Justin Lopez</a></dfn> finished third in the pole vault (11-6)., and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=729" rel="smarttag" rev="729">Obadare Animashaun</a></dfn> was fourth in the vault at 11-0.</p>
