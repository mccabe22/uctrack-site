<p>Union Catholic tied the record for consecutive girls’ team titles at the NJSIAA Meet of Champions with its fifth straight win on November 14 at Holmdel Park. The Vikings scored a girls’ meet-record 24 points and set a course record with an 18:00.67 average for their top five.</p><p>Cayleigh Kaiser finished second in 17:42.46 and Paige Sheppard took third in 17:51.70. Harper Telesca placed fifth, Sophia Thompson eighth, and Kayla Devine 11th. Abigail Robisky and Eden Solorzano rounded out the varsity lineup.</p>
