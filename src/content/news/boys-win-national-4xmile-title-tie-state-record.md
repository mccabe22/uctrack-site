<h2 class="article-headline">UC boys win 4xmile at NB National Championships, Tie State Record</h2>
<div class="noprint story_email"><time class="article-date">Written by: Jim Lambert<br />
6/17/2022</time></div>
<p class="article-byline">The Union Catholic boys got the New Balance National Championships off to an electrifying start by winning the boys 4xmile and breaking the New Jersey state record on Thursday night at historic Franklin Field in Philadelphia.</p>
</header>
<p>The foursome of junior <a title="View athlete profile for Caudell Cajuste" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/9434163-caudell-cajuste" aria-label="View athlete profile for Caudell Cajuste">Caudell Cajuste</a>, senior<a title="View athlete profile for Myles Plummer" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/8233818-myles-plummer" aria-label="View athlete profile for Myles Plummer"> Myles Plummer</a>, senior<a title="View athlete profile for Shane Brosnan" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/8322837-shane-brosnan" aria-label="View athlete profile for Shane Brosnan"> Shane Brosnan</a>, and sophomore <a title="View athlete profile for Jimmy Wischusen" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/10214165-jimmy-wischusen" aria-label="View athlete profile for Jimmy Wischusen">Jimmy Wischusen</a> combined to run 17:12.34, which the state record set by Essex Catholic in 1966 at Highland Park, and is tied for No. 11 in U.S. high school history. Essex Catholic, anchored by the legendary <a title="View athlete profile for Marty Liquori" href="https://pa.milesplit.com/athletes/3590916-marty-liquori" aria-label="View athlete profile for Marty Liquori">Marty Liquori</a>, ran a hand time of 17.12.2, but when converted from a hand to an automatic time (adding .24 as per the National Federation of High School) it comes to 17:12.34.</p>
<p>Union Catholic won the race by 21 seconds over Christian Brothers Academy, second in 17:33.68. Westfield finished sixth in 17:43.7.</p>
<p>Cajuste got UC off to a solid start, splitting 4:27.6 before handing off to Plummer in fifth place. Plummer got right up to the lead pack after the first 800, and then charged to the front and completed a 4:14.9 carry to put UC in the lead for good. Brosnan immediately opened a huge gap on the third leg and kept pouring it on as he built a huge lead with a 4:15.5 split. Wischusen, in a race against the clock when he got the stick, finished off the masterpiece by cranking out a 4:14.2 anchor, which included a 62.5 last lap.</p>
