<p>Jordan Jimerson represented the United States (and Union Catholic) very proudly at the IAAF World Youth Championships in Donetsk, Ukraine. Jordan ran a time of 48.28 in the 1st round to qualify for the semi-finals. The following day Jordan ran a time of 47.97 to finish 17th overall. Congratulations on a great performance! Next up, Taylor McLaughlin in the Medley Relay!</p>
