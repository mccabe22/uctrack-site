<p>The Union Catholic cross-country teams celebrated Senior Day in memorable fashion as the boys and girls swept the Watchung Division titles at Thursday&#8217;s Union County Conference Championships at Oak Ridge Park in Clark.</p>
<p>The boys, who wanted to throw down a performance that solidified its status as the No. 1 ranked team in the state, did exactly that by putting together the fastest race in course history and the fastest race ever by a Union County team when they averaged 15:49 and defeated No. 7 ranked Westfield, and No. 10 Summit.</p>
<p>UC, which broke the CR average of 16:02 that Ridge ran at the NJ. Section 2 Group 4 race last November, finished 1-2-3-5-9 on the way to a 20-41 victory over a very strong Westfield team. Summit was third with 59.</p>
<p>Senior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1845" rel="smarttag" rev="1845">Shane Brosnan</a></dfn> led the record-breaking run for Union Catholic, crossing the finish line first in 15:16, No. 2 in course history. Only Marco Langon of Bridgewater-Raritan (15:10 last month) has run faster at Oak Ridge than Brosnan. UC sophomore <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1852" rel="smarttag" rev="1852">Jimmy Wischusen</a></dfn> was second in 15:26, senior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1851" rel="smarttag" rev="1851">Myles Plummer</a></dfn> placed third in 15:42, junior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1846" rel="smarttag" rev="1846">Caudell Cajuste</a></dfn> was fifth in 16:07, and junior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1847" rel="smarttag" rev="1847">Ryan Cichocki</a></dfn> was ninth in 16:35.</p>
<p>The times by Brosnan and Wischuen&#8217;s are the two fastest times ever recorded by Union County runners on any course, and Plummer&#8217;s time was a personal best, Cajuste was just one second off his PR, and Cichocki&#8217;s time was a new PR.</p>
