<h2 class="article-headline">UC&#8217;s Elijah Brown wins the 800 at the NJSIAA T&amp;F Meet of Champions</h2>
<p class="article-subheadline">The junior ran a personal best 1:53.98</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>Before this season, Union Catholic&#8217;s <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=732" rel="smarttag" rev="732">Elijah Brown</a></dfn> wasn&#8217;t really on many people&#8217;s radar when it came to the 800-meter run.</p>
<p>Now, he&#8217;s a Meet of Champions winner at that distance.</p>
<p>Just 10 months after the first 800 of his high school career, Brown, a junior at Union Catholic, made a huge surge just before the bell to grab the lead in the 800 at Saturday&#8217;s 51st Meet of Champions at the Bennett Center in Toms River.</p>
<p>He was daring someone to catch him, and no one could as Brown hammered his way to victory in 1:53.98, a huge personal best, the second fastest time in the state this season, No.12 in meet history, and a qualifying time for the New Balance Nationals in New York in two weeks.</p>
<p>Miles Liggins of South Brunswick, who had the top time this season off all runners in the race (1:54.67), was second in a NJ#3 1:54.10.</p>
<p>Brown is the second UC runner to win the 800 at the indoor M of C. Allen Eke won it in 2012.</p>
<p><a href="https://nj.milesplit.com/videos/341183">WATCH A VIDEO INTERVIEW WITH BROWN HERE</a></p>
<p>Brown said the race went out fast, so he waited to make his move.</p>
<p>&#8220;Miles took it out in 54, and I wasn&#8217;t expecting it to go that fast,&#8221;&#8217; said Brown, who also anchored the 4&#215;400 to a sixth place finish. &#8220;So I just waited until like 200 to go and just went for it with all I had.&#8217; My legs were a little tight going into that last 200, so it was all heart the rest of the way.g.&#8221; I had to open up my stride and keep my composure because I knew they were coming.&#8221;</p>
<p>Brown, primarily a 400 runner before this season, has come a long way since running his first high school 800 last April at the Blue Devil Classic in Westfield.</p>
<p>&#8220;The season I&#8217;m having in the 800 is a big shocker because it&#8217;s a big transition from the 400,&#8221; said Brown. &#8220;But I&#8217;ve been working really hard at the 800 and my times have kept coming down.&#8221;</p>
<p>Brown said he knew the door was open for him to win his first M of C title when defending champion and 2018 New Balance National Outdoor Champion Luis Peralta of Passaic decided to skip the state meets and run more open races.</p>
<p>&#8220;When I saw that Luis Peralta wasn&#8217;t going to be running the 800 at states, I knew that was my shot at winning,&#8221; said Brown. &#8220;It made me work even harder to win this. I came into this race with a plan and stuck to it, and it went just the way I was hoping.&#8221;</p>
<p>In other highlights for the Vikings, junior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=741" rel="smarttag" rev="741">Conor Reilly</a></dfn> place fourth in the pole vault by clearing 15-0, and freshman <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=748" rel="smarttag" rev="748">Myles Plummer</a></dfn> placed 8th in the 800 and ran on the sixth place 4&#215;400 relay team.</p>
