<h2 class="article-headline">Reilly breaks school record in pole vault, UC wins five events at Lavino Relays</h2>
<p class="article-subheadline">Bonn was a double winner for the UC girls in team LJ and TJ</p>
</header>
<p><dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1300" rel="smarttag" rev="1300">Conor Reilly</a></dfn> broke the school indoor record in the pole vault and was a member of two winning teams, and Alexandra Bonn was a double-winner  for the Union Catholic girls at Saturday&#8217;s Lavino Relays in Lawrenceville.</p>
<p>Reilly, senior, soared over the bar at 15-6 in the pole vault, tied for No. 3 in the state this season, to break the Union Catholic school indoor record. Reilly&#8217;s 15-6 also broke his personal best of 15-3 that he cleared last spring, and it helped UC break the school two-man indoor PV record as Reilly and <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1297" rel="smarttag" rev="1297">Justin Lopez</a></dfn> (12-0) combined for a winning total of 27-6.</p>
<p>In addition to his record stetting exploits in the pole vault, Reilly also soared 21-2 when he teamed up with <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1289" rel="smarttag" rev="1289">Oluwafemi Asenuga</a></dfn> (20-8) to win the team long jump.</p>
<p>On the girls side, UC won the 4&#215;200 in 1:46.31, and finished first in the long jump and triple jump.</p>
<p>Bonn, one of most talented freshman stars in the state, leaped 17-0 when she teamed with A&#8217;Liyah Taylor (16-7) to win the long jump, and went 34-0 in the triple jump when she and Felicia Quainoo (36-1) combined to finish first.</p>
