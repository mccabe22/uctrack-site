<h2 class="article-headline">UC boys T&amp;F team places second in shuttle hurdles and Brown sparkles at Arcadia Invit.</h2>
<div class="noprint story_email"><time class="article-date">4/6/2019 11:29:00 AM</time></div>
<p class="article-subheadline">The two-day meet concluded on Saturday in Arcadia, California</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic boys made its presence felt at one of the biggest track and field meets in the country when the Vikings competed at the two-day Arcadia Invitational that concluded on Saturday in Arcadia, California.</p>
<p>The quartet of <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=355" rel="smarttag" rev="355">Elijah Abraham</a></dfn>, (15.2), <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=375" rel="smarttag" rev="375">Ryan McLaughlin</a></dfn> (15.3), <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=371" rel="smarttag" rev="371">Brandon Leslie</a></dfn> (15.5), and John Animashaun (16.3) combined to place second in the invitational shuttle hurdles in a   NJ#4 1:02.88.</p>
<p>Junior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=362" rel="smarttag" rev="362">Elijah Brown</a></dfn>, the indoor Meet of Champions 800-meter winner, ran a NJ#1 1:53.94 to place sixth in the 800, and anchored the 4&#215;800 that placed 12th in the 4&#215;800 in 8:15.66.</p>
<p>In other highlights, the 4&#215;200 team of Oluwafemi Asengua (23.8), <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=947" rel="smarttag" rev="947">Connor Cooper</a></dfn> (22.1), Chindeu Okoro (22.9) and John Animashaun (23.3) finished sixth in the boys 4&#215;200 in a NJ#20 1:32.30</p>
<p>Freshman <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=957" rel="smarttag" rev="957">Myles Plummer</a></dfn> finished 20th in the 800 in a NJ#3 1:56.79, which smashed the school outdoor freshman record of 2:00.39 set in 2014 by Greg Gibbs, and ran on the 4&#215;800 team.</p>

<h2 class="article-headline">The UC girls T&amp;F team burns up the track at the Arcadia Invitational</h2>
<div class="noprint story_email"><time class="article-date">4/6/2019 11:10:00 AM</time></div>
<p class="article-subheadline">The Vikings ran several N.J. No. 1 times at the 2-day meet in California</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic girls track and field team put on a very impressive show with several N.J. No. 1 times in its first big meet of the season at the two-day Arcadia Invitational that concluded on Saturday in Arcadia, California.</p>
<p>Junior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=407" rel="smarttag" rev="407">Leena Morant</a></dfn> ran a NJ#1 2:11.88 to place fifth in the 800-meter run, ran 55.5 on the sixth place 4&#215;400 that ran a NJ#1 3:51.87, and split 2:13.9 on the NJ#1/US#3 4&#215;800 that ran 9:13.96.</p>
<p>Also for the UC girls, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=404" rel="smarttag" rev="404">Jerika Lufrano</a></dfn> the indoor Meet of Champions 1,600 winner, finished sixth in the mile 5:01.87 and split 2:18.9 on the 4&#215;800.</p>
<p>Lufrano and Morant were joined on the 4&#215;800 by <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=417" rel="smarttag" rev="417">Morgan Williams</a></dfn> (2:17.9) and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=409" rel="smarttag" rev="409">Ashleigh Patterson</a></dfn> (2:22.7), and Morant teamed up with <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=402" rel="smarttag" rev="402">Avery Jordan</a></dfn> (58.9), <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=415" rel="smarttag" rev="415">Sydney Tucker</a></dfn> (57.3) and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=420" rel="smarttag" rev="420">Zamira Ysaac</a></dfn> (58.9) in the 4&#215;400.</p>
<p>Tucker also burned a NJ#1 12.31 to place ninth in the 100 dash, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=467" rel="smarttag" rev="467">A&#8217;Liyah Thomas</a></dfn> hit a NJ#1 mark of 17-10 3/4 to place 10th in the long jump.</p>
<p>The Vikings also ran a NJ#2 1:01.37 to place fourth in the invitational shuttle hurdles, and placed fifth in the 4&#215;200 in a NJ#2 1:41.70.</p>
<p>Senior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=400" rel="smarttag" rev="400">Hope Handlin</a></dfn> cleared 10-7 to place 11th and break her own outdoor school record of 9-6 that she cleared last year, and and Williams (2:18.14), and Patterson (2:19.91) placed 24th and 30th, repsectively, in the 800.</p>
