<h2 class="article-headline" style="overflow-wrap: break-word; box-sizing: border-box; font-size: 1.5rem; margin: 0.83rem 0px;">UC places second in mixed 4&#215;400 relay to earn All-American at New Balance Nationals</h2>
<p class="article-subheadline" style="overflow-wrap: break-word; box-sizing: border-box;">

<p><span style="color: #000000; font-family: 'News Cycle', sans-serif; font-size: 14px;">NEW YORK &#8211; Union Catholic capped off a great weekend at the New Balance National Championships when the Vikings placed second in the first ever mixed 4&#215;400-meter relay on Sunday to earn All-American honors at the New Balance Track and Field Center.</span><br style="overflow-wrap: break-word; box-sizing: border-box; color: #000000; font-family: 'News Cycle', sans-serif; font-size: 14px;" /><br style="overflow-wrap: break-word; box-sizing: border-box; color: #000000; font-family: 'News Cycle', sans-serif; font-size: 14px;" /><span style="color: #000000; font-family: 'News Cycle', sans-serif; font-size: 14px;">The Vikings foursome of Makay Johnson (50.9), </span><dfn style="overflow-wrap: break-word; box-sizing: border-box; color: #000000; font-family: 'News Cycle', sans-serif; font-size: 14px;"><a style="overflow-wrap: break-word; box-sizing: border-box; color: black;" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=106" rel="smarttag" rev="106">Khamil Evans</a></dfn><span style="color: #000000; font-family: 'News Cycle', sans-serif; font-size: 14px;"> (56.5), </span><dfn style="overflow-wrap: break-word; box-sizing: border-box; color: #000000; font-family: 'News Cycle', sans-serif; font-size: 14px;"><a style="overflow-wrap: break-word; box-sizing: border-box; color: black;" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=118" rel="smarttag" rev="118">Leena Morant</a></dfn><span style="color: #000000; font-family: 'News Cycle', sans-serif; font-size: 14px;"> (56.5), and Elijah Brown (48.7)) combined to run 3:33.22 to edge Bullis of Maryland, third in 3:33.24. Team War of Virginia won the race in 3:32.40.</span><br style="overflow-wrap: break-word; box-sizing: border-box; color: #000000; font-family: 'News Cycle', sans-serif; font-size: 14px;" /><br style="overflow-wrap: break-word; box-sizing: border-box; color: #000000; font-family: 'News Cycle', sans-serif; font-size: 14px;" /><span style="color: #000000; font-family: 'News Cycle', sans-serif; font-size: 14px;">The runner-up finish by Union Catholic gave the Vikings three All-American teams at the national meet. The boys 4&#215;400 (fifth place) and the girls shuttle hurdles (sixth place) also earned All-American honors. A total of 12 UC athletes earned All-American status.</span></p>
