<h2 class="article-headline">UC girls run one of the fastest distance medley relay times in state history</h2>
<p class="article-byline"><span style="text-decoration: underline;">By:</span> Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>The girls distance medley relay was hyped up as the race of the night heading into Tuesday&#8217;s 87th Eastern States Championships in the New York Armory&#8217;s New Balance Track and Field Center.</p>
<p>And boy was it ever!!</p>
<p>Union Catholic and Colts Neck hooked up in a red hot battle as the two powerhouses pushed each other to two of the fastest times in state history.</p>
<p>Union Catholic won the race by stopping the clock at 11:45.70, the third fastest time in NJ indoor history. Colts Neck was second in 11:49.73, No. 4 in state indoor history.<br />
UC and Colts Neck are now No. 2 and No. 3 in the U.S. this season behind the 11:21.95 run by the Purple Track Club (Ann Arbor Pioneer High School) in Michigan. That&#8217;s the fastest time in U.S. high school history, but since it was run on a 300m flat track, it&#8217;s not the eligible for national record consideration. Indoor records must be achieved on 200m tracks.</p>
<p>The only NJ teams to ever run faster indoors than UC and Colts Neck are Southern Regional, which ran a state record 11:41.12 to finish first at the 2009 New Balance Nationals Championships, and Haddonfield, which ran 11:43.94 to place second at the 2014 New Balance Nationals.</p>
<p>The splits for UC were 3:39.94 for 1,200 by junior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1970" rel="smarttag" rev="1970">Kaleigh Gunsiorowski</a></dfn>, 1:01.20 for 400 by senior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1329" rel="smarttag" rev="1329">Aniyah Charles</a></dfn>, 2:17.38 for 800 by junior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1345" rel="smarttag" rev="1345">Maameyaa Nyinah</a></dfn>, and sophomore Peyton Hollis anchored in 4:47.18.</p>
<p>Union Catholic turned in several other sparkling performances.</p>
<p>Senior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1330" rel="smarttag" rev="1330">Jada Mia Puryear</a></dfn> won the girls shot put with a throw of 40-6. The girls 4&#215;800 squad finished first in 9:25.40. Junior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1328" rel="smarttag" rev="1328">Alexandra Bonn</a></dfn> soared 17-7.25 to place second in the long jump, and she finished third in the long jump triple jump with a season-best 36-4.75, which is the second best jump in the state this season. Bonn is also ranked No. 4 in the state in the long jump with a 17-9.</p>
<p>On the boys side, Noah Cooper extended his own state freshman record in the long jump with a second-place leap of 21-8. Freshman Evan Weber placed fourth in the two-mile run in a personal best 9:55.64, and the 4&#215;800 team finished ninth in 8:16.53.</p>
