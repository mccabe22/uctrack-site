<h2 class="article-headline">UC girls smash Union County record, advance to 4&#215;800 C of A at Penn Relays</h2>
<p class="article-subheadline">Jerika Lufrano, Leena Morant, Zamira Ysaac, and Morgan Jackson combined to run 9:10.04</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p dir="ltr">PHILADELPHIA &#8211; The Union Catholic girls stormed its way into the 4&#215;800 Championship of America race in record breaking fashion at Thursday&#8217;s 124th Penn Relays at historic Franklin Field.</p>
<p>The Vikings foursome of <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=404" rel="smarttag" rev="404">Jerika Lufrano</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=407" rel="smarttag" rev="407">Leena Morant</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=420" rel="smarttag" rev="420">Zamira Ysaac</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=401" rel="smarttag" rev="401">Morgan Jackson</a></dfn> not only took down the school record, but its time of 9:10.04 also shattered the Union County record as UC placed second in its qualifying heat with the fourth fastest time among the 12 qualifiers for Friday&#8217;s final at 1:05 p.m.</p>
<p>UC&#8217;s 9:10.04, No. 5 in the nation this season, broke the Union County record of 9:12.9 that was set by Summit in 1981. The former UC school record of 9:13.42 was set during the 2015 indoor season.</p>
<p>This marks the third straight year that a UC girls team has advanced to a Championship of America race and the first time the girls 4&#215;800 team has advanced here. The UC girls 4&#215;400 team, led by Olympian Sydney McLaughlin, placed third the past two years in the C of A. It&#8217;s also the fifth time in the past five years that UC will have a team in a C of A race. In 2014, the UC boys ran in both the 4&#215;400 and 4&#215;800 C of A, a remarkable feat.</p>
<p>So how did the UC girls all underclassmen 4&#215;800 relay team make the prestigious C of A?</p>
<p dir="ltr">Running in the first qualifying heat of the day, Lufrano, a junior, led off with a 2:20.38 split. Morant, a sophomore, then cranked out a personal best 2:11.55, the second fastest split of the day, to move the team into second place. Sophomore Ysaac followed with a personal best 2:17.33 to put UC in front. Jackson, a sophomore, closed it out for UC with a PR of 2:20.80 as she came across the line a half step behind Dasia Wilson of Neumann Goretti High School of Philadelphia, which ran 9:09.54.</p>
<p dir="ltr">The only other teams among the 64 that competed in the 4&#215;800 that ran faster than UC were defending champion Holmwood Tech of Jamaica (9:08.49) and Padua Academy (9:08.72).</p>
<p dir="ltr">&#8220;Our goal was all four of us to put it together and make the final,&#8221; said Morant. &#8220;We feel like if we all ran collectively strong we could go under 9:20, so to go 9:10 is a big deal for us. I am very proud of how all of us ran and we will be striving to run faster tomorrow and she what we can do.&#8221;</p>
<p dir="ltr">Morant said she and her teammates were out to prove that UC isn&#8217;t just a sprint school.</p>
<p dir="ltr">&#8220;We always been known as a sprint team, so to come out here and make it to the finals in a different race and be the first girls team from Union Catholic to make it to the final in a 4&#215;800 makes this even more special,&#8221; said Morant.</p>
