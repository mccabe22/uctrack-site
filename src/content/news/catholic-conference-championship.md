<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Results:</span>  <a href="/Reports/Meet_Results/Boys-2013_11_09-Warinanco%20Park-Catholic%20Conference%20Championship_ID480.pdf" target="_blank">Boys </a>| <a href="/Reports/Meet_Results/Girls-2013_11_09-Warinanco%20Park-Catholic%20Conference%20Championship_ID411.pdf" target="_blank">Girls</a>          <a href="http://nj.milesplit.com/meets/148619-njctc-championships" target="_blank">Full Results</a>           <a href="http://uctrack.smugmug.com/CrossCountry/2013-XC/NJCTC-Championships-11913" target="_blank">Photo Gallery</a></p>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Performers of the Meet:</span> Kenny Evely  &amp; Leticia Rovelo</p>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Highlights</span><br />
-Girls Win the Overall Team Title and &#8220;A Division&#8221; with 63 Points. 1st NJCTC Championship in School History!<br />
-Girls Break the School Record for Team Average (20:45)<br />
-Asia Johnson Breaks the School and Sophomore Class Record with a time of 19:54.0<br />
-Tressa Novack Breaks the Freshman Class Record with a time of 20:45<br />
-Boys Win the &#8220;B Division&#8221; Team Title with 144 Points<br />
-Boys Break the School Record for Team Average (17:16)<br />
-Greg Gibbs just misses the Freshman Class Record with a time of 12:37. (Nick Rhein 12:35.9 / 2010)</p>
