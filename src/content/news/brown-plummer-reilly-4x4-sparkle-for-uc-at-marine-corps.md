<p><dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=732" rel="smarttag" rev="732">Elijah Brown</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=748" rel="smarttag" rev="748">Myles Plummer</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=741" rel="smarttag" rev="741">Conor Reilly</a></dfn> and the 4&#215;400-meter relay team all threw down strong performances for the Union Catholic boys track and field team at Saturday&#8217;s Marine Corp Holiday Classic at the 168th Street Armory in New York.</p>
<p>Brown, a junior, ran 49.60 to place fifth in the 400, the second fastest time by a NJ athlete this season, and split 49.77 on the fourth-place 4&#215;400 that ran a NJ#2 3:25.00 to qualify for the New Balance National Championships.</p>
<p>Plummer broke his own school freshman record in the 800 by placing sixth in a NJ#1 1:58.74, and anchored the 4&#215;400 in 51.17. Plummer&#8217;s 1:58.74 broke his school record of 1:59.71 that he ran at the Essex County Coaches Invit. on Dec. 14 at Ocean Breeze,</p>
<p>Reilly, senior, cleared 14-6 to finish third in the pole vault, and led off the 4&#215;400 with a 52.30 carry in the 4&#215;400. Reilly is currently ranked second in the N.J. in the pole vault with the 15-0 he made earlier this month.</p>
<p>Senior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=728" rel="smarttag" rev="728">Elijah Abraham</a></dfn> also had a strong meet for the Vikings by running 8.19 in the trials of the 55 hurdles and running a 51.74 led in the 4&#215;400.</p>
