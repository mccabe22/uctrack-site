<p><dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=420" rel="smarttag" rev="420">Zamira Ysaac</a></dfn> and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=415" rel="smarttag" rev="415">Sydney Tucker</a></dfn> each scored victories and several other Vikings came up with huge performances to spark the Union Catholic girls to its fourth straight title at Thursday&#8217;s Union County Championships at Hub Stine Field in Plainfield.</p>
<p>The Vikings outscored runner-up Westfield, 115 1/4-86.</p>
<p>Tucker, a sophomore, had the best meet of career. She captured the 100 dash in a personal best 12.29, leaped a personal best 17-5 1/2 to place fourth in the long jump, and she was sixth in the 200 in 25.42, also a personal best.</p>
<p>Ysaac, a sophomore, won the 400 hurdles in 1:03.15 and was sixth in the 400 in 59.21.</p>
<p>Several other Vikings came up big to help the team continue its dominance in the county.</p>
<p>Junior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=395" rel="smarttag" rev="395">Khamil Evans</a></dfn> was second in both the 200 (24.89) and 400 (55.87). <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=407" rel="smarttag" rev="407">Leena Morant</a></dfn> finished second in the 800 in 2:13.38. Felicia Quianoo ran a PR of 14.81 to place second in the 100 hurdles.  <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=391" rel="smarttag" rev="391">Alexandra Borik</a></dfn> dropped a huge personal best of 5:13.27 to place fourth in the 1,600. That&#8217;s 19 seconds better than her previous best. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=394" rel="smarttag" rev="394">Asiah Cooper</a></dfn> placed second in the long jump with an 18-1. Freshman <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=467" rel="smarttag" rev="467">A&#8217;Liyah Thomas</a></dfn> ran a PR of 15.68 to finish fourth in the 100 hurdles and soared a PR of 17-7 to place third in the long jump.</p>
<p>The Vikings will be back in action on Monday and Tuesday in the NJSIAA South Jersey, Non-Public A Championships at Donovan Catholic in Toms River.</p>
