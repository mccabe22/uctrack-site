<h2 class="article-headline">Strong races by Plummer and Morant led the charge for UC XC team at Magee Meet</h2>
<p class="article-subheadline">The Vikings had several strong performances on the Greystone course</p>
</header>
<p><dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=538" rel="smarttag" rev="538">Myles Plummer</a></dfn> and <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=549" rel="smarttag" rev="549">Leena Morant</a></dfn> each hammered out impressive races to lead a strong charge by the Union Catholic cross-country squads at Saturday&#8217;s 40th NJCTC Bernie Magee Class Meet on the Greystone course at Central Park in Morris Plains.</p>
<p>Plummer, one of the top young runners in the state, cranked out a 16:29 over the 3.1-mile courre to place second in the boys Sophomore Division race to help lead the Vikings to a 10th place finish. Plummer&#8217;s time was the 11th fastest in the three varsity races combined.</p>
<p>Morant made a strong season debut by running 19:26 to finish fourth in a very talented girls Senior Division field. Morant&#8217;s time was the seventh fastest of the meet and helped lead UC to a second-place finish in the Senior Division behind Ridge, 15-39. UC&#8217;s two other scorers in the Senior Division were <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=559" rel="smarttag" rev="559">Ashleigh Patterson</a></dfn>, 11th in 21:00, and <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=558" rel="smarttag" rev="558">Zoe Lee</a></dfn>, 25th in 21:22.</p>
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="https://ucvikings.com/common/controls/image_handler.aspx?thumb_id=14&amp;image_path=/images/2019/9/23/Abby_Granrath.jpg" alt="1846" width="667" height="500" /><br />
In the girls Sophomore Division, <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=553" rel="smarttag" rev="553">Abby Granrath</a></dfn> (pictured here) placed 12th in 20:44, and <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=552" rel="smarttag" rev="552">Jenna Keith</a></dfn> was 16th in 21:11 to help the UC girls place fifth in the meet merge standings.</div>
