<p><em>By James Lambert</em></p>
<p>Union Catholic pulled out the brooms on Saturday at Holmdel Park.</p>
<p>The Union Catholic boys and girls cross country teams swept team titles at the 51st Shore Coaches Invitational, with the Vikings also taking the girls team merge championship in a dominant showing that showcased the incredible depth of the program.</p>
<p>The UC girls, the preseason No. 1 team in the country, were far from full strength but it didn&#8217;t matter. With seniors Paige Sheppard, Cayleigh Kaiser, Sophia Thompson and Kayla Devine held out, the Vikings showcased their loaded sophomore class and rolled to victory in the E Division.</p>
<p>Union Catholic put four sophomores and a junior in the top seven, going 2-4-5-6-7. Sophomore Harper Telesca led the way by finishing second in 18:50.91, followed by sophomores Abigail Robisky and Eden Solorzano, who placed fourth and fifth in 18:59.07 and 18:59.31, respectively. Jia Jain was sixth in 19:20.37, and junior Saffa Ellis finished seventh in 19:52.74.</p>
<p>The Vikings scored 24 points to defeat No. 6-ranked Haddonfield, which finished with 49. Union Catholic&#8217;s five scorers averaged 19:12 and had a 61-second compression.</p>
<p>Robisky&#8217;s performance was particularly impressive in her first-ever race at Holmdel Park. The sophomore finished third in the 400-meter at last spring&#8217;s Meet of Champions and set a New Jersey freshman record of 2:05.24 in the 800. After putting in serious work to crack UC&#8217;s loaded lineup this season, Robisky has emerged as one of the most pleasant surprises in the state.</p>
<p>She is a perfect example of what can happen when an athlete pours their heart and soul into training and trusts the process.</p>
<p>The Vikings&#8217; depth was on full display, with sophomore Myra Sakhuja finishing 18th in 21:02.36 and senior Aubrey Devers placing 24th in 21:14.45.</p>
<p>Union Catholic also dominated the team merge, scoring 97 points to finish first. No. 2-ranked Ridgewood, the A Division champion, was second with 156 points, followed by Middletown South with 217.</p>
<p>The UC boys, ranked No. 2 in the state, pulled out a thrilling victory in the F Division, edging No. 7-ranked Glassboro, 39-49, and finished third in the overall team merge.</p>
<p>Senior Jayden Aine led the Vikings by finishing third in 16:27.32, followed by senior Danny Kuran in fourth at 16:32.97. Junior Nathan Simonich was sixth in 16:35.04, senior Quintin Clemons placed 11th in 16:56.05, and senior Nick Frank finished 16th in 17:04.46 to complete the scoring five.</p>
<p>Union Catholic averaged 16:43 and had a 37-second compression from its first to fifth runners. Senior Manny Sanchez was 17th in 17:10.86, giving the Vikings six runners in the top 17.</p>
<p>The Vikings also won the boys JV D Division Championship with Jeremiah Shepherd leading the charge with a winning time of 17:15.</p>
