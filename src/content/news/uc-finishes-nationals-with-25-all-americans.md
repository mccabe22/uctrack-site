<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Highlights<strong> </strong></span><br />
-Shuttle Hurdle Relay won the National Title, broke the Meet Record, State Record, County Record and was US#3 All-Time (Animashaun, Scott, McLaughlin, Z. Ghizzone)<br />
-Swedish Relay (100-200-300-400) placed 2nd (Z. Ghizzone, Jimerson, Animashaun, McLaughlin)<br />
-4&#215;400 Relay placed 3rd with a time of 3:11.45. NJ #5 All-Time, County Record, School Record. <span style="text-decoration: underline;">Splits:</span> Jimerson 47.5, Evely 49.1, Z. Ghizzone 48.40, McLaughlin 46.02<br />
-1600 Sprint Medley Relay placed 3rd (Franco, DeAngelis, Jimerson, Evely)<br />
-800 Sprint Medley Relay placed 4th (Scott, Franco, Animashaun, DeAngelis)<br />
-Andrew Ghizzone place 5th in the Decathlon with a School Record score and he broke the School Record in the Pole Vault in the process<br />
-4&#215;200 Relay placed 5th and broke the School Record (Animashaun, McLaughlin, Z. Ghizzone, Jimerson)<br />
-Sean Brennan (Sophomore) placed 2nd in the the Emerging Elite Discus<br />
-Brian Sagendorf (Sophomore) placed 4th in the Emerging Elite Javelin<br />
&#8211;<strong>Girls</strong> Emerging Elite 800 Sprint Medley places 7th (Gibbs, Henry, Beauliere, Coverson)</p>
