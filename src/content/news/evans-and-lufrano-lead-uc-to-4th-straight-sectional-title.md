<h2 class="article-headline">Double winners Evans and Lufrano spark UC T&amp;F team to sectional title</h2>
<div class="noprint story_email"><a href="https://www.addthis.com/bookmark.php?v=250&amp;pub=xa-4a7320e7645958d0"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="https://s7.addthis.com/static/btn/lg-share-en.gif" alt="Bookmark and Share" width="125" height="16" /></a> <time class="article-date">5/25/2018 9:39:00 AM</time></div>
<p class="article-subheadline">The Vikings repeated as South Jersey, Non-Public A Champions</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p><dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=395" rel="smarttag" rev="395">Khamil Evans</a></dfn> and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=404" rel="smarttag" rev="404">Jerika Lufrano</a></dfn> each won two individual races to help spark the Union Catholic girls, the No. 1 ranked team in New Jersey, to its fourth straight sectional title at the NJSIAA South Jersey, Non-Public A Championships on Tuesday at Donovan Catholic High School in Toms River.</p>
<p>The Vikings outscored runner-up Paul VI, 185-92. Full results of the meet are <a href="http://nj.milesplit.com/meets/310108/results#.WwgjMMgvxPa">here</a></p>
<p>Evans, a junior, led a 1-2-3 finish in the 200 with a winning time of 25.36, and won the 400 in 55.98, just off her NJ #4 time of 55.87.</p>
<p>Lufrano, also a junior, ran 5:09.04 to win the 1,600 and 11:03.68 to capture the 3,200. UC also picked up wins from <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=407" rel="smarttag" rev="407">Leena Morant</a></dfn> in the 800 (2:14.04), <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=393" rel="smarttag" rev="393">Amaya Chadwick</a></dfn>, who led a 1-2-3 finish for the Vikings in the 100 hurdles with a NJ #3 time of 14.46, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=415" rel="smarttag" rev="415">Sydney Tucker</a></dfn> in the 100 (12.39) and its 4&#215;100 team (48.88). The 4&#215;100 team consisted of <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=389" rel="smarttag" rev="389">Kira Alexander</a></dfn>, Tucker, Evans and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=411" rel="smarttag" rev="411">Felicia Quainoo</a></dfn>.</p>
<p>The Vikings will be defend its title at the State Non-Public A Championships on June 1 and 2nd at Franklin High School</p>
