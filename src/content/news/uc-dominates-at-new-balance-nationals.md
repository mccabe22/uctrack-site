<p><strong>New Balance Indoor National Championship</strong><br />
<strong>Friday, March 14th &#8211; Sunday, March 16th, 2014</strong></p>
<p>The Union Catholic Boys and Girls teams put on a show at the 2014 Indoor National Meet! The boys repeated as 4&#215;200 and 4&#215;400 National Champions, while the girls earned the first relay All-American honors for UC by placing 4th int the 4&#215;200!</p>

<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Highlights</span><br />
-1st Place &#8211; Boys 4&#215;200 Relay &#8211; 3:15.53 &#8211; State Record, US#8 All-Time<br />
-1st Place &#8211; Boys 4&#215;400 Relay &#8211; 1:27.04 &#8211; NJ#2 All-Time, US#4 All-Time<br />
-2nd Place &#8211; Boys Shuttle Hurdle Relay &#8211; 29.52 &#8211; NJ#2 All-Time, US#4 All-Time<br />
-3rd Place &#8211; Boys Sprint Medley Relay &#8211; 3:26.16 &#8211; Union County Record, NJ#2  All-Time, US#8 All-Time<br />
-4th Place &#8211; Girls 4&#215;200 Meter Relay &#8211; 1:40.51 &#8211; Union County Record, NJ#4 All-Time</p>
