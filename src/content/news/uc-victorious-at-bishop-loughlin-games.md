<strong>Highlights</strong><br />
-Boys win the Team Title!<br />
-Sydney McLaughlin wins and breaks the State Record in the 300m Dash. She just missed the Freshman National Record!<br />
-Obafemi Animashaun wins the 55m Hurdles!<br />
-Boys 4&#215;400 wins in convincing fashion (3:22)!<br />
-Jordan Jimerson places 2nd in the 300m Dash and breaks the School Record!<br />
-Imani Beauliere places 3rd in the 55m Dash!<br />
-Sean Brennan places 3rd in the Shot Put and breaks the School Record!<br />
-Andrew Ghizzone places 6th in the Mile Run</p>
