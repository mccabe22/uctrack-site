<h2 class="article-headline">UC girls win, boys place third, and Reilly breaks PV record at Conference Championships</h2>
<div class="noprint story_email"> <time class="article-date">5/13/2019 11:59:00 AM</time></div>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic girls repeated as champions, the boys placed third, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=381" rel="smarttag" rev="381">Conor Reilly</a></dfn> broke a record in the pole vault at the Union County Conference Track and Field Championships, which concluded on Friday at Gov. Livingston in Berkeley Heights.</p>
<p>The UC girls racked up 209 points to win the Watchung Division by more then 100 points over runner-up Westfield, second with 98.</p>
<p>The Vikings were led in the girls competition by <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=415" rel="smarttag" rev="415">Sydney Tucker</a></dfn>, who won the 100 (12.41) and 200 (25.35), <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=411" rel="smarttag" rev="411">Felicia Quainoo</a></dfn>, first in the triple jump (35-9 1/2) and the 100 hurdles (14.51), and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=466" rel="smarttag" rev="466">Alexandra Kwasnik</a></dfn>, who tied the school record by clearing 5-6 to win the high jump.</p>
