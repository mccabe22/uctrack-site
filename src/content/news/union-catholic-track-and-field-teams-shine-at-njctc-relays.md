<p>By Jim Lambert</p>
<p>The Union Catholic track and field teams had a very strong all-round meet when the Vikings racked up five victories at Saturday&#8217;s New Jersey Catholic Track Conference Relays at Ocean Breeze on Staten Island.</p>
<p>In the girls&#8217; competition, the Vikings won two relays and two individual events.  The sprint medley relay team, which consisted of Kayla Ansong, Sinclair Powell, Leilani Gibson, and Caitlin Toy, finished first in 4:22.80, and Ansong, Sadie Thompson, Aubrey Devers and Sahar Ahad combined to win the 4&#215;400 in 4:20.80. Individually, Alyssa Kenol led a 1-2-3 finish for the Vikings in the shot put with a winning throw of 30-2.50, and Natalie Thompson placed first in the long jump with a 17-6.50.     </p>
<p>In the boys&#8217; competition, Noah Cooper (21-9) and Justice Hicks (21-7) finished 1-2 for the Vikings in the long jump, the 4&#215;200 squad of Cooper, Hicks, Dylan Williams, and Marcus McCoy placed second with a NJ No. 4 time of 1:30.92! UC&#8217;s 4&#215;800 squad, comprised of freshmen and sophomores, finished third in 8:20.92, the 4&#215;400 team placed third in 3:30.79, and Josh Hunsinger cleared 10-0 to place fourth in the pole vault.</p>
