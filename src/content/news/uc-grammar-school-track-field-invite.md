<p><b><br />
<span style="text-decoration: underline;">FINAL INSTRUCTIONS</span><br />
</b><b><br />
Parking Options</b><b><br />
</b>The Parking Lot on the Left Side of Union Catholic<br />
The Driveway in Front of Union Catholic<br />
<i><span style="text-decoration: underline;">At 1:15pm &#8211; Parking will be available at Immaculate Heart of Mary Church across the street from UC.</span></i></p>
<p><b>Team Seating/Tents<br />
</b>All teams are asked to <i><span style="text-decoration: underline;">setup their tents in the parking lot on the gym side</span></i>.<br />
No tents or team setup will be permitted on the back driveway<br />
<i>The driveway needs to be clear for emergencies</i></p>
<p><b>Labels</b><br />
Pre-Printed Labels will be provided to each team at the meet, once payment is received. There will be 3 pre-printed labels for each athlete on your team’s roster. Coaches will need to write the event on each label.</p>
<p><b>Coaches&#8217; Meeting</b><br />
There will be a brief Coaches&#8217; Meeting at 12:45pm on the infield, next to the finish line tent. We ask that at least one coach from each team be at the meeting.</p>
<p><b>Heat Sizes</b></p>
<p>100m Fun Run – 15-20 per heat<br />
200m – 1 Athlete per lane, 6 lanes. (Will Start at the 100m Start)<br />
400m – No Lanes – 10-12 athletes per heat<br />
800m – No Lanes – 12-15 athletes per heat<br />
1600m – No Lanes – 12-15 athletes per heat<br />
Sprint Medley Relay (4-1-1-2) – No Lanes – 8-12 teams per heat</p>
<p><b>Seeding Heats<br />
</b>Coaches please make your athletes aware of who you want to run in the faster heats. Also, you are welcome to come to the staging area to make the Clerk aware of which of your athletes you want seeded first.</p>
<p><b>Awards<br />
</b>-Medals will be placed into a bag for each team. At the end of the meet these bags will be distributed to a team representative.<br />
-1<sup>st</sup> place and Runner-Up Trophies will be awarded at the end of the meet.</p>
<p><b>Additional Rules<br />
</b>-You may run 1<sup>st</sup> or 2<sup>nd</sup> graders in a higher division, if you feel they will be competitive<br />
-Athletes can run in higher divisions, but cannot run in a lower division<br />
-Girls can race in Boys relays, but boys cannot race in girls relays.<br />
-Athletes are allowed any 3 events (The relay counts as one of these events)<br />
-Relay Batons will be provided</p>
