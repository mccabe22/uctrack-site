<p>The Union Catholic girls claimed their sixth consecutive NJSIAA Non-Public A cross-country title on November 7 at Holmdel Park. Cayleigh Kaiser won the 5K in 17:54.52, and the Vikings took all five scoring places to finish with the minimum possible score of 15 points.</p><p>Paige Sheppard placed second in 18:23.74, Sophia Thompson third in 18:29.52, Harper Telesca fourth in 18:30.76, and Kayla Devine fifth in 18:54.19. Eden Solorzano finished sixth and Jia Jain took ninth, giving the Vikings seven finishers in the top ten.</p>
