<aside class="summary cf">New York &#8211; The Union Catholic track and field turned in several red hot performances on Saturday at the Stanner Games</aside>
<section class="body cf">
<div>By Jim Lambert</div>
<div>
<p><strong>Amaya Chadwick, Conor Reilly, Jerika Lufrano</strong>, and<strong> Leena Morant</strong> all turned in sparkling performances for the Union Catholic track and field team at Saturday’s Molloy Stanner Games at the New Balance Track and Field Center in New York.</p>
<p>Chadwick won the girls 55-meter hurdles in a sizzling 8.04, the top time in New Jersey this season, No. 4 in the nation this winter, and a junior class school record.</p>
<p>Reilly cleared a huge personal best of 12-6 on his first attempt to win the boys freshman pole vault division and smash his own freshman school record. His previous best was 11-0.</p>
<p>Morant shattered the Union Catholic freshman school record by placing fourth in the girls 600 in 1:36.26.Lufrano took down the UC sophomore school record in the 1,000 by placing 15th in 3:08.25.</p>
