<h2 class="article-headline">Lestarchick throws huge personal best for UC boys at Rebel Shot Put Invit.</h2>
<p class="article-subheadline">The senior unloaded a 42-0 to place seventh at Howell High School</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>Senior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=737" rel="smarttag" rev="737">Ray Lestarchick</a></dfn> unloaded a huge personal best on his final throw to finish seventh to highlight action for Union Catholic at the Rebel Shot Put Series on Friday night at Howell High School</p>
<p>Lestarchick launched a 42-0 on his last attempt to shatter his previous personal best of 36-0 that he hit at the Union County J.V. Championships last spring.</p>
<p>The 42-0 by Lestarchick was not only a big PR, but it also places him No. 3 in the Union County shot put rankings after the opening weekend of the season.</p>
<p>Also in the boys Senior Division competition, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=743" rel="smarttag" rev="743">Jared Ware</a></dfn> placed 8th for UC with a throw of 41-6 1/2, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=733" rel="smarttag" rev="733">Uchenna Duru</a></dfn> also threw 41-61 1/2 for UC to place ninth.</p>
<p>In the girls Senior Division, Rebecca Penevolpe threw 24-9 for Union Catholic to place 18th.</p>
