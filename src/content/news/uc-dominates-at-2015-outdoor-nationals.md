<p><strong>New Balance Outdoor Nationals</strong><br />
<strong>June 19-21, 2015</strong></p>
<p>Union Catholic Finished the 2015 New Balance National Championship Meet with 13 National Champions + 23 All-Americans!</p>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Highlights</span><br />
1st Place &#8211; 400 Hurdles &#8211; Sydney McLaughlin &#8211; 55.87<br />
1st Place &#8211; Girls Swedish Relay &#8211; Chadwick, Coverson, Herran, McLaughlin &#8211; National Record<br />
1st Place &#8211; Girls 4&#215;400 Relay &#8211; Livingston, Onque-Shabazz, Piiru, McLaughlin &#8211; State Record/US #4 All-Time/Meet Record &#8211; 3:35.90<br />
1st Place &#8211; Boys 4&#215;400 Relay &#8211; Dawson, Christie, Robinson, McLaughlin &#8211; 3:12.14<br />
2nd Place &#8211; 400 Hurdles &#8211; Taylor McLaughlin &#8211; 50.20 &#8211; State Record<br />
4th Place &#8211; Girls Sprint Medley Relay &#8211; Herran, Coverson, Onque-Shabazz, Piiru &#8211; School Record<br />
