<h4 class="article-headline">Written by: Jim Lambert</h4>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic girls, ranked No. 2 in the state, blitzed the competition with a remarkable performance and Jimmy Wischusen opened his season with an impressive victory at Saturday&#8217;s Bowdoin Park Classic in Wappingers Falls, N.Y.</p>
<p>Junior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=2226" rel="smarttag" rev="2226">Peyton Hollis</a></dfn> led the charge for the UC girls in the Division 2 race with a winning time of 18:51 as the Vikings placed 1-2-3-5-10 and steamrolled its way to a 20-91 victory over Bronxville (N.Y).  Ridgewood was third with 95. Union Catholic, who averaged 19:42, also came out on top in the team merge, 39-49, over No. 1 ranked Haddonfield, who won the Division 1 race.</p>
<p>The scorers for UC behind Hollis were senior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=2223" rel="smarttag" rev="2223">Kaleigh Gunsiorowski</a></dfn>, who recovered from a fall to finish second in 19:13, junior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=2227" rel="smarttag" rev="2227">Courtney Kaiser</a></dfn>, third in 19:31, freshman <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=2232" rel="smarttag" rev="2232">Ella Solorzano</a></dfn>, fifth in 20:05, and senior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=2228" rel="smarttag" rev="2228">Victoria Urbaez</a></dfn>, 10th in 20:52.</p>
<p>In the boys race, the Vikings ranked No. 2 in NJ and No. 13 nationally, placed second to Christian Brothers Academy, 41-98, in the Division 2 race. CBA is ranked No. 1 in NJ and No. 9 nationally.</p>
<p>Wischusen, a junior who is the favorite to win the Meet of Champions title this season, opened his season with a very controlled and impressive victory in 15:54.2, the ninth fastest time by a NJ runner in course history. UC, which averaged 16:50, also received a ninth place finish from senior Ryan Cichocki in 16:36, a 19th place from senior Caudell Cajuste (16:55), a 26th place from sophomore Evan Weber in 17:04, and 43rd from junior Alessandro Tullo in 17:43.</p>
