<h2 class="article-headline">UC girls win, Cooper breaks state freshman record in boys long jump at NJCTC Meet</h2>
<div class="noprint story_email">Written by: Jim Lambert</div>
<p class="article-byline">It was another great weekend for the Union Catholic track and field teams.</p>
</header>
<p>The UC girls continued their undefeated season by winning another team title, <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1993" rel="smarttag" rev="1993">Noah Cooper</a></dfn> smashed the state freshman record in the boys long jump, <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1299" rel="smarttag" rev="1299">Myles Plummer</a></dfn> dropped a red hot time, and several Vikings turned in sparkling performances at Saturday&#8217;s New Jersey Catholic Track Conference Wayne Letwink Memorial Championships at Ocean Breeze on Staten Island.</p>
<p>In the girls team competition, junior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1328" rel="smarttag" rev="1328">Alexandra Bonn</a></dfn> was a double winner to help lead the charge for Union Catholic as the Vikings defeated runner-up Immaculate Heart Academy, 151-51.75.</p>
<p>Bonn retook the state lead in the triple jump with a winning mark of 36-4.50, and she won the long jump (16-9.75), and was fourth in the hurdles.</p>
<p>UC also picked up first-place finishes from <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1330" rel="smarttag" rev="1330">Jada Mia Puryear</a></dfn> in the shot put (39-6.50), <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1331" rel="smarttag" rev="1331">Ajanae Thompson</a></dfn> in the 55 hurdles in 8.48 (8.37 in the trials), the 4&#215;400 squad (4:08.24), and from <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1345" rel="smarttag" rev="1345">Maameyaa Nyinah</a></dfn>, who led a 1-2-3 finish for the Vikings in the 600. Freshman <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1995" rel="smarttag" rev="1995">Taylor Cox</a></dfn> also had a big day for UC, placing second in both the 55 dash and the 300, and <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1975" rel="smarttag" rev="1975">Simone Motley</a></dfn> had a PR of 36-0 to place second in the shot put.</p>
<p>On the boys side, UC finished third in the overall team standings and first among teams in the B Division.</p>
<p>Cooper provided one of the biggest highlights when he soared 21-3.50 to finish first and smash the state freshman record of 21-2.50 that set in 2011 by Karl Bostick of Bergen Catholic. Cooper is now ranked No. 7 in the nation among freshman in the LJ.</p>
<p>Plummer also had a huge performance, winning the 1,000 in a NJ No. 1 and US #3 2:27.79, which is No. 13 in NJ history.</p>
<p><dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1336" rel="smarttag" rev="1336">Nick Givan</a></dfn> and <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1951" rel="smarttag" rev="1951">Shane Brosnan</a></dfn> also had big days for the Vikings.</p>
<p>Givan won the hurdles in 7.81, placed second in the 300 in a N.J. No. 3 34.65, and ran a personal best 6.59 in the 55 dash trials. Brosnan finished first in the 3,200 in 9:39.15.</p>
