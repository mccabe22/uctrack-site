<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>PHILADELPHIA &#8211; Union Catholic&#8217;s <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=380" rel="smarttag" rev="380">Brandon Patterson</a></dfn> placed seventh in the shot put and the Vikings 4&#215;400-meter relay team ran one of the fastest times of the meet at the 124th Penn Relays at Franklin Field.</p>
<p>Patterson, a senior, placed seventh in the shot put with his throw of 57-11 1/2, and the 4&#215;400 relay team, despite a dropped baton, still ran 3:23.05, the 12th fastest time among all N.J. teams and the 59th fastest time among the 536 teams that competed.</p>
<p>The UC 4&#215;400 team consisted of <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=376" rel="smarttag" rev="376">Daryl Miller</a></dfn>, who ran 50.34, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=362" rel="smarttag" rev="362">Elijah Brown</a></dfn>, who dropped a blistering 47.84, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=370" rel="smarttag" rev="370">Makay Johnson</a></dfn>, who split 50.10, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=356" rel="smarttag" rev="356">Christian Albanese</a></dfn>, who anchored in 54.78.</p>
