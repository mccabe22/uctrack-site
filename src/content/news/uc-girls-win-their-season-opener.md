<h2 class="article-headline">Double winner Tucker helps UC girls roll to season opening victory</h2>
<p class="article-subheadline">The Vikings won the Essex County Coaches Season Opener by more than 100 points</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p><dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=769" rel="smarttag" rev="769">Sydney Tucker</a></dfn> won two sprint races with personal best times and Union Catholic picked up victories from five other athletes as the Vikings overpowered the competition on the way to a huge victory in its season opener at the Essex County Track Coaches Invitational at Ocean Breeze on Friday on Staten Island.</p>
<p>Tucker, a junior, won the 55-meter dash in 7.30 and the 200 in 25.90 to help the Vikings roll to a 157-38 victory over runner-up West Orange.</p>
<p>UC, favored to win its fifth straight Non-Public A  State Track and Field title this season, also received victories from <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=712" rel="smarttag" rev="712">Khamil Evans</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=764" rel="smarttag" rev="764">Leena Morant</a></dfn>, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=717" rel="smarttag" rev="717">Hope Handlin</a></dfn>, A&#8217;Liyah Thomas, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=768" rel="smarttag" rev="768">Felicia Quainoo</a></dfn>, and the 4&#215;400 was also victorious (4:05.90).</p>
<p>Evans won the 400 in 58.35, Morant led a 1-2-3-4 finish for the Vikings in the 800 with a winning time of 2:19.92, Handlin won the pole vault at 9-6, Thomas captured the high jump (4-10) and was second in the long jump (16-3 1/2), and Quainoo won the 55 hurdles in 8.49.</p>
<p>In other highlights, freshman <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=780" rel="smarttag" rev="780">Sydney McDonald</a></dfn> made a great high school debut by placing third in the 20 in 26.75, and fifth in the 55 dash in 7.60. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=770" rel="smarttag" rev="770">Angelique Williams</a></dfn> finished second in the high jump (4-10), fourth in the long jump (16-1), and was sixth in the 55 hurdles in 9.01. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=757" rel="smarttag" rev="757">Isabella Gray</a></dfn> came up with a third place finish for the Vikings in the pole vault by clearing 9-0.</p>
