<h2 class="article-headline">Bonn and Givan win the hurdles to lead strong season opener for the UC track and field teams</h2>
</header>
<p>Written by: Jim Lambert</p>
<p><a href="https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1MDopTP-4jBrdTSOUNpH3DuD5sESSAoeDviG_n9n53fY/edit#gid=1392187694" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Results</a> | <a href="https://nj.milesplit.com/meets/408435-union-county-qualifying-meets-pod-a-b-c-2021/results#.YDQMHmpKhs8" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Videos/Full Results</a></p>
<p>The Union Catholic track and field teams opened its season on Saturday with several strong performances at a Watchung Division Pod Meet at the Bennett Center in Toms River.</p>
<p>The top two finishers in each event automatically qualified for the Union County Series Championship on March 21 at the Bennett Center.</p>
<p>The Vikings dominated the hurdles as sophomore <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1328" rel="smarttag" rev="1328">Alexandra Bonn</a></dfn> led a 1-2-3 finish for the girls in the 55-meter hurdles with a winning time of 9.04. Senior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1321" rel="smarttag" rev="1321">A&#8217;Liyah Thomas</a></dfn> was second in 9.13, and sophomore <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1331" rel="smarttag" rev="1331">Ajanae Thompson</a></dfn> placed third in 10.07. Thompson, who also finished third in the 55 dash in 7.46, blasted an 8.67 in the trials.</p>
<p>In the boys 55 hurdles, UC junior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1336" rel="smarttag" rev="1336">Nick Givan</a></dfn> picked up the victory with a big personal best of 8.08.</p>
<p>Also for the UC boys, senior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1292" rel="smarttag" rev="1292">Connor Cooper</a></dfn> ran 6.88 to place second in the 55 dash, junior Shane Brosnan (a personal best 4:23.20), and junior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1299" rel="smarttag" rev="1299">Myles Plummer</a></dfn> (4:23.89) finished 1-2 in the 1,600.</p>
<p>Jimmy Wischusen, running the first track race of his life, made a memorable debut. The freshman showed off his wheels on the way to winning his heat of the boys 1600 in 4:50.18 to place 10th overall. Wischusen displayed great closing speed by blasting a swift 32 second final 200 to secure the win.</p>
<p>On the girls side, senior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1324" rel="smarttag" rev="1324">Morgan Williams</a></dfn> (5:33.25), and junior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1305" rel="smarttag" rev="1305">Abby Granrath</a></dfn> (5:36.24)  finished 5-6 in the 1600.</p>
