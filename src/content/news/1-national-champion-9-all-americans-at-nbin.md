<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Highlights</span><br />
-Sydney McLaughlin wins the National Championship in the 60m Hurdles with a National Sophomore Record time of 8.17   <a href="http://highschoolsports.nj.com/news/article/295162638338027328/sydney-mclaughlin-takes-down-state-and-national-records-with-victory-before-injury-forced-her-to-miss-4x400-at-nationals/?NC=1234" target="_blank">NJ.com Article</a><br />
-Girls Shuttle Hurdle Relay places 2nd (Herran, McLaughlin, Coverson, Gibbs) and breaks the County Record<br />
-Taylor McLaughlin runs 46.80 in the 400m Trials. NJ#2 All-Time, US#6 All-Tim<br />
&#8211;<span style="text-decoration: underline;">Note:</span> Taylor ran a time of 46.50 to win in the final, but was disqualified for a lane infraction<br />
-Boys 4x400m Relay places 6th to earn All-American Status (Henderson, Sagendorf, Gibbs, McLaughlin)<br />
-Girls 4&#215;800 places 7th overall and breaks the County Record by 10 seconds (Onque-Shabazz, Johnson, Piiru, Sullivan)  <a href="http://highschoolsports.nj.com/news/article/7418127314836916661/an-inside-look-at-how-union-catholic-took-down-long-standing-county-record-in-the-4x800-relay/?NC=234" target="_blank">NJ.com 4&#215;800 Article</a><br />
-Girls EE 4&#215;200 runs 1:43.99 to place 9th with 3 frosh/1 soph (Scott, Cooper, Gardner, Chadwick)</p>
