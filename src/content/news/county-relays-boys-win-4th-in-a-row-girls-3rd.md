<p>The Union Catholic Boys made it 4 Union County Outdoor Relay Championships in a Row on Friday! Defeating runner-up Scotch Plains-Fanwood by a score of 122-65. The Girls squad placed 3rd overall and broke 6 school records in the process.</p>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Performers of the Meet:</span> Andrew Ghizzone, Serge Gilbert, Sydney McLaughlin and Taylor Gardner</p>
