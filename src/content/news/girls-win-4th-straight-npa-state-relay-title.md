<h2 class="article-headline">UC girls sweep track events, steamroll to 4th straight Non-Public A Relay title</h2>
<p class="article-subheadline">The Vikings defeated runner-up Paramus Catholic, 72-37</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic girls did it again!</p>
<p>In a performance that solidified its status as the top team in New Jersey, the Vikings threw down a remarkable display by sweeping all six track events and steamrolling its way to a 72-37 victory over runner-up Paramus Catholic to capture its fourth straight State Non-Public A Relay title on Thursday night at the Bennett Center in Toms River.</p>
<p>UC won the shuttle hurdles in a NJ #2 32.34, the distance medley in a school record and NJ #1 12:24.00, the sprint medley in 4:20.58, the 4&#215;200 in 1:46.21, the 4&#215;400 in 4:09.59, and the 4&#215;800 in 9:41.47. In the field, the Vikings placed second in shot put and fourth in the high jump.</p>
<p>Several athletes had outstanding performances to lead the Vikings to the dominating victory.</p>
<p><dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=115" rel="smarttag" rev="115">Jerika Lufrano</a></dfn> anchored the DMR with a 5:10.3 carry and ran 2:28.7 in the 4&#215;800. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=130" rel="smarttag" rev="130">Zamira Ysaac</a></dfn> led off the DMR with a 3:52.6 split and ran 60.7 in the 4&#215;400. Mia Bennet-Jones ran 61.4 in the DMR and 63.4 in the 4&#215;400. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=118" rel="smarttag" rev="118">Leena Morant</a></dfn> went 2:19 in the DMR and 2:27 on the anchor of the SMR. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=106" rel="smarttag" rev="106">Khamil Evans</a></dfn> ran 25.6 in the 4&#215;200 and 58.8 in the SMR. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=107" rel="smarttag" rev="107">Brianna Ewing</a></dfn> ran 7.7 in the shuttle hurdles and 26.6 in the 4&#215;200. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=125" rel="smarttag" rev="125">Sydney Tucker</a></dfn> ran 26.5 in the 4&#215;200 and 27.1 in the SMR. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=121" rel="smarttag" rev="121">Felicia Quainoo</a></dfn> ran 8.2 in the shuttles and 27.2 in the 4&#215;200. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=120" rel="smarttag" rev="120">Ashleigh Patterson</a></dfn> split 63.1 in the 4&#215;400 and 2:24 in the 4&#215;800. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=126" rel="smarttag" rev="126">Morgan Williams</a></dfn> ran 62.1 in the 4&#215;400 and split 2:24.5 in the 4&#215;800. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=128" rel="smarttag" rev="128">Angelique Williams</a></dfn> ran 8.5 in the shuttles and cleared 5-0 in the high jump. In the shot put, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=116" rel="smarttag" rev="116">Sophia Martinez</a></dfn> threw a 33-1 1/2 and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=122" rel="smarttag" rev="122">Christina Samuels</a></dfn> had a 31-9.</p>
<p>&#8220;The goal was to sweep the track events like we did at the county meet,&#8221; said UC head girls track and field coach <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/staff.aspx?staff=2" rel="smarttag" rev="2">Mike McCabe</a></dfn>. &#8220;We wanted to dominate on the track and go as big as we could in the fields, and just perform at our best. I&#8217;m really happy with the way so many girls stepped up for us.&#8221;</p>
