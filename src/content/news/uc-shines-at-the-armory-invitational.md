<strong>Armory Track &amp; Field Center</strong></p>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Results:</span> <a href="/Reports/Meet_Results/Boys/PDF/Boys-2015_01_30-Armory%20Track%20&amp;%20Field%20Center,%20NY-Armory%20Invitational_ID549.pdf" target="_blank">Boys </a>| <a href="/Reports/Meet_Results/Girls/PDF/Girls-2015_01_30-Armory%20Track%20&amp;%20Field%20Center-Armory%20Invitational_ID476.pdf" target="_blank">Girls</a></p>
<p>Highlights<br />
-Girls &amp; Boys 4&#215;400 Relays place 4th. Both times are US#4 performances<br />
-Sydney McLaughlin wins the 300m with a time of 37.49. US#2 All-Time. National Sophomore Record<br />
-Taylor McLaughlin places 2nd in the 300m with a time of 33.62. Union County Record<br />
-Girls 4&#215;200 places 3rd with a season best time of 1:41.07</p>
