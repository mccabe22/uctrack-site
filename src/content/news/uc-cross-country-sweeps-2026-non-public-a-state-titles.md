<p>Union Catholic won both NJSIAA Non-Public A cross-country team titles on November 7 at Holmdel Park. The girls earned their sixth consecutive group championship by sweeping the first five places for a perfect 15 points.</p><p>The boys beat top-ranked Christian Brothers Academy 34–40. The two wins gave the Vikings a sweep of the group championships on the same course, one week before the Meet of Champions.</p><p><img alt="Union Catholic girls race through the woods at Holmdel Park." decoding="async" loading="lazy" src="/wp-content/uploads/news/uc-state-sweep-2026-girls-race.webp"/></p><p><img alt="Union Catholic and Christian Brothers Academy runners race on the Holmdel Park course." decoding="async" loading="lazy" src="/wp-content/uploads/news/uc-state-sweep-2026-boys-race.webp"/></p>
