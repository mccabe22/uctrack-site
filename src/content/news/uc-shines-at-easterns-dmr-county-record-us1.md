<p>The UC Girls dominated the DMR<a title="View team profile for Union Catholic Reg. HS" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/teams/4073">,</a> which blitzed the field with a US#1 and Union County record 11:59.38. The former Union County record was 12:01.3 by Summit in 1981.</p>
<p>UC&#8217;s splits were 3:42.96 for 1200m by <a title="View athlete profile for Leena Morant" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/6399421">Leena Morant</a>, 59.90 for 400m by <a title="View athlete profile for Avery Jordan" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/7348296">Avery Jordan</a>, 2:21.86 for 800m by <a title="View athlete profile for Morgan Williams" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/7348298">Morgan Williams</a>, and a huge PR of 4:54.68 for 1600m by<a title="View athlete profile for Jerika Lufrano" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/5577462"> Jerika Lufrano</a>. It&#8217;s the first time Lufrano has cracked 5:00.</p>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Personal Bests from Easterns<br />
</span>Boys 4&#215;200 Relay &#8211; 1:31.63 (Season Best)<br />
Avery Jordan &#8211; 8.39 for 55 Hurdles<br />
Sydney Tucker &#8211; 24.85 for 200 meters<br />
Chi Chi Nkulume &#8211; 57.24 for 400 meters<br />
Jenna Keith &#8211; 2:22.97 800 meter Split on 4&#215;800</p>
