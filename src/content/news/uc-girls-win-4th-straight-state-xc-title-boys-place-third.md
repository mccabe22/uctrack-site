<p>The Union Catholic girls overcame some adversity to win their fourth straight Non-Public A Cross-Country Championship and the boys placed third at the NJSIAA Group Championships on Saturday at Holmdel Park.</p>
<p>In the girls race, Union Catholic, ranked No. 1 in the state and No. 7 in the country, didn&#8217;t race at full strength and had two runners unable to finish, but several runners leveled up to lead the Vikings to a 45-70 victory over Pingry, ranked No. 11 in the state.</p>
<p>While sophomores <a title="View athlete profile for Paige Sheppard" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/9290971-paige-sheppard">Paige Sheppard</a>, who won her second straight title in 17:56, and <a title="View athlete profile for Cayleigh Kaiser" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/13800529-cayleigh-kaiser">Cayleigh Kaiser</a> (18:44), finished 1-2 to ignite the attack for the Vikings, it took some gutsy and clutch races from their 3-4-5 runners to seal the deal.</p>
<p>UC, who held out sophomore <a title="View athlete profile for Sophia Thompson" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/13800535-sophia-thompson">Sophia Thompson</a> (she&#8217;s expected back for the Meet of Champions) needed huge runs from<a title="View athlete profile for Emmy O'Hearn" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/12289907-emmy-ohearn"> Emmy O&#8217;Hearn</a>, <a title="View athlete profile for Ella Solorzano" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/12289923-ella-solorzano">Ella Solorzano</a>, and <a title="View athlete profile for Joelle Ruccio" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/13254480-joelle-ruccio">Joelle Ruccio</a>. And the juniors delivered in a big way.</p>
<p>O&#8217;Hearn ran a strong race, getting out well, holding her spot and finishing with a big kick to finish sixth with a Holmdel PR of 19:45. Solorzano showed lots of heart on her way to a 17th place finish in 20:26, and Ruccio put an exclamation point on the 4-peat with a 19th place finish in 20:35. Big shoutout to Ruccio, who was inserted into the lineup in place of Thompson and up with the race of her life  in her first time running on such a big stage.</p>
<p>UC coach Mike McCabe had a feeling Ruccio might be a huge factor for his squad</p>
<p>&#8221;Right before we went to the line, I told Joelle to be ready and that if I tell her she is 5, she had to make it happen,&#8221; said McCabe. &#8216;&#8221;At 1K, I told her she was 5 and she came through for us.&#8221;</p>
<p>The girls will race next at the Meet of Champions this Saturday at Holmdel Park where they are favored to win their third straight title.</p>
<p>In the boys race, UC also advanced to the Meet of Champions with a strong third-place finish in the Non-Public A race. The Vikings, ranked No. 8 in the state, finished with 103 points. Christian Brothers Academy  ranked No. 1 in NJ and No. 2 in the nation, finished first with 15 points and No. 7 ranked St. Peter&#8217;s Prep was second with 74.</p>
<p>The Vikings, who averaged 16:56, earned a spot at the M of C as one of the eight wild cards, finishing as the sixth best team in the overall merge that didn&#8217;t include the six automatic qualifiers.</p>
