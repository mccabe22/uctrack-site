<h2 class="article-headline">UC&#8217;s Myles Plummer wins high school debut at Battle at Ocean County Park</h2>
<div class="noprint story_email"><a href="https://www.addthis.com/bookmark.php?v=250&amp;pub=xa-4a7320e7645958d0"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="https://s7.addthis.com/static/btn/lg-share-en.gif" alt="Bookmark and Share" width="125" height="16" /></a> <time class="article-date">9/15/2018 10:22:00 AM</time></div>
<p class="article-subheadline">Plummer won the Freshman Division race</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>Union Catholic&#8217;s <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=538" rel="smarttag" rev="538">Myles Plummer</a></dfn> opened lots of eyes with a sensational high school debut when he won the Freshman Division at the Battle at Ocean County Park on Friday in Lakewood.</p>
<p>Plummer, who has all the makings of a future star, covered the 3,200-meter course in 10:08 as he won the race by four seconds over runner-up Jack Moran of Wall Township.</p>
<p>Led by Plummer, the Vikings tied with Toms River North in the team standings with 53 points.</p>
<p>In the Junior Division race, UC&#8217;s Zach Turner placed 39th in 17:38, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=546" rel="smarttag" rev="546">Andrew Palacio</a></dfn> was 78th in 18:27, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=545" rel="smarttag" rev="545">Daniel Labrie</a></dfn> was 79th in 18:28. Matt Swinehart ran 18:55 to finish 92nd ifor UC n the Senior Division race.</p>

<h2 class="article-headline">UC girls cross-country team wins two team titles at Battle at Ocean County Park</h2>
<div class="noprint story_email"><a href="https://www.addthis.com/bookmark.php?v=250&amp;pub=xa-4a7320e7645958d0"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="https://s7.addthis.com/static/btn/lg-share-en.gif" alt="Bookmark and Share" width="125" height="16" /></a> <time class="article-date">9/15/2018 9:54:00 AM</time></div>
<p class="article-subheadline">The Vikings won the Senior and Freshman Team Titles</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic girls cross-country team opened its season by winning two titles at Friday&#8217;s Battle at Ocean County Park in Lakewood.</p>
<p>The Vikings, the defending state Non-Public A champions, captured both the Senior Division and Freshman Division team championships.</p>
<p>In the Senior Division, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=548" rel="smarttag" rev="548">Jerika Lufrano</a></dfn> finished second in the 5K race in 18:36, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=554" rel="smarttag" rev="554">Megan McCabe</a></dfn> was 16th in 20:34, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=560" rel="smarttag" rev="560">Monika Wlodarski</a></dfn> placed 24th in 21:05 to give the Vikings a winning total of 36 points, two points ahead of runner-up Colts Neck.</p>
<p>Abby Granrath led the charge for the Vikings in the Freshman Division by placing second over the 3,200-meter course in 12:17. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=552" rel="smarttag" rev="552">Jenna Keith</a></dfn> was fifth for UC in 12:31, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=551" rel="smarttag" rev="551">Daniela Peralta</a></dfn> finished 13th in 13:15 for a first-place score of 18 points. Southern Regional was second with 28.</p>
<p>In the Junior Division race, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=558" rel="smarttag" rev="558">Zoe Lee</a></dfn> ran a strong race by placing 12th for the Vikings in 19:57.</p>
