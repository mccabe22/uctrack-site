<p>By Jim Lambert</p>
<p>The Union Catholic girls blew apart the track by running three NJ No. 1 times, smashing a meet record, and winning four races on the way to a record 10th straight championship at the Union County Relays on Saturday at the Jersey City Armory.</p>
<p>Union Catholic left lots of smoke on the track en route to a 66-36 victory over runner-up Rahway.</p>
<p>The biggest highlight came when Rhia Randolph, Taylor Aska, Natalia Thompson, and Sydney Chadwick combined to win the 4&#215;200 with a sizzling NJ No. 1/US No. 2 time of 1:40.70, which shattered the meet record by more than two seconds. The former record of 1:43.10 was set by last year. This performance was especially impressive because it was run on a flat track! I haven&#8217;t found a faster time yet in NJ history on a flat track. </p>
<p>Keep in mind that the state record in the 4&#215;200 is 1:38.60, set by UC last year when the Vikings placed fourth at the New Balance Nationals. There&#8217;s no doubt that UC has the potential to take that record down when it loads up to run on a banked track.</p>
<p>UC&#8217;s two other NJ No. 1 times came in the shuttle hurdles (31.84) and the sprint medley (4:16.88), and its fourth victory was in the DMR with a NJ No.2 time of 12:35.75.</p>
<p>The UC boys, who held out several of their top distance runners, also had a very strong meet with a third-place finish with 35 points.</p>
<p>The big highlight for the Vikings came in the shuttle hurdles when Al-Qamar-Rojas, Chris Wilson, Marcus McCoy and Justice Hicks combined to finish first in 30.27, the fourth-fastest time in the state this season. UC also tied for first in the high jump with Hicks and Kevin Philistin each clearing 5-10, and finished second in the 4&#215;800, third in the DMR, and fifth in the 4&#215;400.   </p>
