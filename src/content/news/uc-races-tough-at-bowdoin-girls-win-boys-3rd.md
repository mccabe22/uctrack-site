<p>The Union Catholic girls, ranked NJ No. 1/US No. 7, made a huge statement when the Vikings scored a convincing win at the Bowdoin Park Classic and the UC boys finished a very strong third on Saturday in Wappingers Falls, N.Y.</p>
<p>In the girls Division 2 race, sophomore <a title="View athlete profile for Paige Sheppard" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/9290971-paige-sheppard">Paige Sheppard </a>fueled the attack for the Vikings with a runway victory in 18:11.3, a second faster than she ran to win the Nike Northeast Regional Championship last November at Bowdoin. Sheppard&#8217;s time is the second fastest by a NJ girl in course history. <a title="View athlete profile for Charlotte Bednar" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/8453607-charlotte-bednar">Charlotte Bednar</a> of Lawrenceville ran 18:00.0 to win the NE Regional in 2019.</p>
<p>Sheppard&#8217;s victory sparked a 1-4-5-12-15 finish for the Vikings, who averaged 19:30.0 and outscored New York power Saratoga Springs in the Division 2 race, 35-93. Princeton ranked No. 9 in NJ, finished third with 96. The four other scorers for UC&#8217;s all underclassmen squad were sophomore <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/sports/womens-cross-country/roster/sophia-thompson/3265" rel="smarttag" rev="3265" data-airgap-id="48">Sophia Thompson</a></dfn>, fourth in 19:25.4, junior<a title="View athlete profile for Ella Solorzano" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/12289923-ella-solorzano"> Ella Solorzano</a>, fifth in 19:37.0, freshman Jasmine Parran, 12th in 20:06.9, and junior<a title="View athlete profile for Emmy O'Hearn" href="https://nj.milesplit.com/athletes/12289907-emmy-ohearn"> Emmy O&#8217;Hearn</a>, 15th in 20:14.1.</p>
<p>In the boys competition, UC, ranked No. 5 in the state, placed third with 148 points and averaged 17:08 on the rugged layout. The only teams to finish ahead of UC were No. 1 ranked CBA and No. 4 Haddonfield.</p>
<p>Senior Evan Weber led the way for the Vikings, placing 11th in 16:33. Senior Aidan Cichocki was 31st in 17:12, sophomore Finn Riely placed 34th in 17:15, senior Michael Devine was 41st in 17:15, and freshman Manny Sanchez finished 41st in 17:23.</p>
