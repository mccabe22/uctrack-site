<header>
<p class="article-byline">Union Catholic, ignited by <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/sports/womens-cross-country/roster/paige-sheppard/3263" rel="smarttag" rev="3263" data-airgap-id="46">Paige Sheppard&#8217;s</a></dfn> course record and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/sports/mens-cross-country/roster/evan-weber/3268" rel="smarttag" rev="3268" data-airgap-id="47">Evan Weber&#8217;s</a></dfn> victory, swept the Watchung Division team titles at Wednesday&#8217;s Union County Conference Championships at Oak Ridge Park in Clark.</p>
</header>
<p>The UC girls, ranked No. 1 in the state and No. 7 nationally, simply blitzed the competition by throwing down a perfect score of 15 and obliterating the course record average on the way to a repeat title.</p>
<p>Sheppard, the sensational sophomore, led the UC charge by crushing her own course record with a jaw-dropping time of 17:12.1, slashing 29 seconds off the course record of 17:41 that she ran at this meet last year. Sheppard&#8217;s sizzling victory sparked UC&#8217;s 1-2-3-4-5 finish as the Vikings torched the 3.1-mile layout to the tune of an 18:20 average, which destroyed the course record.</p>
<p>UC&#8217;s top five were Sheppard, sophomore <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/sports/womens-cross-country/roster/cayleigh-kaiser/3260" rel="smarttag" rev="3260" data-airgap-id="48">Cayleigh Kaiser</a></dfn>, was second in 17:43.7, junior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/sports/womens-cross-country/roster/jimmiea-king/3261" rel="smarttag" rev="3261" data-airgap-id="49">Jimmiea King</a></dfn>, third in 18:49.9, freshman Jasmine Parran, fourth in 18:52.4, and junior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/sports/womens-cross-country/roster/emmy-o-hearn/3262" rel="smarttag" rev="3262" data-airgap-id="50">Emmy O&#8217;Hearn</a></dfn> completed the shutout by finishing fifth in 19:03.4.</p>
<p>The 18:20 average by UC, which held out a couple of its regulars,  shattered the girls course record of 18:35 that UC set at the conference meet a year ago, and is the third fastest average ever run by a NJ girls team in a single race on any course. Colts Neck, who ran 18:16 at the 2021 Shore Conference Championships at Ocean County Park, owns the state record and UC is No. 2 with the 18:18 average it ran in the Sophomore Division race at the Battle of Ocean County Park. UC&#8217;s 5-runner average at the Battle of Ocean County Park was 17:50 in the Sophomore and Junior races combined.</p>
<p>In the boys Watchung Division race, UC, ranked No. 8 in the state, Weber, a senior, dropped a huge PR of 15:39.2 to win the race and the Vikings jammed its five scorers in the top 10 and averaged 16:09 en route to a 28-47 win over No. 4 Westfield.</p>
<p>UC&#8217;s scorers were Weber, senior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/sports/mens-cross-country/roster/aidan-cichocki/3266" rel="smarttag" rev="3266" data-airgap-id="51">Aidan Cichocki</a></dfn>, fourth in 16:09, junior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/sports/mens-cross-country/roster/elijah-mccoy/3296" rel="smarttag" rev="3296" data-airgap-id="52">Elijah McCoy</a></dfn>, sixth in 16:15, senior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/sports/mens-cross-country/roster/michael-devine/3267" rel="smarttag" rev="3267" data-airgap-id="53">Michael Devine</a></dfn>, seventh in 16:16, and sophomore <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/sports/mens-cross-country/roster/quintin-clemons/3292" rel="smarttag" rev="3292" data-airgap-id="54">Quintin Clemons</a></dfn> was 10th in 16:23.</p>
<p>UC and Westfield will clash again at the Union County Championships on October 24 at Warinanco Park in Elizabeth. UC has won the last four titles.</p>
