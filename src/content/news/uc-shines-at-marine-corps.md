<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Highlights</span><br />
-Taylor McLaughlin places 1st in the 300m Dash and breaks the Union County Record with a time 33.85.  NJ#4 All-Time. Current US#2. <a href="http://www.runnerspace.com/video.php?video_id=129810#ooid=trYzFqcjp5Drr6s3lTxY41PFGNCzH590" target="_blank">Video</a><br />
-Sean Brennan places 1st in the Shot Put with a School Record Throw of 57&#8217;5 1/2&#8243;. NJ#2. Current US#6<br />
-Boys Sprint Medley Relay places 1st with a US#1 time of 3:38.35 (Henderson, T. McLaughlin, Morgan, G. Gibbs)  <a href="http://www.runnerspace.com/video.php?video_id=129831#ooid=U0ZTVqcjr9gr3c0j5UCUbIGExnKUVES7" target="_blank">Video</a><br />
-Girls Shuttle Hurdle Relay places 2nd with a NJ#1/US#3 time of 33.63 (Coverson, Chadwick, J. Gibbs, S. McLaughlin)<br />
-Girls 4&#215;200 Relay places 4th with a NJ#1/US#7 time of 1:43.38 (Chadwick, S. McLaughlin, J. Gibbs, Coverson) <a href="http://www.runnerspace.com/video.php?video_id=129819#ooid=9rYjJqcjrM0DmZvL2gYug5M80JiV-Gsq" target="_blank">Video</a><br />
-Girls Distance Medley places 6th and breaks the School Record with a time of 12:53.71 (Johnson, M. Williams, Sullivan, Ca. Glynn)</p>
