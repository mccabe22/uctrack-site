<p>The Union Catholic cross-country teams celebrated Senior Day in memorable fashion as the boys and girls swept the Watchung Division titles at Thursday&#8217;s Union County Conference Championships at Oak Ridge Park in Clark.</p>
<p>On the girls side, Union Catholic, ranked No. 11 in the state, edged No. 10 Kent Place, 47-50.</p>
<p>UC went 3-8-10-12-14 and averaged 20:07, and Kent Place went 2-6-11-15-16 and averaged 20:05. The rematch at the county meet should be another great battle between these two powerhouses.</p>
