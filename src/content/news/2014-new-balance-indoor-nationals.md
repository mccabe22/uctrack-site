<p><strong>Friday, March 14th &#8211; Sunday, March 16th, 2014</strong><br />
<strong>Armory Track &amp; Field Center, NY</strong></p>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Notes</span><br />
-Plan your meals, snacks and drinks for each day.<br />
-Get proper rest. Hydrate!<br />
-We will be car pooling to the meet throughout the weekend. Stay in contact with Coach McCabe about your transportation.<br />
-BE ON TIME!!! If you have an issue, contact call Coach McCabe on his cell.<br />
-Our schedule, entries and arrival times are listed below.<br />
-When you arrive at the track for the first time, call Coach McCabe so he can bring your bib number down. Your bib is what will allow entry into the meet. Bring it back each day or you won&#8217;t be admitted.<br />
-Don&#8217;t forget your racing spikes and remember to put in 1/4 inch pyramid spikes (Coach has extra).<br />
-Bring the proper uniform. If you are unsure. Bring more than one uniform.<br />
-Make sure Coach McCabe has your USATF #</p>
