<p class="article-subheadline">The 14-9 1/4 by Reilly on Friday broke a 37 year-old record</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>A few days ago, <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=381" rel="smarttag" rev="381">Conor Reilly</a></dfn> was doing a little research on what the state sophomore record in the pole vault was.</p>
<p>When he discovered that it was 14-9, the sophomore at Union Catholic flashed a huge grin.</p>
<p>After making 15-0 in practice this season, Reilly knew it was just a matter of time before that record would be his.</p>
<p>The time came on Friday when Reilly scaled over the bar at 14-9 1/4 at the Union County Conference Championships at Gov. Livingston High School to become the No. 1 sophomore vaulter in N.J. outdoor history.</p>
<p><a href="http://www.milesplit.com/videos/288102/conor-reilly-breaks-state-soph-pv-record">WATCH REILLY&#8217;S RECORD BREAKING VAULT </a></p>
<p>Reilly&#8217;s 14-9 1/4 clearance broke the state sophomore record that was set 37 years ago when Ray Maragni of Seton Hall Prep  made 14-9 in 1981. The 14-9 1/4 also vaulted Reilly to NJ #1 this season (not including dual meet marks) and is US #19 among all sophomores.</p>
<p>The 5-8 Reilly, who came into the indoor season with a PR of 13-0 and climbed up to 14-6 during this past winter, now has his sights set on making 15-0 and beyond this season.</p>
<p>Reilly, who is the starting goalie on the UC soccer team, has always had a flare for doing things in the air. And like many great vaulters, he has a strong gymnastics background.<br />
&#8220;I think the thing that makes me so successful in vaulting is that I&#8217;ve had a long career in gymnastics,&#8221; said Reilly. &#8220;I did gymnastics for like nine years, and it (the pole vault) just feels very natural for me.&#8221;</p>
<p>Reilly&#8217;s gymnastics experience was responsible for helping to get him introduced to vaulting.</p>
<p>When he attended a track camp at Union Catholic in eighth grade, Andrew Ghizzone was working the camp. Ghizzone won the pole vault for Union Catholic by clearing 16-0 at the 2014 outdoor Meet of Champions, and he&#8217;s now starring at the University of Colorado,</p>
<p>&#8220;Andrew recognized me from gymnastics and said I should try the vault,&#8221; said Reilly. &#8220;I&#8217;ve loved it ever since.&#8221;</p>
