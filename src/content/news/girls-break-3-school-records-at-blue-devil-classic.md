<h2 class="article-headline">UC girls break 3 school records, win 3 events at Blue Devil Classic</h2>
<p class="article-subheadline">The girls won the shuttle hurdles, 4&#215;100 and DMR</p>
<p class="article-byline">By Jim Lambert</p>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic girls tuned up for this week&#8217;s Penn Relays by winning three events and breaking three school records at the 30th Blue Devil Classic on Saturday at Gary Kehler Stadium in Westfield.</p>
<p>The Vikings captured the shuttle hurdles in a NJ #2 1:02.74, the 4&#215;100 in a NJ# 3 48.69, and the distance medley relay in a NJ # 3 and school record 12:27.90. The old DMR school record of 12:33.09 was set last year. In addition to the DMR school record, the Vikings also broke school records in the three-person team discus and three-person team pole vault.</p>
<p>In the shuttle hurdles, senior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=393" rel="smarttag" rev="393">Amaya Chadwick</a></dfn>, who returned to the track earlier in the week after a hamstring injury sidelined her for most of the indoor season, led off in 14.0 to put the Vikings ahead to stay. Freshman <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=467" rel="smarttag" rev="467">A&#8217;Liyah Thomas</a></dfn> had a strong 15.5 leg on the shuttles. Junior <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=395" rel="smarttag" rev="395">Khamil Evans</a></dfn> had a strong meet for UC. She ran the third leg on the 4&#215;100 and cranked out a 57.8 in the DMR. Also in the DMR, UC had splits of 3:50.1 from <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=407" rel="smarttag" rev="407">Leena Morant</a></dfn>, 2:21.8 from <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=420" rel="smarttag" rev="420">Zamira Ysaac</a></dfn>, and 5:17.2 from <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=404" rel="smarttag" rev="404">Jerika Lufrano</a></dfn>.</p>
<p>In the discus, Christine Samuels (102-6), Bella Giacobbe (79-2), and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=414" rel="smarttag" rev="414">Danielle Torres</a></dfn> (63.3) combined for a school record 244-11, breaking the mark of 237-10 set in 2010. In the pole vault, Danielle Rappa cleared a school freshman record 7-6, and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=400" rel="smarttag" rev="400">Hope Handlin</a></dfn> and <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=399" rel="smarttag" rev="399">Isabella Gray</a></dfn> also made 7-6 for a school record total of 22-6. <dfn><a href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=413" rel="smarttag" rev="413">Tyler Scott</a></dfn> leaped a school senior class record 35-5 3/4 in the triple jump.</p>
