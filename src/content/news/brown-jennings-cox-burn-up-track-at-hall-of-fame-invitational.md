<p>By Jim Lambert</p>
<p>Taylor Cox lit up the track with a sizzling performance at Saturday&#8217;s Coaches Hall of Fame Invitational at the New York Armory.</p>
<p>Cox ran a NJ No. 1 time.</p>
<p>In a red hot showdown in the hurdles, took over the state lead with a US No. 2 winning time of 7.95 to edge Kassidy Hopkins of Bullis in Maryland, who was second with a US No. 3 time of 7.99. Cox came close to her personal best and NJ No. 5 all-time of 7.88 that she ran to win the Meet of Champions last year.</p>
