<p>Taylor McLaughlin earned a silver medal at the World Youth Championships in the Medley Relay. Taylor split 31.5 on the 300 meter leg! Congratulations to Taylor for a spectacular meet! Taylor split 46.9 on Saturday in the trial of the Medley Relay.</p>
<p>A final congratulations to <em><strong>Taylor</strong></em> and <strong><em>Jordan</em></strong> from the Union Catholic Track &amp; Field Community! You represented the United States of America and Union Catholic proudly! Articles, Photos, Videos are below.</p>
