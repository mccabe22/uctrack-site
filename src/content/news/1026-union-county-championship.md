<p><strong>Wednesday, October 26th, 2016</strong><br />
<strong>Warinanco Park</strong></p>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Results:</span> <a href="/Reports/Meet_Results/Boys/PDF/Boys-2016_10_26-Warinanco%20Park-Union%20County%20Championship_ID639.pdf" target="_blank">Boys </a>| <a href="/Reports/Meet_Results/Girls/PDF/Girls-2016_10_26-Warinanco%20Park-Union%20County%20Championship_ID567.pdf" target="_blank">Girls</a></p>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Highlights</span><br />
-Boys Team Places 2nd and runs the 3rd Fastest Team Average (16:33) in School History!<br />
-Nate Papachristos Breaks the School Record and runs the 5th Fastest Time in Course History!<br />
-Josh Ciano wins the Boys Freshman Race!<br />
-Girls Team places 2nd with a 20:12 Team Average!<br />
-Personal Bests for almost the entire team!</p>
