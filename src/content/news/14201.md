<h2 class="article-headline">Union Catholic opens cross-country season with several strong performances</h2>
<p class="article-subheadline">Granrath, Lee, Power and Plummer star for the Vikings</p>
</header>
<p>The Union Catholic cross-country teams opened its season with several impressive performances at the Roxbury Invitational on Saturday at Horseshoe Lake Park in Succasunna.</p>
<p>In the girls competition, sophomore <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=553" rel="smarttag" rev="553">Abby Granrath</a></dfn> led the charge for the Vikings by placing 12th in 19:48  to help Union Catholic place fourth in the Fatsest 4 race. Senior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=558" rel="smarttag" rev="558">Zoe Lee</a></dfn> placed 17th in 19:59, junior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=557" rel="smarttag" rev="557">Morgan Williams</a></dfn> was 24th in 20:28, and senior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=559" rel="smarttag" rev="559">Ashleigh Patterson</a></dfn> was 29th in 20:33. The Vikings averaged 20:12 and finished with 77 points, just 21 behind Voorhees, firs with 56.</p>
<p>The girls also had a very strong showing in the Future 4 race as rising star <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1168" rel="smarttag" rev="1168">Kathryn Power</a></dfn> hammered her way across the line in second place in 20:02, the fastest time of the day for a freshman, to spark the Vikings to a runner-up finish. UC, which placed second to Mendham, 21-40, also picked up a fourth place from freshman <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1172" rel="smarttag" rev="1172">Maameyaa Nyinah</a></dfn> (20:13), a 13th place from sophomore <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=551" rel="smarttag" rev="551">Daniela Peralta</a></dfn> (21:21), and 25th from senior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=556" rel="smarttag" rev="556">Morgan Jackson</a></dfn> (22:24).</p>
<p>In the boys competition, sophomore <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=538" rel="smarttag" rev="538">Myles Plummer</a></dfn> ran 16:20 to place 13th in the Fastest 4 race. Senior Zach Turner finished 57th in 17:25, senior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=546" rel="smarttag" rev="546">Andrew Palacio</a></dfn> was 63rd in 17:32.</p>
<p>Junior <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1165" rel="smarttag" rev="1165">Sam Hernandez</a></dfn> led the way for the Vikings in the Future 4 race by placing 24th in 17:38 to help UC place 10th with 164 points.Sophomore <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1167" rel="smarttag" rev="1167">Noah Alston</a></dfn> was 27th in 17:43, sophomore <dfn><a class="sidearm-roster-link-normalized" href="https://ucvikings.com/roster.aspx?rp_id=1166" rel="smarttag" rev="1166">Noah Handlin</a></dfn> was 63rd in 18:40, and senior Dan Labrie finished 64th in 18:41.</div>
