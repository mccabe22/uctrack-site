How to Use

Enter time, distance, and pace values to solve a missing field, or use + − × ÷ directly within any selected field. Press Calculate to complete the calculation.

Use time, distance, and pace together to solve a missing value, or perform arithmetic directly within any individual field.

The calculator supports miles, kilometers, and meters, with pace shown per mile or per kilometer. Arithmetic results for time and pace retain tenths of a second when needed for more accurate short-distance calculations.

Enter a valid time and distance, then tap Splits to view cumulative race splits.
